# After the peak: timing, the fall, the money and the risks

A slop game is a short business. This page gives the real shape of that business so the plan matches it.

## How long before anything happens

The two biggest slop hits of late 2026 were not instant:

| Game | Created | Broke out | Days live before breakout | Peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steal An Egg | 25 Jul 2026 | Sat 12 Sep 2026 (the largest one-day gain RoStats has recorded) | 49 | 14.3M, seven days later |
| Throw Food For Animals | 18 Sep 2026 | 23 Oct 2026, with its first update (daily average up 313%) | 35 | 2.47M, three weeks later |
| Grow a Garden | 26 Mar 2025 | About 1,000 concurrent players in April 2025 when a studio bought in | about 50 to the first 5M peak | 21.3M on 21 Jun 2025 |

Sources: [RoStats records](https://rostats.io/records), [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden), Roblox public API for creation dates.

**Our read:** five to seven weeks of being live, small and updated came before each breakout. Launch day decides very little. The weeks after it, spent tuning and shipping, are where these games were made.

## The fall

"Now" is a weekday morning (US) on 25 November 2026, so it understates evenings and weekends, but the scale is clear:

| Game | Peak | Now | Now as share of peak | Time since peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grow a Garden | 22.3M | 18K | 0.08% | 15 months |
| Steal a Brainrot | 25.4M | 42K | 0.17% | 13 months |
| Escape Tsunami For Brainrots | 4.1M to 5.0M | 3.8K | about 0.09% | 10 months |
| Ride A Pet | 1.55M | 1.6K | 0.1% | 2 months |
| Kick a Lucky Block | about 533K | 12K | about 2% | 3 months |
| Steal An Egg | 14.3M | 852K | 6% | 2 months |

Sources: peaks from [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden), [RoStats](https://rostats.io/records) and [RoVitals](https://rovitals.com/); "now" from Roblox's public API.

Three things soften that table:

- **Peaks are event spikes.** Grow a Garden's 21.3M record fell to 7.6M within an hour and 5.5M within two; its 14-day average at the time was 2.7M ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)). A fair baseline is the weekly average, and against that the falls are large but less extreme.
- **Saturdays still work.** Steal a Brainrot reached 729K on Saturday 21 November 2026 against about 37K on weekdays. A game that keeps its weekly event keeps a weekly audience long after the charts have moved on.
- **The fall is where the money was already made.** Steal a Brainrot's purchases were estimated at US$64 million by March 2026 ([Bloomberg](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/03/25/robloxs-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)).

**Our read:** plan for a hot window of four to ten weeks after a breakout. Everything you want from the game has to be possible inside that window: the weekly events, the full product list, a second game ready to follow. The exceptions are the one or two flagships per year that hold a top-ten place through events.

## What the hits do when the trend cools

- **Rename and re-tag.** Titles carry a seasonal emoji tag that changes with each event, and games drift off the fading trend word: Plants vs Brainrots is now "Plants & Brainrots".
- **Borrow an audience.** A Bruno Mars concert inside Steal a Brainrot in January 2026 drew a reported 12.8M concurrent players ([GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/roblox-claims-bruno-mars-steal-a-brainrot-performance-drew-a-record-128-million-concurrent-users-issued-54-million-brainrot-collectibles)). Steal An Egg ran a Jonas Brothers event on 31 October 2026.
- **Cross-promote.** Steal An Egg's record week included a crossover with the maker of Steal a Brainrot, and one studio, DoBig, now owns both plus a share of Grow a Garden.
- **Keep the Saturday.** All of them still ship every week.

## The platform changed in 2026

Three rules arrived that bear directly on this kind of game.

**1. Recommendations now look at 28 days.** On 15 June 2026 Roblox widened the window its "Recommended For You" system uses from 7 days to 28, with separate signals for day 1, days 2 to 7 and days 8 to 28, and it counts play days, playtime and spend days ([DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)). Roblox's stated reason was that games with exciting thumbnails were crowding out games that keep players. Playtime counts only up to 60 minutes per player per day. Details in [foundations/02-the-platform.md](../foundations/02-the-platform.md).

**2. A new game has to earn its way to children.** Since June 2026 a new experience is shown first only to age-checked players of 16 and over, and reaches under-16s after a set number of "highly engaged" players within 60 days. The bar was 500 at launch, 250 from August 2026, and Roblox's documentation now gives 100 ([docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select), [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164)).

**3. No reward for watching a feed.** On 25 August 2026 Roblox removed from its under-16 catalogues any game that shows a media feed with autoplay or endless scroll and rewards the player for continuing to watch ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-new-restrictions-on-reward-driven-media-feeds/4829188)). Steal An Egg's treadmill had shown short videos while the player trained; the game was pulled briefly and returned without them ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/roblox-cracks-down-on-doomscrolling-games-like-steal-an-egg-that-forces-players-to-watch-reels-while-running-on-a-treadmill-2000727952)).

**Our read** on what these mean together:

- A slop game's first audience is now older teenagers and adults, because they are the ones who can see it. The first five minutes have to work for a 16-year-old before a 9-year-old ever arrives.
- A game that only has a strong first hour is ranked on a window it cannot fill. Days 8 to 28 need their own reason: the weekly event, a rebirth ladder that takes weeks, a collection that grows every Saturday, friends.
- Filling idle time with something that is not your game is a risk. Make the waiting part of the game (see mechanism 10 in [02-why-it-holds.md](02-why-it-holds.md)).

The short version: **a slop hook plus one real reason to return.** The hook gets the click and the first session. The reason to return is what the 28-day window, and the second month of revenue, are made of.

## Risks

### Other people's characters

The Italian brainrot characters began as AI-generated memes with individual creators. One of them, Tung Tung Tung Sahur, was removed from Steal a Brainrot in September 2025 after a licensing dispute with its creator and restored in November 2025, and a dispute over whether such characters can be owned at all was still active in 2026 ([Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot), [Wikipedia: Italian brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_brainrot)). The game's own rebirth table had to be rewritten around the removal ([wiki](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth)).

Rule: a meme being everywhere does not make it free. Use characters you made or licensed.

### Being sued by the game you copied

By March 2026 the companies behind Steal a Brainrot had filed at least four lawsuits against imitators, one seeking at least US$1.8 million. Their claims rest on copied layout, menus and art, not on the idea of stealing or on the meme characters ([Bloomberg](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/03/25/robloxs-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)).

Rule: copy how a system works. Never copy how it looks, what it is called or how its screens are laid out. This is also the rule that produces the followers that succeed (see [03-quality-bar.md](03-quality-bar.md)).

### Being shown less

Roblox's discovery documentation says experiences with copied or misleading titles and thumbnails are shown less ([Discovery docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/discovery)).

### The press and parents

Steal a Brainrot was criticised for selling its strongest protection and for theft that upsets young players; Grow a Garden for monetisation added after its sale and for a real-money market in its items that breaks Roblox's terms ([Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot), [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)). About a third of this audience is under 13.

Rule: build as if a parent is watching the screen. [foundations/15-the-line.md](../foundations/15-the-line.md) turns that into checks.

### Cheats

A game about owning things attracts scripts that take them. Decide every purchase, carry and lock on the server from the first day; it is far harder to add later.

## What to carry to the next game

A slop studio's real product is the pipeline, because any single game fades. Keep, from each one:

- The base, unit, ladder, mutation, clock and rebirth systems as reusable modules.
- The funnel events and the dashboard that reads them.
- The Saturday event tooling.
- A written record of what moved each stat ([kit/templates/update-plan.md](../kit/templates/update-plan.md)).

The teams that followed a hit within two weeks could do it because everything except the new verb was already built.
