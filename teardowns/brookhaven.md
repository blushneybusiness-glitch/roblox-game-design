## 1. Core loop

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pick a house | Opens the Houses icon, chooses one, spawns it on a plot marked Vacant, saves by hand | not known | The house appears on the plot | 1 of 15 free standard plots ([wiki: House Guide](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/House_Guide)) |
| Pick a car | Opens Vehicles | not known | The car spawns beside the player | none |
| Pick a role | Opens Jobs | not known | An icon above the head | none; roles pay nothing ([wiki: Jobs](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Jobs)) |
| Dress | Opens the Avatar Editor | not known | New outfit | none |
| Play a scene | Drives to a building, acts with others | open-ended | Other players' icons, cars, houses | none |

Cycle time: not known. There is no currency and no progression, so there is no dead time in the usual sense: the player is never waiting for a number. Getting a new house is gated by a cooldown that the wiki gives as both 10 minutes (VIP skips it) and 2 minutes on the same page, so neither is confirmed ([wiki: House Guide](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/House_Guide)).

## 3. Progression

None. In place of a ladder, Brookhaven has a toolkit. Counts from two fan sources that differ:

| Thing | Count | Free | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Houses | 107 / over 115 | 80+ standard styles, many free | [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp), [wiki: Houses](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Houses) |
| Standard plots | 21 | 15 (6 need Land Unlocked) | [wiki: House Guide](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/House_Guide) |
| Estate plots | 3 | 0 | same |
| Vehicles | 234 / 190+ | at least 82 street vehicles | [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp), [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game), [wiki: Vehicles/Street](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicles/Street) |
| Roles | 81 | 80 | [wiki: Jobs](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Jobs) |
| Named buildings / locations | about 40 / 55 | all | [wiki index](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Special:AllPages), [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp) |
| Emotes | 120 | not known | [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp) |
| Weather and disasters | 13 | Disaster Pass 375 R$ | same |
| Secrets | 12 (about 10 on the wiki) | all | same |

The one ladder is the prop limit ([wiki: Props Limit](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Props_Limit)): public builds start at 15 props (20 VIP) and buy +10 for 80 R$ ([data, Nov 2026]; the wiki gives 100) up to 45 (50 VIP). Private servers start at 500 and buy +100 for 80 R$ up to 2,000. Our arithmetic: 240 R$ fills the public cap at 8 R$ a prop; 1,200 R$ fills the private cap at 0.8 R$ a prop.

**Our read** on "a toy, not a game" as a brief: the measurable promise is roughly 100 houses, 200 vehicles, 80 roles, 40 buildings and 100 emotes, with 99% of roles and 71% of plots free. The design work is content volume and menus, not curves.

## 4. Session and return hooks

- Weekly update, Fridays at 6 pm EST ([wiki: Changelog](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Changelog)). 20 weekly entries from 19 Jun to 6 Nov 2026; the list as read has no 26 Jun entry. The wiki lags the live game by two to three weeks (newest wiki entry 6 Nov; API update 23 Nov).
- A typical entry (our count): 1 to 2 houses, 1 to 3 vehicles, 5 to 12 props, 1 to 6 tools. A location or system change about every second week. A new pass or paid bundle every 2 to 3 weeks. A collaboration (Minions, Jurassic World, a fashion brand) every 2 to 4 weeks.
- Seasonal events: seven listed, all ended; the latest, Halloween, ran 23 Oct to 6 Nov 2026 ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp)).
- 70 active codes on 25 Nov 2026 ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp)).
- Daily rewards, offline progress, timers inside a session: none found.

## 5. Other players

30 per server. Others see your role icon, your house on its numbered plot and your car. Nothing can be stolen. Almost every house has a hidden wall safe for crime scenes, and most have hidden rooms ([wiki: House Guide](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/House_Guide)). No trading, no leaderboards. What gets clipped: not measured.

## 7. Why it works

Developers' own account: none found. Measured: visits rose from about 60B to about 80B in just over a year after the 2025 sale, while updates went weekly ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/roleplay-games-rule-roblox-adopt-me-and-brookhaven-command-900k-players)). An aggregator credits near-instant entry, no quests or currency, and self-directed social play ([RoWatcher explainer](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game)).

**Our read:**
- Zero cost to start a scene: every input is free and one tap away.
- 30 per server means about 11,000 parallel towns at 332K players, each small enough to be one cast.
- Passes sell more of a thing the player already likes, at 18 price points, so almost any budget finds one.
- The weekly drop gives players a new prop for this week's scenes.

## 8. What went wrong

Players call it aimless once the newness fades. Chat features drew scrutiny for inappropriate content and dating behaviour ([RoWatcher explainer](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game)). The March 2026 update expanded Quick Chat ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/roleplay-games-rule-roblox-adopt-me-and-brookhaven-command-900k-players)). A fourth neighbourhood, Ridgeway, was removed in 2021 ([wiki: Neighborhoods](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Brookhaven_Neighborhoods)). No decline is visible in the numbers found.

## 9. What to take from it

- Free core: 80 of 81 roles, 15 of 21 standard plots, 80+ street vehicles free.
- Paid breadth: 18 passes in three tiers (35 to 85, 180 to 375, 599 to 749 R$), none giving power.
- A repeatable build product: +10 props for 80 R$ [data, Nov 2026], capped at 3 steps in public.
- One themed drop every Friday: 1 to 2 houses, 1 to 3 vehicles, 5 to 12 props.
- 30 players per server, even at 332K live.
