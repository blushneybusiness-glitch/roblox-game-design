> You pick a hero and fight 14 strangers in one free-for-all arena with no levels to grind. It set the template that most Roblox battlegrounds games copy, down to the pass prices.

## 1. Core loop

Controls from the game's description: left click punch, F block, Q dash, Q while ragdolled to cancel, double-tap W to run, G for ultimate, B for emotes. Timings are community wiki figures relayed by [rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/the-strongest-battlegrounds/updates/ragdoll-cancel-and-evasive), which says none are developer-published. **No developer-stated combat timing was found.**

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Close in | Run, dash | Forward/back dash 5 s cooldown, side dash 2 s | Dash of 3 to 4.5 tiles | Forward dash hit 4% |
| String | Hold left click | First hit in 9 to 15 frames (0.15 to 0.25 s, our arithmetic) | 4 hits | 3/3/4/4% = 14% |
| Ragdoll | 4th hit lands | Knockdown 2 s, then 0.5 s stun immunity | Target flies, lies down | Guaranteed on hit 4 |
| Answer | Victim presses Q | Cooldown not stated | Ragdoll cancel | Combo broken |
| Awaken | Press G with a full bar | 45 s ([wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Strongest_Battlegrounds)); some characters have no timer | New moveset | Bar fills from damage: 200 to 420 points by character |
| Kill | Finish the target | Not known | Kill feed | +1, or +2 for a finisher |

Cycle time per kill, respawn time and dead time: not known. Our arithmetic: 7 full strings kill an unblocking target. A Critical Hit is 3 light attacks' damage and a Black Flash 6, 24% on a mini uppercut or downslam, inside about 4 s after a Critical Hit (community).

## 3. Progression

There are no levels. Kills do not unlock characters. Progress is:

- **Techniques.** 176 listed, 17 universal, 33 for Martial Artist alone, with names such as "Curve Dash" ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/the-strongest-battlegrounds/techniques)).
- **Kill badges** ([Roblox badges API, 25 Nov 2026](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/3808081382/badges?limit=100)):

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 171,392,111 | 100% | |
| 10 | 92,301,778 | 53.9% | 53.9% |
| 100 | 32,438,447 | 18.9% | 35.1% |
| 1,000 | 10,019,762 | 5.8% | 30.9% |
| 10,000 | 1,091,741 | 0.64% | 10.9% |
| 100,000 | 850 | | |

- **Kill totals** unlock accessories and auras; monthly kill placement unlocks titles that reset each month; private-server kills do not count ([rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/the-strongest-battlegrounds/updates/how-to-unlock-every-character)).
- **Catalogue:** 31 accessories, 17 auras, 11 titles, 20 limited emotes ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/the-strongest-battlegrounds)).
- **Emote spin:** 50 kills or 25 R$. Our arithmetic: one kill is priced at 0.5 R$.
- **Ranked:** 1v1, 2v2, 3v3; point values not found.

**Roster.** The general wiki counts 10 playable characters plus 2 exclusives ([wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Strongest_Battlegrounds)); Bloxodes counts 14. Characters enter in early access and turn free later: Martial Artist after about 6 months (free 21 Dec 2024), Tech Prodigy after about 17 (21 Dec 2024 to 17 May 2026). These dates come from one community page that partly contradicts itself.

## 5. Other players

15 per server, one public free-for-all. Everyone sees everyone's auras and kills. No trading and nothing to lose but a streak. Bots ("Outsiders") appeared only for The Hunt event in March 2024. Clipped: named techs, Black Flashes, comebacks in awakening mode.

## 7. Why it works

Measured: 171M players have a kill; 54% of them reach 10 and 19% reach 100. Players praise: not recorded in the sources.

**Our read:**

- Every fight-deciding thing is free, so a new player loses only to skill.
- Progress is learned (176 techniques) and shown (auras), so the game needs no grind and no levels.
- Each 10x kill badge keeps about a third of the step before until 1,000.
- Early access sells the same head start again with every new character.

## 8. What went wrong

From the wiki's criticism section ([wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Strongest_Battlegrounds)): updates slower than promised (one gap near 4 months); free-for-all produces 2v1 and 3v1 fights; no visible anti-cheat; hit detection depends on ping. The rating, 83.7%, is below the grind RPGs (Blox Fruits 92.3%). Players went from an October average of 52,423 to 44,365 on 25 Nov 2026, inside the Halloween event.

## 9. What to take from it

- A 4-hit string at 3/3/4/4% ending in a forced 2 s ragdoll, 0.5 s immunity and a dash-cancel (community figures).
- Dash cooldowns of 2 s (side) and 5 s (forward/back).
- Kill badges at 1, 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000; expect about 54%, 19%, 6%, 0.6% of first-killers.
- A cosmetic roll every 50 kills, or 25 R$.
- Early access at 299 R$, free after 6 to 17 months; 10 of 12 playable characters free.
- 15 players per server in one shared arena.
- Rolls priced flat with no bulk discount (emote 25 R$, spin 79 R$), plus products named as server events: Spawn The Crab Boss 299 R$, Candy Rain 999 R$ [data, Nov 2026].
