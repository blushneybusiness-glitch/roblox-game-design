# Anime Vanguards

> You summon units from hourly banners at 50 gems a pull and send them through tower defense stages, raids and dungeons. It mattered because its wiki publishes every number of the gacha stack, and because a copyright (DMCA) problem forced it to redraw 12 of 14 planned units. It is now titled "Vanguards: Embers of Justice".

## 1. Core loop

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Summon | Pulls on the Special banner | a few | Unit reveal by rarity | Gems -50 (or -500 for 10) |
| Equip | Puts units in the team | a few | Team slots | Team power |
| Play a stage | Places and upgrades units over the waves | not known | Waves, boss | Story act: 80 gems, 3 to 6 Green Beans, 100 gold |
| Improve | Rerolls a trait, evolves a unit | a few | Trait roll | Reroll count down; Monarch 0.1% |
| Repeat | Back to the banner, which changes every hour | | New featured units | Pity counters up |

Cycle time and dead time: not known. Act length and wave count were not found. During waits the player can sit in the Time Chamber, an AFK screen that pays gems ([AV wiki Time Chamber](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Chamber)).

## 3. Progression

**Summon** ([AV wiki Summon](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Summon)):

| Rarity | Rate | Pity | Auto-sell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rare | 75.496% | | 50 |
| Epic | 20% | | 100 |
| Legendary | 4% | 50 pulls (2,500 gems) | 500 |
| Mythic / Exclusive | 0.5% | 400 pulls (20,000 gems) | 1,500 |
| Secret | 0.004% | | 3,000 |
| Vanguard | 0.005% | 25,000 pulls, once (1,250,000 gems) | 5,000 |

Pull 50 gems, 40 with VIP; 10 for 500. The banner rotates every hour: the centre unit takes 50% of Mythic results, each side unit 20%. A Selection banner lets the player fix the centre Mythic for 24 hours and shares pity. Legendary and Mythic pity reset when hit. Shiny chance 1.5%, 3% with the Shiny Hunter pass. Luck potions do not affect Secret, Vanguard or shiny and do not lower pity.

**Our read on pity:** the chance of no Legendary in 49 pulls is 0.96^49, about 13.5%, and of no Mythic in 399 pulls 0.995^399, also about 13.5%. Both pities sit at twice the expected count. The centre unit's rate is 0.25%, so it takes 400 pulls on average, the same as Mythic pity.

**Traits** ([AV wiki Traits](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Traits)): one reroll changes a unit's trait. Monarch 0.1% (pity 1,500; +300% damage, 1 placement), Ethereal 0.175% (pity 858), Deadeye 0.375% (pity 400), Solar 0.5% (pity 300), Blitz 1.85%, Fortune 2.5%, Marksman 6.5%. Pity is per unit.

**Evolution**: a Mythic needs 30,000 gold, 12 unit drops, 20 Red and 20 Green essence, 1 Rainbow; a Vanguard 100,000 gold and more essence ([AV wiki Update Log](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Log)).

**Modes** ([AV wiki Gamemodes](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Gamemodes)): Story 12 stages x 6 acts (Normal and Nightmare); 11 Legend stages; 4 Raids of 20 waves; Portals of 20 waves; Dungeons of 30 waves with modifier cards; weekly bosses; Rifts; Odyssey; PvP; Tournaments; Infinite.

## 4. Session and return hooks

- **Time Chamber**: 20 gems every 4 minutes (35 Premium, 40 VIP, 55 both): 300 / 525 / 600 / 825 gems an hour. Our arithmetic: one pull per 10 minutes; Mythic pity is 66.7 hours, or 19.4 hours with VIP and Premium (16,000 at 825).
- **Challenges** refresh 30 minutes, 24 hours or 7 days after a clear, capped at 20 a day, doubled on weekends.
- **Daily rewards**: day 1 1,000 gems, day 2 500 plus 5 rerolls, day 5 1,000, day 6 2,500, weekly reset ([AV wiki Daily Rewards](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Rewards)). Our arithmetic: 5,000 gems a week from those days, a quarter of Mythic pity.
- **Weekly**: 4 rotating bosses; a tournament paying the top 36 2 to 50 rerolls; Odyssey floor 13 pays 10 rerolls once a week.
- **Free rerolls**: the wiki sizes supply in weekly terms: about 35 from dailies, 40 each from the boss and PvP shops, plus one-off totals (206 from level milestones, 420 from achievements) ([AV wiki Trait Rerolls](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Trait_Rerolls)). Our arithmetic: Monarch pity (1,500) is about 13 weeks of those 115 a week.

## 5. Other players

Servers of 24, Rifts up to 6. Trading came in Update 9.0, at level 50, for limited units that can no longer be earned, skins, non-Vanguard familiars, gifted passes and Robux-bought rerolls. Traits and pity reset on a traded unit. A dupe means an automatic trade ban until review. No official value list ([AV wiki Trading](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Trading)). Serialized shinies (0.5% on top of shiny for event units) give the first 10 serials their own cosmetics. What gets clipped: not known.

## 7. Why it works

The developers delayed trading because one mistake could ruin the economy, and shipped anti-dupe checks first ([AV wiki Stage Notes](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Stage_Notes)). Measured: 96.7% rating, 44-minute average session. **Our read:**

- The faucet is open and the counters are long: idle play gives 144 pulls a day, while Vanguard pity needs 1,250,000 gems.
- Trait rerolls with their own pity keep players spending after their roster is complete.
- Four reset clocks (30 minutes, a day, a week, an hour for the banner) mean something finishes in every session.

## 8. What went wrong

The Update 8.5 notes say a DMCA impact delayed Update 9.0, all content must now be original, and 12 of 14 planned units needed rework. The developers refer to avoiding the "Defenders route", where characters stayed recognisable. The game now carries the title "Vanguards: Embers of Justice" (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). **Our read:** the rename fits the copyright pressure, though no source in hand states it as the reason. Players fell from 340,167 to 10,576, 3.1% of peak; a 30-day peak of 28,943 shows updates still lift it.

## 9. What to take from it

- Pull 50 gems, 10 for 500, banner changes every hour with a 50 / 20 / 20 split.
- Legendary pity 50, Mythic pity 400: both at twice the expected pulls; about 900 and 7,200 R$ at 18 R$ a pull [data, Nov 2026; our arithmetic].
- Idle room at 300 gems an hour, one pull per 10 minutes; VIP doubles it.
- First clear 100 gems, repeat clear 80.
- Trait rerolls with per-unit pity from 300 to 1,500.
- Trading only from level 50, only for items that can no longer be earned.
