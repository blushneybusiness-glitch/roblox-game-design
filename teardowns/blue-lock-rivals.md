## 3. Progression

There are 32 styles and 25 flows. Styles are character kits; flows are an awakening layer rolled on a separate spin.

**Style Spin odds**, community table "as of August 2026" ([Bloxguides](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/blue-lock-rivals), [wiki mirror](https://bluelockrivals-wiki.wiki/styles/tier-list/)):

| Rarity | Regular | Lucky Spin | Expected regular spins (our arithmetic) | Cash (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rare | 62.25% | 0% | | |
| Epic | 30% | 0% | | |
| Legendary | 7% | 92.5% | 14 | 35,700 |
| Mythic | 0.5% | 5% | 200 | 500,000 |
| World Class | 0.25% | 2.5% | 400 | 1,000,000 |
| Limited | 0.125% | 1% | 800 | 2,000,000 |
| Master | 0.0325% | 0.2% | 3,077 | 7,690,000 |

The columns sum to 100.16% and 101.2%. An older table gave Master 0.0175% regular and 0.175% lucky. The Fandom wiki lists only Rare to World Class, with the same odds for styles and flows ([wiki: Flow](https://blue-lock-rivals.fandom.com/wiki/Flow)). Flow examples: Godspeed 0.175%, Crow 0.4% regular and 7% lucky ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/roblox/news/how-to-get-the-loki-style-and-godspeed-flow-in-blue-lock-rivals), [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/crow-blue-lock-rivals-flow-overview-get)).

**Pity:** after 50 Style Spins with no Legendary or better, the next one is Legendary or better. Flow has its own 50-spin pity. Our arithmetic: Legendary or better is 7.9% per spin, so a player reaches 50 dry spins only 1.6% of the time. Nothing guarantees Mythic, World Class, Limited or Master.

**Lucky Spins** at 199 R$ (one guide, and it is not clear what the price is attached to). Our arithmetic at that price: a Mythic in 20 spins (3,980 R$), a World Class in 40 (7,960 R$), a Master in 500 (99,500 R$).

**Ranked** with ELO arrived in June 2025, with limited ranked rewards; one guide reports another ranked update in March 2026 ([earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/blue-lock-rivals-vs-volleyball-legends)). Ranked gate and ladder: not known.

## 7. Why it works

No official developer statements were found.

**Our read:**
- The spin sells a playstyle from a known anime, so every roll changes how the next match plays.
- A Legendary arrives about every 14 spins, so most players own one inside their first spending.
- Ten players per server means a goal always has an audience of nine.
- Four of the twelve passes sell a sound for a moment of play (goal, steal, save, ankle breaker).

## 8. What went wrong

Peak 1,097,082 in March 2025 to 14,469 now: a fall of 98.7% in 20 months. The 30-day peak (91,395) is 8% of the all-time peak. Cause: not known. **Our read:** the top tiers have no ceiling. A Master takes 3,077 regular spins on average and the only pity stops at Legendary, so the long hunt has no visible end. Compare Volleyball Legends' second pity in [genres/sports.md](../genres/sports.md).

## 9. What to take from it

- Two separate spins (style 2,500, flow 2,000), each with its own 50-spin pity.
- Odds ladder 62 / 30 / 7 / 0.5 / 0.25 / 0.125 / 0.03%.
- Paid spin removes the bottom two tiers and multiplies top tiers 6 to 10 times.
- One 5v5 match per 10-player server, with 3v3 and 8v8 variants.
- Cosmetic sound passes at 149 R$ for named moments; skip-animation pass at 99.
- Loadout slots sold one at a time on a rising ladder, 113 to 499 R$ for slots 2 to 7 [data, Nov 2026].
