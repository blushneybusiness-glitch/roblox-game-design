# Teardowns

How individual hits work, in numbers. Each follows [the same template](../kit/templates/teardown.md): loop,
first session, progression, return hooks, other players, money, why it works, what went wrong, and what to take
from it. Unless a page says otherwise, the game was studied from its wiki, Roblox's public endpoints and
published sources in November 2026, and was not timed on a device.

| Game | In one line |
|---|---|
| [99 Nights in the Forest](99-nights-in-the-forest.md) | A party of 1 to 5 keeps a campfire alive for 99 in-game days while rescuing four lost kids; it set the 2025 benchmark for co-op survival runs with a 14 million player peak. |
| [Adopt Me!](adopt-me.md) | You hatch pets, raise them by filling their needs, and trade them toward rarer and glowing versions; it is the longest-running pet game at the top of Roblox. |
| [Animal Hospital (Anomaly)](animal-hospital.md) | Check in animal patients, test each one three ways, and shut out the ones that are not really animals, while the hospital fills with hazards. It was the anomaly-spotting hit of 2026 and won Best New Game, then fell to about 1% to 2% of its peak in under five months. |
| [Anime Vanguards](anime-vanguards.md) | You summon units from hourly banners at 50 gems a pull and send them through tower defense stages, raids and dungeons. It mattered because its wiki publishes every number of the gacha stack, and because a copyright (DMCA) problem forced it to redraw 12 of 14 planned units. It is now titled "Vanguards: Embers of Justice". |
| [BedWars](bedwars.md) | Teams collect iron from a generator, buy blocks and weapons, guard their bed and break everyone else's. Once your bed is gone, your next death is final. It peaked at 334,747 players in 2022 and sells 113 kits. |
| [Bee Swarm Simulator](bee-swarm-simulator.md) | Your bees gather pollen, your hive turns it into honey, honey buys more bees: a gather-and-convert simulator that still holds about 49,000 players 8.7 years after it was made. |
| [Blox Fruits](blox-fruits.md) | You take quests, level up across three seas and hunt for fruits that give powers. It is the template for the anime grind RPG and has held hundreds of thousands of players since 2019. |
| [Blue Lock: Rivals](blue-lock-rivals.md) | 5v5 anime soccer where each player's moves come from a style rolled on a spin. It reached 1,097,082 players in March 2025, the biggest peak of any Roblox sports game on the sports page, and has since lost almost all of it. |
| [Brookhaven RP](brookhaven.md) | A town where the house, the car and the job are free from the first second, and players make up the story; it is the biggest roleplay game on Roblox. |
| [Build a Boat for Treasure](build-a-boat-for-treasure.md) | Build a boat from blocks, launch it down a river of stages, and earn gold for every stage it survives. It shows how to wrap a free building toy in one clear goal, and it has done so since 2016. |
| [Dead Rails](dead-rails.md) | Up to four players drive a train 80 km through a zombie-filled West, feeding it anything that burns; it peaked at about 1.5 million players and made fuel the clock of a journey game. |
| [Doors](doors.md) | Open 100 numbered doors in a haunted hotel with up to three friends, and answer each monster with the one move that beats it. It set the template for one tell, one counter and one fixed damage number per threat. |
| [Dress to Impress](dress-to-impress.md) | Up to 13 players get a theme, dress for 6 minutes, walk a runway one at a time and rate each other from 1 to 5 stars. It made the timed fashion contest one of Roblox's largest formats of 2024, and every dress-up game of 2026 copies it. |
| [Epic Minigames](epic-minigames.md) | Twelve players play a random playlist of about 141 small games, one after another; it has run since 2015 and sells control over the playlist rather than power. |
| [Fisch](fisch.md) | You cast, win an 8-second reeling minigame, and fill a bestiary of 1,527 fish across 153 locations; it mattered because it set the template that Fish It!, The Forge and Prospecting! followed. |
| [Forsaken](forsaken.md) | One killer hunts the other players of a 9-player server for a 4-minute round while they repair generators. It is the live benchmark for one-killer-versus-many horror on Roblox, and it peaked above 1.2 million players. |
| [Grow a Garden](grow-a-garden.md) | Buy seeds when the shop restocks, plant, harvest, sell, and show off lucky crops. It set Roblox's concurrent-player record twice in 2025 and is the reference for grow-and-harvest. |
| [Jailbreak](jailbreak.md) | Escape prison, rob banks and stores, and outrun the police, or play the police and cash in criminals' bounties. It is the longest-running open-world cops-and-robbers game on Roblox, with 8.1 billion visits. |
| [Murder Mystery 2](murder-mystery-2.md) | One murderer, one sheriff and up to ten innocents play a 3-minute round of hide, hunt and guess; a trading economy of cosmetic knives and guns has kept a 2014 game above 100,000 concurrent players in 2026. |
| [Pet Simulator 99](pet-simulator-99.md) | Hatch pets, break things with them, buy the next area: the longest priced pet ladder on Roblox with public numbers, still in the top 10 four years after launch. |
| [RIVALS](rivals.md) | Short gun duels, first to 5 rounds, queued from pads on a shared lobby floor. It is the only Roblox shooter at scale in 2026. |
| [Sol's RNG](sols-rng.md) | You press one button every 3.2 seconds and get an aura labelled "1 in N"; it matters because it is three years old, still has about 40,000 players, and does it with one button and a set of clocks built around it. |
| [Steal a Brainrot](steal-a-brainrot.md) | Buy meme characters off a shared carpet, earn from them at a base, lock it, and steal from the neighbours. The first Roblox game past 25 million concurrent players, and the template for steal-and-defend. |
| [Steal An Egg](steal-an-egg.md) | Train Speed on a treadmill, run into a farther biome, grab an egg and get it home before the guardian or another player knocks you down. The biggest slop hit of 2026 and the bridge between steal-and-defend and plus-one. |
| [The Strongest Battlegrounds](the-strongest-battlegrounds.md) | You pick a hero and fight 14 strangers in one free-for-all arena with no levels to grind. It set the template that most Roblox battlegrounds games copy, down to the pass prices. |
| [Throw Food For Animals](throw-food-for-animals.md) | Train Throw Power, throw food from a launch strip into farther areas, and the animal it attracts goes to your pen and earns. The newest giant of the plus-one family, at 2.47M players three weeks after its first update. |
| [Tower Defense Simulator](tower-defense-simulator.md) | You place and upgrade towers against 20 to 50 waves with up to three friends, and unlock new towers with coins and levels. It mattered because it set its all-time peak more than six years after it was created, with no summon banner. |
| [Tower of Hell](tower-of-hell.md) | Climb a randomly built tower with no checkpoints before the clock runs out; it is the obby that has held a large audience the longest, with 29.5B visits in eight years. |
