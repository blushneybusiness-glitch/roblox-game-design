## 1. Core loop

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Points pets at breakables | not known | Pets swarm the object; coins burst out | Coins |
| 2 | Hatches the current area's egg | not known | Egg opens; pet and rarity shown | Pets owned |
| 3 | Equips the best pets | not known | Pets follow the player | Damage on breakables |
| 4 | Buys the next area | not known | Gate opens; new biome, new egg | Area (1 to 274) |

Cycle time and dead time: not known. Coins per break are not on the wiki, so time per area cannot be estimated. During waits the player can claim timed gifts (section 4) or run the Classic Obby at area 5.

First reward, first purchase and first rare moment: not timed. First contact with others: the Trading Plaza, opened at rebirth 1 (area 25). First long goal shown: not known. The wiki has no beginner's guide.

## 3. Progression

**Eggs** ([wiki template](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Template:PS99World1EggsList)). Prices 90, 150, 250, 650, 1,500, 4,000, 6,000, 9,500, 20,000, 45,000 coins for eggs 1 to 10, then 70k, 100k, 200k, 350k, 500k; from egg 16 eggs cost Gold Bars. Average step 1.85x (our arithmetic). Odds are listed per pet: a 60% to 90% filler, a 20% to 38% middle pet, and a 3% to 6% prize, until egg 9's Flamingo at 0.16%. Egg 10 then offers Flamingo at 6.1%. The wiki counts 281 eggs as of Update 68.

**Areas.** Coins: 900, 2.5k, 8k, 20k, 60k, 150k, 400k for areas 2 to 8 (2.76x average step). Gold Bars from area 9: 1, 2, 6, 15, 35, 80 ... 750,000 at area 25 (2.33x). Platinum Coins from area 26, with a 500,000 Gold Bar bridge payment ([wiki template](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Template:PS99SpawnWorldAreaList), [itemlevel](https://itemlevel.net/pet-simulator-99-complete-biomes-list-update-5/)). World 1 runs through Emerald and Sapphire currencies to area 99. The wiki counts 274 areas in 4 worlds and 8 sub-worlds; third-party guides say 279.

Features unlock by area: area 4 pet-equip machine, 6 potion vending, 8 Egg Machine, 9 enchants, 20 Daycare and Boss Chest, 28 Fuse Machine, 31 Golden Machine, 41 Rainbow Machine.

**Rebirths** ([wiki: Rebirth](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth_(Pet_Simulator_99))). Maximum 9 as of Update 79. Rebirths no longer reset areas or coins ([wiki: Ranks](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks_(Pet_Simulator_99))). Rebirth 1 is at area 25. Requirements for 2 to 9 are not known.

| Rebirth | Diamonds | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10,000 | Cannon teleport, free Auto Hatch, Castle Key (Trading Plaza, Spinny Wheel) |
| 2 | 25,000 | Clans; Huge pets hatchable from the best egg |
| 3 | 50,000 | Pet teams, enchant loadouts, Index rewards, gift bundle |
| 4 | 100,000 | Ultimates, Tech World, Mastery |
| 5 | (items instead) | Secret pets hatchable, Area Quests, shiny chance boost |
| 6 to 9 | 500,000 each | Charged and Golden Eggs (6), Void World (8), Ultra Mastery potion (9) |

Every rebirth also gives +75% pet strength; the wiki does not say whether these add or multiply.

**Pet tiers.** 10 identical pets make one golden (+13%); 10 goldens make one rainbow (+27%); both machines are free ([Golden Machine](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Machine_(Pet_Simulator_99)), [Rainbow Machine](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Rainbow_Machine_(Pet_Simulator_99))). Huge pets deal your strongest pet's damage times a level and variant bonus, so they never fall behind; Titanics are 3x your best pets ([Huge Pets](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Huge_Pets_(Pet_Simulator_99)), [Titanic Pets](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Titanic_Pets_(Pet_Simulator_99))). Eleven rarities, Basic to Secret. Huge and Titanic hatch odds: not known.

**Enchants.** 6 free slots from ranks 1, 3, 5, 8, 11, 20, plus 3 bought slots (400, 800, 1,200 R$). Copies of one enchant count 100%, 60%, 38.3%, 27.5% ([wiki: Enchants](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Enchants_(Pet_Simulator_99))).

Rebirth chests refill every 1, 2, 3 and 4 hours. Ranks run to 40. Daily login and playtime rewards: not known. Update day: not known; the last update was Tuesday 24 Nov 2026.

## 7. Why it works

No developer statements were found. Measured: 110,007 players and 94.7% approval four years after launch, while the same chart's top spot is held by a four-month-old egg game.

**Our read:**
- Every price is in a currency where the number stays under about a million, then a new currency starts.
- The next egg makes the last egg's prize common, so each area is a visible step.
- Rebirth adds features instead of taking progress away.
- Pads from 30 minutes to 5 hours give a reason to open the game several times a day.

## 8. What went wrong

The in-game tooltip says a rainbow pet is "10x Better"; the wiki measures about +27% over normal. Sources disagree on basic counts (8 or 9 rebirths, 274 or 279 areas). The wiki marks the top pet's odds in many eggs from egg 13 on as "??". A decline is not visible in our numbers.

## 9. What to take from it

- Egg prices step about 2x; the rarest pet stays at 3% to 6% for the first 8 eggs.
- Egg N's prize returns at about 6% in egg N+1.
- Area price equals 4 to 10 current eggs; a new currency every 17 areas at about 750,000.
- Rebirth every 25 areas with no reset, paid in features plus a diamond bundle that grows 2x to 2.5x.
- Duplicate sink: 10 copies for +13%, 100 for +27%.
- Free pads with cooldowns of 30 minutes to 5 hours.
- Paid eggs in ladders of 1, 3, 10 and 100, from 90 R$ to 39.5 R$ an egg; the top egg pack costs 32,000 R$ [data, Nov 2026].
