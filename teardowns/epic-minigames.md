# Epic Minigames

> Twelve players play a random playlist of about 141 small games, one after another; it has run since 2015 and sells control over the playlist rather than power.

## 1. Core loop

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lobby | Plays with the Spleef area, cannon, pool, slide, beach | not known | Shop stalls, a leaderboard room, a fan-art room, an Update Board | none |
| Pick | Waits, or spends a minigame choice | not known | The next minigame: random unless someone paid | Choices owned |
| Play | Follows one rule: dodge, jump, shoot, survive | not known | Solo Winner, Free-For-All (some Top 3), or Team (Red against Blue) | Players left |
| Result | Wins or is out | not known | Losers wait in the lobby | 10 coins per win (15 on Pro) |

Cycle time and dead time: not known; the wiki gives no round or intermission seconds. A Solo Winner game with several survivors at the timeout ends in a draw ([wiki: Solo Winner](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Solo_Winner)). Dead players wait in the lobby, which is built as a playground for that wait ([wiki: The Lobby](https://epic-minigames-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Lobby)).

Count of minigames: the wiki says 141 in 2026, the game's description 142, an older guide 120 or more ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/epic-minigames-guide)). Many have 2 to 4 map variants, so one ruleset fills several playlist slots ([wiki: Free-For-All](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Free-For-All)). 12 Team games are listed ([wiki: Team](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Team)). A second mode, Epic Party, is a board game where players race for crystals.

## 3. Progression

**Coins per win:**

| Pass | Normal and Large | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| None | 10 | 15 |
| VIP | 15 | 20 |
| Double Coins | 20 | 25 |
| Both | 25 | 30 |

**Other coin sources:** Community Chest 30 once an hour; 3 daily missions at 30 each (a 4th with VIP; all 3 give a random effect, gear or title); daily rewards of 25, 50 or 100 on set days; playtime 15, 30 and 45 at 5, 20 and 40 minutes (reset 00:00 UTC); Invite Friends 100 ([Daily missions](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_missions)).

Our arithmetic: in a 12-player Solo Winner game with equal chances, a player expects 10 / 12, about 0.8 coins per game from winning. A 40-minute session that clears 3 missions and one chest earns 210 coins from timers. The timers carry the economy for most players.

**Server ladder:** Pro servers unlock at level 24 with the Pro badge or for R$199, pay 15 coins a win and shorten timers in some games. Large servers hold 36 and allow up to 3 winners in solo games. Private servers have been free since 8 Jan 2021. Level curve: not known.

**Epic Pass** ([wiki](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Pass)): launched 11 Mar 2022; 15 seasons by 1 Dec 2025. Since Season 5 it starts by itself at 00:00 UTC on 1 March, 1 June, 1 September and 1 December, and runs 56 days. Stars come from daily and weekly challenges. Unspent stars convert at 5 coins each. Star-shop coin items cost 5, 10 and 20 stars for 200, 500 and 1,500 coins (our arithmetic: 40, 50 and 75 coins per star, 8 to 15 times the conversion rate).

## 7. Why it works

Developer statements: not found. Measured: 11 years live, 2.37B visits, 1,190 players on a weekday in Nov 2026.

**Our read:**

- The product is variety. 141 rules and their map variants mean a player rarely sees the same game twice in a session.
- Paid choice sells control of a random playlist. It changes what everyone plays without making the buyer stronger, so it avoids paid random items and pay-to-win.
- Timer income outweighs win income for an average player, so losing does not stop progress.
- A pass on fixed calendar dates returns four times a year without an announcement.

## 8. What went wrong

Three experiments were tried and removed:

| Experiment | Ran | Days (our arithmetic) | What it tried | Why it ended |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Massive servers | 2 to 16 May 2020 | 14 | 72 players, six times normal | not stated |
| Voice Chat servers | 22 Jan to 12 Mar 2022 | 49 | A voice server type | Unpopular |
| Choice Rains | 28 Jul to 21 Oct 2023 | 85 | Paid rains: 1,000 / 2,500 / 10,000 coins for R$149 / 369 / 1,299; 15 / 40 / 100 choices for R$199 / 519 / 1,249 | Drops got stuck on the lobby roof |

**Our read:** each change touched the server shape or the lobby, and each was undone. A 72-player server turns a 1-in-12 win into 1 in 72; a voice server splits the queue; a physical drop needs a lobby built to catch it. The safe pattern: run a change for a few weeks with a removal date in mind.

Decline: peak history is not known, so the size of any decline is not known.

## 9. What to take from it

1. 12 players per server, so an even-chance solo win comes about 1 game in 12.
2. Timer rewards of 15 / 30 / 45 coins at 5 / 20 / 40 minutes against 10 coins a win.
3. Paid pick of the next round priced per seat: R$9 for 12, R$27 for 36.
4. A 56-day pass that starts at 00:00 UTC on the 1st of March, June, September and December.
5. 2 to 4 map variants per ruleset to multiply content.
6. A monthly cap on private-server wins (1,000) so leaderboards stay honest.
