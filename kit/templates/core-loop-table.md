## If dead time is over one third

Pick one for each waiting step and write it in:

1. **Shorten it.** Cut the wait until the step is under 2 seconds.
2. **Give the wait a job.** The player collects, aims, dodges, repositions, chooses the next target or watches another player during it.
3. **Make the wait the tension.** The outcome is visibly approaching and uncertain: a bar filling toward a risky edge, an enemy closing in, a reveal counting down.
4. **Move the wait off-screen.** It becomes a timer the player leaves and returns to, and another loop fills the gap.
