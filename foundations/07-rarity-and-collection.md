# Rarity and collection

Open this page when you write an odds table, set a pity counter, decide when a roll gets a cutscene or a server message, or add an index, badges or trading. It covers the odds real Roblox games publish, the gaps between tiers, the pity formula, how reveals are staged, what collections pay, and where trading breaks.

## What is known

### Two rolls, not one

Most collection games roll twice: once for the thing, then once for an upgrade on it.

| Game | Roll 1: the thing | Roll 2: the upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| [Steal a Brainrot](../teardowns/steal-a-brainrot.md) | unit by spawn weight on the conveyor ([wiki](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Carpet)) | Gold x1.25 at 10%, Diamond x1.5 at 5%, Rainbow x10 at 1% ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/steal-a-brainrot/mutations)) |
| [Anime Vanguards](../teardowns/anime-vanguards.md) | unit by rarity | shiny 1.5% (3% with a 1,299 R$ pass); then a separate trait reroll ([wiki](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Summon)) |
| [Fisch](../teardowns/fisch.md) | fish by zone, time and weather | one mutation per fish; Shiny and Sparkling 1.85x at 1%, Alpha 2.5x at 0.1% ([Fischipedia](https://fischipedia.org/wiki/Mutations)) |
| [Grow a Garden](../teardowns/grow-a-garden.md) | seed from the shop | Silver x5, Gold x20, Rainbow x50, plus weather mutations up to x365 ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/tools/grow-a-garden-crop-value-calculator)) |

Steal a Brainrot's copy counts match its belt odds: Gold 10.2%, Diamond 5.0%, Rainbow 0.9% of all copies of its entry unit (our arithmetic from the [wiki](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Noobini_Pizzanini)).

**Our read:** the second roll gives a common pull a 1-in-6 chance (SAB: 16%) of a visible tag, so the bottom of the table still produces a reveal.

### Pity

Hard pity guarantees the tier at pull N. Expected pulls: `E = (1 − (1 − p)^N) / p`; the effective rate is 1/E ([design maths notes](https://library.keqingmains.com/evidence/general-mechanics/gacha) for the gacha rule; formula is our arithmetic).

| Case | Base rate | Pity | Expected pulls | Share who reach pity | Worst case / mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AV Mythic ([wiki](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Summon)) | 0.5% | 400 | 173 (about 8,650 gems vs 20,000 worst) | 13.5% | 2.3x |
| AV Legendary | 4% | 50 | 21.8 (about 1,090 gems) | 13% | 2.3x |
| BLR Legendary+ ([Bloxguides](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/blue-lock-rivals)) | 7.9% | 50 | 12.4 (about 31,100 cash) | 1.6% | 4x |
| Volleyball Legends Legendary+ ([RBLXGuide](https://rblxguide.com/games/volleyball-legends/styles)) | 2.5% | 50 | 28.6 | 28% | 1.7x |
| Combat Warriors Epic, daily spin ([wiki](https://combat-warriors.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_spin)) | 1.5% | 75 | 45 days average, 75 worst | 32% | 1.7x |
| ALS Mythic (odds flagged above) | 0.5% | 250 | 143 | 29% | 1.7x |

Without pity, the tail is long:

| Case | Rate | Expected rolls | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink Game Mythic power | 0.1% | 1,000; 50% chance by 693, 99% by 4,603 | about 169,900 R$ at 10 rolls for 1,699 ([wiki](https://ink-game.fandom.com/wiki/Powers)) |
| [Murder Mystery 2](../teardowns/murder-mystery-2.md) box godly | 0.2% | 500 boxes | 500,000 coins at 40 coins a round ([wiki](https://murder-mystery-2.fandom.com/wiki/Boxes)) |
| King Legacy copper Legendary | 0.05% | 2,000 keys | 200 hours at 10 keys an hour ([wiki](https://king-legacy.fandom.com/wiki/Gacha)) |
| Toilet Tower Defense Mythic | 0.1% | 1,000 crates | 100,000 coins; at 50 coins per 7 to 15 min Easy win, over 200 hours ([wiki](https://toilet-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Easy_Mode)) |

Other guarantee shapes:

- **Soft pity.** Phantom Forces cases add +0.25% Legendary per case opened and reset on a hit ([wiki](https://roblox-phantom-forces.fandom.com/wiki/Skins)).
- **Chosen pity.** Volleyball Legends: after 200 spins a 50% chance at a chosen Secret, after another 200 a certainty ([VL news](https://volleyballlegends.github.io/news/master-spins-and-pity-mechanics-in-volleyball-legends.html)).
- **Timed guarantee on a shared shop.** Steal a Brainrot's conveyor guarantees a Legendary within 5 minutes, a Mythic within 15, and a Brainrot God within about 45 (the God figure is the wiki's estimate) ([wiki](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Carpet)). Every player in the server sees the same unit, so the guarantee also starts a race.

Players also stack trait pity on top: AV's Monarch trait is 0.1% with pity at 1,500 rerolls ([wiki](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Traits)).

### Reveal staging

| Game | What the player sees and hears |
|---|---|
| Sol's RNG, 1 in 1,000 (Epic) | black-screen cutscene and a server chat line naming player, aura and odds |
| Sol's RNG, 1 in 10,000 (Unique) | adds a drop-beat, a coloured star and lines flashing around the map |
| Sol's RNG, 1 in 100M (Glorious) | every player in the server gets a monochrome effect, thunder and a beam; a [GLOBAL] chat line ([wiki](https://sol-rng.fandom.com/wiki/Cutscenes)) |
| Sol's RNG settings | players set their own cutscene threshold; a guide suggests about 1 in 1,000 ([rblxguide](https://rblxguide.com/games/sols-rng/updates/beginners-guide)) |
| Steal a Brainrot | Brainrot God and Secret spawns announced to the server, OG spawns to all servers; a theft is broadcast coloured by rarity ([wiki](https://stealabrainrot.fandom.com/wiki/Stealing)) |
| Blade Ball crates | roulette of about 5 s ([wiki](https://bladeball.fandom.com/wiki/Crates)) |

Cutscene length in seconds: not known for any game.

Roll speed sets how often a server hears a line. Sol's rolls take about 3.2 s, or 1.2 s with the 100 R$ Quick Roll pass ([wiki](https://sol-rng.fandom.com/wiki/Rolling)). Our arithmetic: 20 players at 1,125 rolls an hour each, base luck, give about 22 Epic-or-better chat lines an hour (one every 3 minutes) and 2.25 Unique-or-better.

Players pay to skip the animation: Volleyball Legends 49 R$, UBG 25, Blue Lock: Rivals 99, Blade Ball 120, Jujutsu Infinite 149 ([Rolimons VL](https://www.rolimons.com/game/73956553001240); [UBG passes API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/4730278139/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100); [Rolimons BLR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/18668065416); [Rolimons Blade Ball](https://www.rolimons.com/game/13772394625); [JI passes API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/3808223175/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100)). **Our read:** a reveal that plays on every common pull becomes a wait players pay to remove.

### Expected value of mutation tables

Expected multiplier = sum of chance × multiplier (our arithmetic).

| Table | Expected multiplier | Top multiplier / expected |
|---|---|---|
| SAB belt: 84% none, 10% x1.25, 5% x1.5, 1% x10 | 1.14 | 8.8x |
| [Throw Food For Animals](../teardowns/throw-food-for-animals.md) (odds unconfirmed) | 1.41 ([slop/02-why-it-holds.md](../slop/02-why-it-holds.md)) | 14x |
| Fish It: 12 mutations, 14.9% total, one per catch assumed ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/fish-it/mutations)) | 1.34 | 4.9x |
| Fisch natural mutations | about 1.02 | 2.5x |
| [slop/04-build-it.md](../slop/04-build-it.md) start table: 82/11/5/1.5/0.5% at x1/1.5/3/10/20 | 1.385 | 14x |

**Our read:** the second roll adds 14% to 41% to average income while the top result is 5x to 14x the average. The average shapes the economy; the top result shapes the clip.

### Paid random items: the rules in three lines

Since 26 May 2026: numeric odds summing to 100% before purchase, updated live while a luck boost is active, and paid pity and luck count as paid random items. Every outcome must give something. Gate with `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted`, offer an alternative in Australia, Belgium, the Netherlands, the UK and Brazil, and gate trading with `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/clarifying-requirements-for-paid-random-items/4654622); [docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)). Details: [15-the-line.md](15-the-line.md).

## The rules

1. **Make the last tier step the biggest, 25x to 125x.** AV (125x), TTD (100x), Anime Defenders (25x) and Ink Game (29x) all end that way. Our read: a top tier far beyond the one below is what makes its announcement news.
2. **Keep the first steps at 2x to 4x.** SAB, TTD and King Legacy open at 1.7x to 2.5x, so the first 10 pulls show at least 3 tiers.
3. **Cap the worst case at 1.5x to 2x the expected pulls.** AV's Mythic pity is 2.3x the mean and 13.5% of players hit it; Volleyball Legends' is 1.7x. A 0.1% item with no pity takes 4,603 rolls for a 99% chance.
4. **Show the pity counter on the roll screen.** Pity counts only if the player sees "n / 400"; Combat Warriors shows 75 of 75.
5. **Scale the reveal to rarity, and let players skip below their own threshold.** Sol's: instant below 1 in 1,000, cutscene above, server effect at 1 in 100M. Skip passes selling at 25 to 149 R$ show what an unskippable reveal costs.
6. **Pick the announcement tier so a server hears one line every 3 to 5 minutes.** Sol's base rate gives about one every 3 minutes at 1 in 1,000.
7. **Keep the second roll's expected value at 1.1 to 1.4.** SAB 1.14, Fish It 1.34. A higher average inflates the whole income ladder.
8. **Pay for completing a collection with something visible.** Fisch pays a bobber and gates rods at 50% and 100%; SAB adds +0.5x per index entry.
9. **Gate trading before it opens.** Every game in the trading table added a level, victory or tier gate; MM2 and TTD added theirs after dupes.

## How to do it

1. Write the odds table in [kit/templates/economy-sheet.md](../kit/templates/economy-sheet.md). Check it sums to 100% and compute the step ratios.
2. For each tier above 1%, compute E with the pity formula and the share who reach pity, `(1 − p)^N`. Change N until worst / mean is 1.5x to 2x.
3. Compute rolls per server-hour: players × 3,600 / seconds per roll. Multiply by each tier's rate. Announce the first tier that lands about 12 to 20 times an hour.
4. Compute the second roll's expected value and put it in the `income_multiplier` field for [tools/pacing.py](../tools/pacing.py).
5. If any roll costs R$, follow [15-the-line.md](15-the-line.md) before you build the UI.

**Start with:**

| Tier | Chance | Step from the tier above | Pity | Reveal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 58% | | | instant, under 0.5 s |
| Uncommon | 27% | 2.1x | | instant, colour flash |
| Rare | 10.5% | 2.6x | | about 1 s with a sound |
| Epic | 3.6% | 2.9x | | 2 s |
| Legendary | 0.8% | 4.5x | Legendary+ at 150: E 82.5, 26% reach it, worst 1.8x mean | 3 s cutscene plus a server line |
| Mythic | 0.098% | 8.2x | Mythic+ at 1,500: E 777, 22% reach it, worst 1.9x mean | effect for the whole server |
| Secret | 0.002% | 49x | none; the chase | message to every server |

Reasoning: the step ratios follow the real tables (2x to 3x at the bottom, a near-50x last step). Pity values are set by the 1.5x to 2x rule. Announcement: 10 players rolling every 3 s make 12,000 rolls an hour, so Mythic+ lands about 12 times an hour, one every 5 minutes; Secret lands 0.24 times an hour per server, rare enough to send to all servers. On a shared shop, use timed guarantees in place of pity: Legendary within 5 minutes, Mythic within 15, as on SAB's conveyor.

Second roll: use the slop table (82/11/5/1.5/0.5% at x1/1.5/3/10/20, expected 1.385) or SAB's (expected 1.14) for a slower ladder.

## Checks

- [ ] Every odds table sums to 100% and is shown with numbers before any paid roll.
- [ ] Step ratios rise from bottom to top, with the last step 25x or more.
- [ ] The first 10 pulls show 3 or more tiers in expectation.
- [ ] Every tier at or above 0.5% has pity, with worst case 2x the mean or less.
- [ ] The pity counter is on the roll screen.
- [ ] Rolls below the player's chosen threshold resolve in under 0.5 s.
- [ ] Each server hears one announcement every 3 to 5 minutes.
- [ ] The second roll's expected value is 1.1 to 1.4, and its value is in the pacing file.
- [ ] Completing an index pays something shown on the player or their base.
- [ ] At least one badge is held by under 1% of players within the first month.
- [ ] Trading has a level or progress gate and a confirm wait of 5 s or more before launch.

## Mistakes

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Players skip or pay to skip every roll | Reveal plays on commons | Instant below Rare; player-set cutscene threshold |
| Server chat is a wall of rare finds | Announcement tier too low | Move it up until lines come every 3 to 5 minutes |
| No one has seen the top tier after a week | Rate too low with no pity or chase path | Add a chosen pity or a lucky version that removes bottom tiers |
| Forum posts about 1,000 rolls without a hit | No pity, long tail | Hard pity at 1.5x to 2x the mean |
| Income ladder finishes early | Second roll's expected value above 1.4 | Lower mutation chances; put the expected value into pacing.py |
| Index ignored | Completion pays nothing visible | Add a tool, a slot, a title or a % bonus at 50% and 100% |
| Values collapse after an update | Duplication through trading | Serials, level gate, trade ban on detection, and a sink for dupes (TTD's Clone Crate) |
| Game removed or blocked in some countries | Paid odds not shown, or no restricted-user path | Follow [15-the-line.md](15-the-line.md) |
