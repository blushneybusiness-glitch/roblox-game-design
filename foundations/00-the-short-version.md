# The short version

The whole handbook in twenty-five lines. Each line links to the page with the evidence. Numbers are from the hits as measured in November 2026; values to aim for are marked "start with".

## The idea

1. **Sell one feeling, in one sentence a nine-year-old would repeat.** "It's the game where you steal other people's pets." ([03](03-idea-and-reference.md))
2. **Start from a live game and change the verb.** Steal became throw, kick, break. A reskin that kept the verb, made by a top studio, has 7 players. ([03](03-idea-and-reference.md), [slop/03](../slop/03-quality-bar.md))
3. **The title is the instruction.** A verb and a thing, two to four words. Bait titles carry like ratios of 59% to 70%; plain ones 85% to 97%. ([11](11-clips-and-packaging.md))

## The first five minutes

4. **Keep the title's promise within 30 seconds.** Start with: first input in 5 seconds, first reward in 10 to 15, first purchase in 45 to 60, a long goal on screen by minute 5. ([05](05-first-session.md))
5. **Nothing can fail in the first minute.** The hits hand over a thing that pays for itself in 8 to 25 seconds. ([05](05-first-session.md))
6. **No text, no offers, no pop-ups.** The hits have no tutorial. Their descriptions are five lines of verbs. ([05](05-first-session.md))
7. **Design it as if it is the only session.** The median game sees 10.3% of new players return the next day. The top 1% see 22.2%. ([02](02-the-platform.md))

## The loop

8. **Write the loop with a stopwatch.** Seconds in every row. Dead time under a third of the cycle. ([04](04-core-loop.md))
9. **Twenty rewards in the first five minutes.** Steal a Brainrot's real ladder gives 21. ([04](04-core-loop.md))
10. **Every wait is shortened, given a job, made tense, or moved off-screen.** ([04](04-core-loop.md))
11. **Skill gates come after the player owns something.** A gate keeps between a fifth and a half of the players who meet it. ([04](04-core-loop.md))

## The numbers

12. **Price by payback time.** The first unit pays for itself in 25 seconds; nothing in normal play takes more than 300. ([06](06-progression-and-economy.md), [slop/01](../slop/01-anatomy.md))
13. **After the first quarter of an hour, stock clocks do the pacing, not prices.** A Legendary every 5 minutes, a Mythic every 15. ([slop/04](../slop/04-build-it.md))
14. **Two rolls on everything: what it is, and whether it is upgraded.** About one in six gets a small upgrade; one in a hundred gets a tenfold one. ([07](07-rarity-and-collection.md))
15. **The first reset comes at 20 to 40 minutes and changes what the player can do.** A slot, a tool, a visible change, and a number. ([06](06-progression-and-economy.md))

## Other people

16. **Put wealth in the world.** A stranger should be able to rank three bases from one screenshot. ([slop/02](../slop/02-why-it-holds.md))
17. **Four to eight players per server when players own things side by side.** Bigger for races and shows. ([09](09-other-players.md))
18. **Put something at stake, and a way to protect it that runs out.** A 60-second lock. Then check it against the line. ([slop/02](../slop/02-why-it-holds.md), [15](15-the-line.md))

## Coming back

19. **A 5-minute clock on the screen.** The game with the longest sessions in its family restocks every 5 minutes. ([08](08-retention.md))
20. **It grows while they are away.** Leaving costs nothing; returning pays first and asks later. ([08](08-retention.md))
21. **One live hour a week, same day, same time.** Saturday events put the hits at 5 to 36 times their weekday players. ([13](13-live-ops.md))
22. **Update every week for as long as the game lives.** Of the top 100 games, 58% had published within the last 24 hours and 93% within a week. Every dead clone had stopped. ([13](13-live-ops.md), [data](../data/ANALYSIS.md))
23. **Days 8 to 28 need their own reason.** Roblox's recommendations have looked at 28 days since June 2026. ([02](02-the-platform.md), [08](08-retention.md))

## Money and honesty

24. **Sell little, sell relief, never sell at a loss.** The median top-100 game lists 5 passes and 57 products that can be bought again; 91% have something at 25 R$ or less, and a revive is a 45 R$ product, not a pass. The two biggest trend hits of late 2026 list two passes each. No purchase window opens by itself because the player just lost something. Odds, timers and prices are always true. ([12](12-monetization.md), [15](15-the-line.md))

## The method

25. **Measure the funnel, fix the worst step, change one thing per update.** Then read the number again a week later. ([14](14-metrics-and-diagnosis.md))
