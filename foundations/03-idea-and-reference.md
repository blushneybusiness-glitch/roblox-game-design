# Choosing the idea: a live reference plus one change

Open this page before you build anything, and again whenever you are about to start a second game. It covers how to pick the idea: the fantasy in one sentence, which live game to start from and what to change, which kind of bet you are making, how much time you have, and how to test the idea in a week before you spend a month on it. The output of this page is section 1 and 2 of [kit/templates/one-page-design.md](../kit/templates/one-page-design.md).

## What is known

### The fantasy is one sentence and a list of verbs

Hit games describe themselves in five to seven short lines, each starting with a verb. [Steal a Brainrot](../teardowns/steal-a-brainrot.md)'s whole "how to play" is five verbs: buy, steal, generate money, rebirth, troll (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). [Grow a Garden](../teardowns/grow-a-garden.md) teaches itself in four numbered lines: equip a seed, click a plot, wait, harvest (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).

The people who made the 2025 hits describe the idea the same way: one familiar theme plus one new mechanic.

| Game | Familiar part | New part | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grow a Garden | Gardening | Plants keep growing while you are offline, which its operator called new for Roblox | [GamesBeat interview with Janzen Madsen](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/) |
| [99 Nights in the Forest](../teardowns/99-nights-in-the-forest.md) | The "survive N days" trend, ideas from Dead Rails | A cozy forest; "99" chosen because it sounded better than 100 | [DevForum Creator Spotlight](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940) |
| Steal a Brainrot | An earlier game, *Steal a Character*, whose rights the developer says he bought | Italian-brainrot meme characters on a shared conveyor | [Bloomberg via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/03/25/robloxs-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators) |

Madsen, who runs Splitting Point and says he made 30 to 40 games before Grow a Garden, also says players must be hooked within about the first 30 seconds and that young players do not share genre conventions ([GameAnalytics, a vendor piece](https://gameanalytics.com/blog/splitting-point-how-field-trip-z-got-45m-players/)).

Across the top 300, 18% of titles start with a verb, the median core title is 3 words, and the openings that repeat are "Build a" and "Steal a/an" (4 games each), then "Throw a", "Throw food", "Raise a" and "Fish for" (2 each); "+1" titles are 2% [data, Nov 2026].

The +1 mechanic itself is old. "Every Second You Get +1 Jump" was created in September 2022 and has 54 players today; the 2026 hit added a themed course, 22-player races and a cosmetic shop (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).

Roblox's own wording, from the [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq.md): reach is reduced for "non-unique" games and for visually indistinguishable thumbnails with near-identical titles. Among near-identical games, visibility goes to the one that first added unique metadata or a unique place file, not to the one created first. Separately, the owners of Steal a Brainrot had filed at least four lawsuits against imitators by March 2026. The claims rest on copied layout, menus and art, not on the idea of stealing ([Bloomberg via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/03/25/robloxs-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)). Deconstructor of Fun says copies of games that succeeded off Roblox have "proven all but fruitless" on it ([Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/how-to-build-a-real-business-on-roblox-in-2026)).

A new game's honest target is a place in the top 300, where most games are under a year old.

Players and Robux sit in different genres [data, Nov 2026]. Simulation is 36% of the top 300 games and holds 34% of their players, but only 5 of the 25 top earners on computer. RPG holds 9 of those 25 (all Action RPG) with 3% of top-300 games, and tower defense 3. **Our read:** progression-heavy RPG and tower defense games turn each player into more Robux than the simple sims of this wave. Pick the genre for the bet: players for a wave, earnings per player for an evergreen.

### Rules of thumb from people with a record

| Rule | Owner and record | Source |
|---|---|---|
| About 3 new players per hour sustain about 1 concurrent player | Alexander Hicks, Twin Atlas (World Zero, Creatures of Sonaria) | [Naavik](https://naavik.co/podcast/launching-original-ip-on-roblox/) |
| Under 10K concurrent, much daily traffic is algorithmic, so the game is fragile | Hicks | same |
| About 10 one-month prototypes a year; cut the weak ones | Hicks | same |
| "90th percentile across all those metrics" means a hit | Zach Letter, WonderWorks (SpongeBob TD) | [Deconstructor of Fun](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/how-to-build-a-real-business-on-roblox-in-2026) |
| Market only to seed a launch to a few thousand to about 10K players, then let the algorithm decide | Nathan Clemens, 24 games, over 850M sessions | [Naavik](https://naavik.co/podcast/mastering-the-roblox-meta/) |
| Drop a game when it stops paying for its team | Clemens | same |
| A backlog of about 200 ideas; cycles of about 3 months, up from 1.5 | Clemens | same |
| Pick projects where the studio has an edge (reused tech, reused cosmetics) | Hicks | [Naavik](https://naavik.co/podcast/launching-original-ip-on-roblox/) |
| Do not port mobile games | Madsen | [GameAnalytics](https://gameanalytics.com/blog/splitting-point-how-field-trip-z-got-45m-players/) |

**Our read** on the Hicks rule: concurrent players equal arrivals per hour times hours each player stays. 3 arrivals per hour giving 1 concurrent means each new player stays about 20 minutes. So the rule holds only for a game whose first session lasts about 20 minutes. If yours lasts 7, you need about 9 new players per hour per concurrent player.

## The rules

1. **Write the fantasy as "It's the game where you ___" in eight words or fewer, with one verb.** The hits' store pages are five to seven verb lines. **Our read:** if the sentence needs "and", you have two games.
2. **Start from a game with players today and write down its count and the date.** Use the public endpoint `games.roblox.com/v1/games` and take two same-time snapshots before you trust any tracker, because tracker peaks for one game differ by up to four times (Grow a Garden: 5.6M on RoWatcher, 22.3M in the press).
3. **Change the verb, keep the payoff.** Every follower in the table above that holds 40K or more today changed what the player does. The theme-only copies all peaked under 50K, and most sit under 5,000 now. BIG Games, a top studio, failed with the same verb.
4. **Copy how a system works. Never copy how it looks, what it is called or how its screens are laid out.** That is what the lawsuits claim and what the Discovery FAQ penalises.
5. **Pick your bet before you pick your scope.** See the two bets below.
6. **Wave bet: be live within 14 days of the original's breakout, or drop the wave version.** The working followers came 7 days before to 15 days after. Same-verb copies a month or more late sit in the hundreds.
7. **Playable in 6 weeks or less with 4 people or fewer.** That is the SpongeBob TD figure. 99 Nights took 3 months; Anime Life took 5 and its maker says 2 of those were spare.
8. **Plan for five to seven weeks of small, updated life before anything happens.** Steal An Egg broke out on day 49, Throw Food on day 35 with its first update.
9. **Expect to make several games to land one.** Madsen: 30 to 40 games, 5 or 6 good. Hicks: about 10 prototypes a year.

### Two kinds of bet

| | Wave bet | Evergreen bet |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A neighbour of a game that is breaking out this month | A game meant to hold players for years |
| Evidence | Breakouts on day 35 to 56; falls to under 6% of peak within 2 to 12 months | RIVALS peaked on about day 690 and keeps 17%; Brookhaven, Blox Fruits, Adopt Me, MM2 are 4 to 12 years old and lead the chart |
| Time to ship | 14 days from the breakout | 6 weeks to 3 months to the first public version |
| Team needs | A finished base, ladder, mutation, clock and rebirth module set, so only the new verb is new work ([slop/04-build-it.md](../slop/04-build-it.md)) | A weekly update team for months before the peak; RIVALS launched to 20 players |
| What it lives on | The first session and the first 4 to 10 weeks | Days 8 to 28 in Roblox's 28-day ranking window ([02-the-platform.md](02-the-platform.md)) |
| When to stop | When the wave's original drops below 20% of its peak and your game has no reason to return on its own | When it stops paying for its team (Clemens) |

**Our read:** the bets are both legitimate. The failure is mixing them: a wave game that takes three months, or an evergreen game with nothing to do on day 9. The 2026 hits that held best (Steal An Egg at 852K two months after peak) are wave games that added a weekly reason to return ([13-live-ops.md](13-live-ops.md)).

## How to do it

1. **Fill the promise.** Write the title, the "game where you ___" sentence, one feeling (power, greed, fear, pride, belonging, mischief, mastery, curiosity, calm) and the thumbnail in words. Section 1 of [one-page-design.md](../kit/templates/one-page-design.md).
2. **Find the reference.** Pick a live game. Record its players now, its server size, its creation date and its pass list from the public API.
3. **Find three neighbours.** Search the reference's verb and noun. For each result, record created date, last updated date, players now and what it changed. Fill the "three games that tried something close" line.
4. **Write the change.** One sentence: "Same payoff as X, but the player ___ instead of ___." Check it against rule 3.
5. **Choose the bet** from the table and write its deadline on the page.
6. **Run the four checks below.** Each takes a day or less.
7. **Grey-box the first five minutes** with placeholder parts and the real numbers ([05-first-session.md](05-first-session.md), [04-core-loop.md](04-core-loop.md)).
8. **Watch three new players in silence.** Say nothing, do not touch their screen, write down the second each one stops, frowns or asks a question. 99 Nights' team lists "watch players when they don't know they're being watched" among its lessons ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-spotlight-the-story-behind-99-nights-in-the-forest/4036940)).
9. **Decide** with the kill criteria at the end of this page.

### The four checks before building

| Check | How | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| The title is the instruction | Show only the title to someone who has not heard of the game. Ask what they will do in it | They say your verb within 5 seconds. Title is 2 to 4 words, verb then thing, with no "X999", "FREE", "ADMIN" or borrowed name. Bait titles carry like ratios of 59% to 70% against 85% to 97% for hits ([slop/03-quality-bar.md](../slop/03-quality-bar.md)) |
| The thumbnail comes from the sentence | Draw one scene: one to three characters caught in the middle of the verb | A stranger names the verb from the drawing alone ([11-clips-and-packaging.md](11-clips-and-packaging.md)) |
| The 15-second clip | Record or storyboard 15 seconds of the core verb with the sound off | Someone who has never seen the game can say what happened and what the player got |
| The silent grey box | Steps 7 and 8 above | No player is stuck for more than 10 seconds; all three complete the loop once inside 5 minutes. Roblox's retention guide asks for players to reach the core within about 5 minutes ([retention doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/analytics/retention.md)) |

**Start with** these traffic numbers for your launch plan, from the Hicks rule and your own first-session length:

| Target concurrent | First session 20 min | First session 10 min |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | 300 new players per hour | 600 per hour |
| 1,000 | 3,000 per hour (72,000 a day) | 6,000 per hour |
| 10,000 | 30,000 per hour | 60,000 per hour |

The arithmetic is arrivals per hour equals concurrent divided by hours per stay. Use it to see whether a seed of "a few thousand to 10K players" (Clemens) can reach your target or only test it.

Honest targets from the chart on a Wednesday in Thanksgiving week [data, Nov 2026]: rank 300 by players took 6,429 concurrent, rank 100 took 17,099 and rank 50 took 27,134. With a 20-minute first session that is about 19,300, 51,300 and 81,400 new players per hour (our arithmetic, Hicks rule).

## Where credible people disagree

| Question | One side | Other side | Our read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copy or originate? | Steal a Brainrot came from bought rights to an earlier game; 99 Nights is a named trend plus Dead Rails; BedWars adapted a Minecraft format (third-party source, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/bedwars-in-2026-can-easy-gg-s-kit-machine-outlast-rivals)) | Roblox cuts reach for non-unique games; ports from mobile and other platforms have done badly | The hits copied Roblox-native systems and changed one thing. Nobody in the evidence won by cloning the look |
| Marketing or quality? | Madsen says marketing matters less than quality ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/podcast/mastering-the-roblox-meta/)) | Roblox says the five newest games to reach the top 20 by earnings all started with Sponsored ads ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106)) | Clemens's middle line fits the platform rule that ad-acquired players do not count in recommendations: ads buy a sample, the game keeps it |
| Launch early or polish? | Grow a Garden in days; SpongeBob TD in 6 weeks | Overlook Bay took 7 to 8 months and built 1M favourites first; Clemens moved from 1.5 to 3-month cycles | Early wins when the bet is a wave. Polish wins only with an audience already waiting |

## Checks

- [ ] The fantasy sentence has 8 words or fewer and one verb.
- [ ] The reference game has players today; count and date are written down.
- [ ] Three neighbours are recorded with created date, last update, players now and what they changed.
- [ ] The change is a different verb, and it is written in one sentence.
- [ ] Nothing copies the reference's art, names, characters or screen layout.
- [ ] The bet is named. A wave bet ships within 14 days of the original's breakout; an evergreen bet has a weekly update plan for the first 12 weeks.
- [ ] The first playable build is planned at 6 weeks or less.
- [ ] All four pre-build checks pass.
- [ ] The traffic needed for your target concurrent is written down with the arithmetic.

## Kill criteria

Write these on the one-page design before you start, in section 10 of [one-page-design.md](../kit/templates/one-page-design.md). **Start with:**

| Stage | Stop or rework if | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| Before building | The verb is the same as the reference's | Every theme-only follower in the table peaked under 50K |
| Wave bet, before launch | The original broke out more than 14 days ago | Working followers came 7 days before to 15 days after |
| Grey box | 2 of 3 silent testers fail to finish the loop inside 5 minutes, after two rounds of fixes | Roblox's 5-minute guideline |
| Live, weeks 1 to 2 | D1 below the median for your session length after two rounds of fixes: 7.91% for 7 to 12 minute sessions, 10.24% for 13 to 18 ([GameAnalytics 2025, vendor data](https://www.gameanalytics.com/cn/reports/2025-roblox-report)) | Letter's hits sit at the 90th percentile on every metric; the median is the floor |
| Live, any time | Revenue no longer pays the team | Clemens's rule |

The funnel and benchmarks behind these lines are in [14-metrics-and-diagnosis.md](14-metrics-and-diagnosis.md).

## Mistakes

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Players arrive, then leave within a minute; like ratio under 70% | The title or thumbnail promises something the game does not do | Rename to verb plus thing; show the real verb in the thumbnail |
| Your game sits at a few hundred players next to a hit with the same verb | A reskin: players already have progress in the original | Change the verb, keep the payoff |
| A clone launched on time and still failed | Same verb, even with a big studio's audience (Steal a Pet: 7 players) | Timing does not rescue a reskin; change the verb first |
| The wave is over by launch day | A wave game built on an evergreen schedule | Ship the follower in 14 days from reusable modules, or make it an evergreen bet |
| Strong first week, nothing by week 4 | A first hour with no day-9 reason | Add the weekly reason before launch ([08-retention.md](08-retention.md), [13-live-ops.md](13-live-ops.md)) |
| A sequel to your own faded hit has almost no players | The audience moved on from the theme (Grow a Garden 2: 1,056 against the original's 18K) | Make a neighbour with a new verb, not a "2" |
| Months of building, then a launch to almost nobody, then no plan | No kill criteria written down | Fill section 10 of the one-page design before building |
| Takedown notice or legal letter | Copied art, layout or characters, including memes someone else made | Use characters you made or licensed ([slop/05-after-the-peak.md](../slop/05-after-the-peak.md)) |
