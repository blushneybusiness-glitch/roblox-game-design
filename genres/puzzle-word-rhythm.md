# Puzzle, word and rhythm

> **The feeling sold:** "I'm the fastest speller (or drawer, or player) in this server, and everyone just saw it."

This page covers word games, trivia, draw-and-guess, puzzle rounds and rhythm games. The evidence is thin. Our research has server sizes and player counts for about a dozen games, a full song shop and rank table for one rhythm game, and almost nothing on timers, phone input or dictionaries. Every gap is marked "not known". Do not treat this page as a recipe; treat it as a map of what to measure.

Player counts marked "now" are from the Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026, a weekday.

## Why people play it

No source we found says. **Our read:** these are short skill contests where the result is a visible score in front of a small group, and word and drawing games need no fast reflexes, which may suit players on any device. Age and device split: not known.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Leader with the most documentation: RoBeats ([RoBeats wiki](https://robeats.fandom.com/wiki/RoBeats!)).

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Pick a song | Chooses from owned songs; solo, or 2 to 4 players in Compete (gears count) or Casual (gears off) | not known | Song list by difficulty | none |
| 2. Play | Hits notes in 4 lanes; holds long notes and releases them on time | Song length: not known | A vertical scroller; Fever boosts score and drops on a miss | Accuracy, Fever |
| 3. Grade | Reads the result | not known | A rank from C+ to S by accuracy | Rank, coins (amount not known) |
| 4. Shop | Buys new songs from the daily shop | Shop refreshes daily at 12:00 AM PST | 4 price tiers by difficulty | Coins spent, collection points |

How the others differ:

- **Funky Friday**: a 1v1 duel. 4 arrows (default W, A, S, D), each player plays only their side, higher score wins ([wiki](https://funky-friday.fandom.com/wiki/Funky_friday_Wiki)).
- **Word Bomb**: public servers run Default Mode, the easiest, with 3 bomb types (Normal Black, Red, Skull); other modes need a private server and a /mode command ([wiki: Default Mode](https://word-bomb.fandom.com/wiki/Default_Mode)). Turn timer and lives: not known.
- **Word Battle, Hyper Draw, Spelling Race, Answer or Die**: loop not documented in our research.

The loop stack: not known beyond RoBeats' daily shop at 12:00 AM PST and weekend song pack.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100 players, no update in years | Stale question set | Longest Answer Wins: 47 players, last update June 2023. **Our read:** a content pipeline before launch |
| An early leader falls behind new entrants | Format copied with fresher content or better placement | Word Bomb (2018) 65 against Word Battle (2026) 4,715 |
| Songs disappear or change name | Music rights | Funky Friday renames |
| Old rhythm game small despite updates | not known | RoBeats: 262 players, updated 24 Nov 2026 |
| Public servers show only the easy mode | Harder modes locked to private servers | Word Bomb. **Our read:** new players never see the other modes; effect not measured |

## Checklist

1. The player is in a round within 30 seconds of joining (our gate; no source).
2. Server size is 12 for word or draw games unless you have a reason for more.
3. A rhythm game uses 4 lanes.
4. Grades are shown as accuracy bands, with the top band near 97.5%.
5. Something new can be bought every day at a fixed time.
6. Every licensed song has a plan for removal or rename.
7. Content (words, questions, songs) gets an update at least monthly. **Our read:** the oldest-updated games here hold under 70 players.
8. You have timed a leader's turn and round with a stopwatch, because no source gives those numbers.

## Twists and open space

Already working: Whisper Draw (80 players, 1,055 now) runs drawing in a large server; Hyper Draw holds 10,905 with 12. Funky Friday turned rhythm into a 1v1 duel.

**Our read** on open space: our research shows no word or trivia game above 5,000 players, and no documented phone-first input design. A word game built for a phone keyboard, with turn timers tested on phones, has nothing in our research to compete against. That is an absence of evidence, not proof of demand.
