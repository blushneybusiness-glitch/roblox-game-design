# Hangout

> **The feeling sold:** "I hang out, try on anything for free, talk to people, and they notice me."

This page covers four kinds of game with no goal beyond being there and being seen: voice and vibe spaces (MIC UP, TTD 3), rating hangouts (Rate My Avatar 2), avatar-shopping and try-on games (Catalog Avatar Creator, My Avatar!), and donation or social-economy games (PLS DONATE, PLS WAIT). Player counts marked "now" are from Roblox's public API on 25 Nov 2026.

Two gaps up front. **No retention evidence was found for any hangout**: no D1, no session length, no developer statement on what keeps people in a game with no goal. **No first-minute flow was found** for any of these games. What follows is built from store descriptions, price lists, platform rules and tracker numbers.

## Why people play it

The store pages say what is offered. Catalog Avatar Creator: try on accessories, hats, limiteds, hair, bundles and animation packs for free, browse more than 50M community outfits, save avatars ([Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/7041939546/Catalog-Avatar-Creator)). MIC UP: a classic hangout for spatial voice on every device from phone to VR (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). TTD 3: more than 1,100 emotes from games and social trends, available on joining ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ttd-3-information-hub/1513129)). PLS DONATE: claim a booth, write a sign, receive and give Robux ([Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/8737602449/PLS-DONATE)).

Who plays is set by rules more than by design. Social hangouts were closed to under-13s in an announcement of 7 Nov 2024 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/07/roblox-adds-new-safety-measures-to-ban-kids-under-13-from-social-spaces)), and voice is for age-checked users of 13 and over ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/voice-chat-is-now-supported-in-servers-of-up-to-100-users/4534031)). Device split is not known.

**Our read:** nearly every product sold here makes the buyer more visible (signs, booth skins, nametags, glowbands, featured booths, ads). The hangout sells attention.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Leader: Catalog Avatar Creator. The step order comes from its store page; no source times any step.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Browse | Searches the catalog or community outfits | not known | Item thumbnails, 50M+ outfits | none |
| Try on | Clicks an item | not known | The item on their own avatar, free | none |
| Save | Saves the look | not known | The outfit in a saved list | Saved outfits |
| Buy | Buys an item through the in-game prompt | not known | Roblox purchase prompt; the item lands in their Roblox inventory | The owner's 40% share, paid after 30 days |

Sources: [Roblox page](https://www.roblox.com/games/7041939546/Catalog-Avatar-Creator), [Roblox: Marketplace fees](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions).

How the others differ:
- **PLS DONATE:** claim a booth, edit its text, wait. Other players click a green button with a Robux amount on your booth to donate ([wiki: Booth](https://please-donate.fandom.com/wiki/Booth)). A search summary says the money moves through game passes and clothing the booth owner has on sale; no official source confirms it.
- **Rate My Avatar** (the original): rent a booth, set its description and image, receive star ratings; a Top Rated board per server; a lounge door only players rated 4 or more may pass ([GamerTweak](https://gamertweak.com/rate-my-avatar-roblox/)).
- **MIC UP:** join a server of up to 100 and talk. Nothing else is described.

Loop stack: what closes at each time scale is mostly not known. The known clocks are paid ones: Rate My Avatar 2's Featured Booth lasts 10 minutes; PLS DONATE's ads run 24 hours, 7 days or 1 month, and its VIP 30 days; the donation limits reset daily and monthly.

Creators on the DevForum have argued that 40% to the experience is too high next to 30% to the maker ([thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-merch-booth-developer-module/1797914), via a search summary).

## What brings them back

No retention evidence was found for any hangout. What the games offer:

**Later in the same session.** TTD 3: levels and tokens (x3 Levels pass 250 R$). Rate My Avatar: a Top Rated board and a 4+ lounge to qualify for. My Avatar!: points from in-game purchases, spent in a store of more than 100 free accessories (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).

**Tomorrow.** Not known. No daily rewards appear in our research for any hangout.

**Next week and after.** TTD 3 has more than 30 limited-time items now off sale (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). PLS DONATE added gift boxes and booth cosmetics in late March 2026 and has released no promo codes since Jan 2025 ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/3317679266)). Catalog Avatar Creator ran an Adidas UGC store and an animation contest that drew over 2M votes in a week ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/2711375305)). Rate My Avatar 2 lists seasonal themes and badges.

## Other players

Other players are the whole game. Platform rules, by date:

| Date | Rule |
|---|---|
| 7 Nov 2024 | Social hangouts, games whose main purpose is chatting, closed to under-13s ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/07/roblox-adds-new-safety-measures-to-ban-kids-under-13-from-social-spaces)) |
| 15 Aug 2025 | Hangouts set in private spaces (bedrooms, bathrooms) or adult settings (bars, clubs) need ID-verified 17+ ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/08/extending-roblox-policy-on-romantic-and-sexual-content)) |
| 9 Jan 2026 | Age check by face estimate or ID required to chat. Groups: under 9, 9 to 12, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, 18 to 20, 21+. Chat within your group and the next one; under 9 needs parental consent ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/age-check-requirement-to-chat-now-live-globally/4226101), [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/11/roblox-requires-age-checks-limits-minor-and-adult-chat)) |
| 24 Mar 2026 | Voice servers up to 100 players, from 50 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/voice-chat-is-now-supported-in-servers-of-up-to-100-users/4534031)) |
| 7 May 2026 | Voice on by default for age-checked 13+ users in new experiences up to 100 players; voice only within your own age group; 200 named as the next target (same post) |

**Our read:** in a 100-player voice server, users only hear their own age group, so the server can split into groups that cannot talk to each other. Match players by age group when filling voice servers. A round game such as [Dress to Impress](../teardowns/dress-to-impress.md) is not a social hangout under the definition above; a pure voice lounge is.

What players see of each other: booths and signs (PLS DONATE, Rate My Avatar), nametags and glowbands (TTD 3), star ratings and a Top Rated board. What they can do to each other: donate, rate, comment (Rate My Avatar 2 sells Leave a Comment at 35 R$ and Stronger Avatar Votes at 99). Nothing can be lost except Robux given away. PLS DONATE keeps top-donation stats private behind a chat command ([wiki: Features](https://please-donate.fandom.com/wiki/Features_of_PLS_DONATE)). See [foundations/09-other-players.md](../foundations/09-other-players.md).

## Live ops

Update rhythm is not known for any hangout. Last updates: MIC UP 8 Aug 2026, PLS DONATE 16 Aug 2026 (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026), so 109 and 101 days without an update by 25 Nov (our arithmetic). My Avatar!'s Update 8 added points bought with Robux and voice servers ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/7554119808)). Catalog Avatar Creator's events are brand stores and contests.

When updates stop: PLS DONATE is at 2,239 against 50K in Dec 2025, 96% down (our arithmetic). The cause is not documented.

**Our read:** a small team's live ops here can be content Roblox's catalog supplies for free (new items to try on) plus one visibility product per event (a booth skin, a nametag, a sign).

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Under-13s cannot join or chat | The game counts as a social hangout (rule of Nov 2024); chat needs an age check (Jan 2026) | Give the space an activity, such as rounds, so chatting is not its main purpose |
| Experience restricted to ID-verified 17+ | A bedroom, bathroom, bar or club setting (Aug 2025 rule) | Public settings only |
| Removed from Roblox | Moderation (the original Rate My Avatar, reason not given) | Filter booth text and images; PLS DONATE filters booth text |
| 96% below peak, no update for 3 months | Nothing new to show or buy (PLS DONATE, 50K to 2,239; no codes since Jan 2025) | A visibility drop each month |
| 86% to 88% below peak with almost nothing for sale | A voice room with no goal (MIC UP, 14K to 17K down to 2,011) | Add a reason to stay: levels, ratings, booths |
| Silent pockets in a full voice server | Age groups cannot hear each other | Fill servers by age group |
| Donations stay tiny | Plus requirement and the 500/1,000 R$ limits | Earn from booths and ads around donations, as PLS DONATE does |

## Checklist

- [ ] A new player makes a visible change to their avatar or space within 30 s, with one click.
- [ ] Try-on is free, and every avatar item that can be enabled is enabled for the 40% share.
- [ ] Servers hold 24 to 28 for booth and try-on games, up to 100 for voice.
- [ ] Voice servers are filled by age group.
- [ ] The main purpose is an activity, unless the game is meant to be a 13+ social hangout.
- [ ] No bedroom, bathroom, bar or club setting unless the game is ID-verified 17+.
- [ ] Booth text and images are filtered.
- [ ] Something rates, counts or ranks how others see the player (stars, a Top Rated board, a level).
- [ ] At least one owned space is for sale (a booth builder at about 899).
- [ ] Timed visibility is for sale at a shown price (100 R$ for 10 minutes).
- [ ] Larger currency packs never give a worse rate than smaller ones.
- [ ] Donation features state Roblox's Plus requirement and limits on screen.
- [ ] A visible new item ships at least monthly.

## Twists and open space

Variations already tried:
- **Points back on purchases.** My Avatar! pays points for buying items in-game and 1,000 points for joining Roblox Plus there, spent on more than 100 free accessories. It peaked at 31K in Oct 2026.
- **Try-on plus a contest.** Catalog Avatar Runway and Slay or Nay add a theme, a clock and a runway to try-on, and sell the items worn. See [dress-up.md](dress-up.md).
- **Booths plus ratings.** Rate My Avatar: rentable booths, star ratings and a lounge gated at 4 stars.
- **Create and sell.** Starving Artists was not found in any source. The closest data point is Paint to Get Rich (created 20 Aug 2026, 6 per server, 3,832 now, RoWatcher peak 47K in Oct 2026), which sells cash multipliers from 3 to 199 R$ and painting crates from 24 to 899 R$ ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10744310825)).

**Our read:** open space.
- A try-on game with a reason to come back tomorrow: none of the try-on games in these notes shows a daily reward.
- A donation game where giving earns a visible rank that survives the 1,000 R$ monthly cap.
- A voice space with a light activity every few minutes, which keeps it out of the "chatting is the main purpose" category and gives quiet players something to do.
