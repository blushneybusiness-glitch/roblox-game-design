# Tower defense

> **The feeling sold:** "I put my towers in the right spots and nothing got through. Next time I bring a stronger one."

Two branches share one match: classic tower defense, where towers unlock with coins and levels, and anime gacha tower defense, where units come from summon banners. Card-collection and strategy games close the page. Player counts are from 25 November 2026 unless dated.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Core verb | Place a tower on the path, then upgrade it between waves |
| Study these first | [Tower Defense Simulator](../teardowns/tower-defense-simulator.md): created 2019, still 12K, coin-and-level unlocks. [Anime Vanguards](../teardowns/anime-vanguards.md): the gacha stack, fully published. Toilet Tower Defense: the largest trading economy. Garden Tower Defense: 77 versions in about 528 days |
| Session that works | Match games 9 to 14 minutes; gacha progression games 19 to 44 minutes (Rolimons average playtime, 25 Nov 2026) |
| Players per server | Lobbies of 20 to 50 (TDS 50, ASTD 38, TTD, Anime Adventures and Anime Crusaders X 30, Anime Vanguards 24, Garden TD 20); matches of 1 to 4, up to 6 in Anime Vanguards Rifts (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026) |
| Who owns the genre | Open. The 2024 anime leaders are near zero (Anime Defenders: 377,836 peak, 188 now). Cameraman TD, created 26 Aug 2026, peaked at 133,264 within about 12 weeks. The classic leaders, created 2019 and 2023, hold 12K to 38K |
| Build cost | Not known. **Our read:** content is the cost: TDS has 10 modes plus events, Anime Vanguards 12 story stages of 6 acts, and leaders add units every week |
| Money comes from | VIP (299 to 375 R$), luck and shiny passes (99 to 1,299 R$), speed passes (389 to 799 R$); in classic TD, early-unlock tower passes (225 to 2,249 R$) |
| What kills clones | Stopping updates or borrowing someone else's characters: Anime Defenders had no numbered update after Dec 2024 and fell from 377,836 to 188 |

## Why people play it

The sources give no age or device split. They do show two session shapes that both reach 100K: short matches (TDS 12.96 minutes, ASTD 9.52, Cameraman TD 10.54) and long progression (Anime Vanguards 44.22, Anime Crusaders X 38.82, Anime Adventures 19.41) ([Rolimons TDS](https://www.rolimons.com/game/3260590327), [Rolimons AV](https://www.rolimons.com/game/16146832113), [Rolimons ACX](https://www.rolimons.com/game/107573139811370)).

**Our read:** the classic player comes to beat a mode with a plan; the gacha player comes to pull a unit and see it on the map.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Leader: Tower Defense Simulator, Easy mode, the mode the wiki gives new players ([TDS wiki Easy Mode](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Easy_Mode)).

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Join a match | Walks into a lobby elevator | not known | Lobby of up to 50 | none |
| First towers | Places two Scouts ($125 each) from $500 starting cash | 0 to 20 | Tower drops on the path | Cash 500 to 250 |
| A wave | Watches towers fire | about 21 on average (timer 1:00, skip after 0:20) | Cash pops per kill | Wave bonus 180 at wave 1 solo |
| Upgrade | Buys a tower level | a few | Tower model changes | Scout DPS 1 to 24.6 over 4 upgrades |
| Answer a new enemy | Adds a hidden-detection tower before wave 8 | between waves | Hidden enemies walk past blind towers | Lives lost if wrong |
| Boss and result | Kills the Brute on wave 20 | about 7:00 total | Triumph screen | 300 coins, 50 EXP, 25 tower EXP |
| Spend | Buys a tower in the shop | 30 to 60 | Tower in loadout | Paintballer 100, Demoman 200, Boomerang 300 coins |

Sources: [Scout](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Scout), [Towers](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Towers). Our arithmetic: 7:00 for 20 waves is 21 seconds a wave, so players skip almost every wave as soon as the button appears at 0:20.

**How the anime games differ.** Same match; the spend step is a summon at 50 gems a pull from a banner that changes every hour ([AV wiki Summon](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Summon)). A stage pays gems (80 per act on Anime Vanguards' first story world). A second loop sits on top: rerolling traits on owned units.

**The loop stack:**

| Every | What finishes | Example |
|---|---|---|
| About 20 seconds | A wave, a wave bonus, an upgrade | TDS Easy |
| Every 2 waves | A new enemy type | TDS Easy: hidden at 8, armored at 13 |
| 7 to 15 minutes | A match and its reward | TDS Easy 7:00; TTD Easy 7 to 15 minutes |
| 10 minutes idle | One pull's worth of gems | Anime Vanguards AFK room, 20 gems per 4 minutes |
| 30 minutes / 1 hour | A personal challenge / the banner | Anime Vanguards |
| 1 day | 20 challenge clears used up; raids refill every 6 hours | Anime Vanguards; TTD 3 runs per 6 hours |
| 1 week | Boss rotation, leaderboard reset, update | Anime Vanguards bosses; Garden TD leaderboard and Saturday update |

Sources: [AV wiki Gamemodes](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Gamemodes), [Time Chamber](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Chamber), [TTD wiki Raids](https://toilet-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Raids), [GTD wiki Update Logs](https://garden-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Logs).

## The first five minutes

First-session flows were not found for any tower defense game: no tutorial record, first-clear time or first-unit timing. Known: TDS gives a new player Scout and Sniper at level 0 and points to Easy; TTD's start is Easy, which one Basic Speakerman can clear solo; Anime Vanguards starts players in Story, where a first act clear pays 100 gems ([TDS wiki Towers](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Towers), [TTD wiki Easy Mode](https://toilet-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Easy_Mode), [AV wiki Story](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Story)). **What a pity costs** (our arithmetic; Anime Vanguards' AFK room pays 300 gems an hour, 600 with the 299 R$ VIP pass, [AV wiki Time Chamber](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Chamber)):

| Target | Pulls | Gems | Gems with VIP | AFK hours | AFK hours with VIP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| One pull | 1 | 50 | 40 | 0.17 (10 min) | 0.07 (4 min) |
| Legendary pity | 50 | 2,500 | 2,000 | 8.3 | 3.3 |
| Mythic pity | 400 | 20,000 | 16,000 | 66.7 (2.8 days) | 26.7 |
| Vanguard pity, once | 25,000 | 1,250,000 | 1,000,000 | 4,167 (174 days) | 1,667 (69 days) |

At 80 gems an act, Mythic pity is 250 clears.

**Our read on where pity sits:** at 4%, the chance of no Legendary in 49 pulls is 0.96^49, about 13.5%. At 0.5%, the chance of no Mythic in 399 pulls is 0.995^399, also about 13.5%. Both counters sit at twice the expected pulls, so about one player in seven reaches them. The banner's centre unit is 50% of 0.5%, so it averages 400 pulls, the same as Mythic pity. Whether pity gives the featured unit is not known.

**Upgrade curve.** TDS Scout: place $125, upgrades $50, $375, $1,350, $2,200 (total $4,100); cost per DPS rises from $125 to $166.5 ([TDS wiki Scout](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Scout)).

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** Anime Vanguards challenges refresh 30 minutes after a clear, capped at 20 a day, doubled on weekends ([AV wiki Gamemodes](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Gamemodes)). The banner changes every hour. Every TDS triumph adds a guaranteed item: consumable 50%, ticket 30%, crate or support item 20%.

**Tomorrow.** Anime Vanguards daily rewards: 1,000 gems on day 1, 500 plus 5 rerolls on day 2, 1,000 on day 5, 2,500 on day 6, weekly reset ([AV wiki Daily Rewards](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Rewards)). Our arithmetic: 5,000 gems, 100 pulls, a quarter of Mythic pity. TTD raids refill 3 runs every 6 hours. Garden TD's Zen Garden pays 10% while offline ([GTD wiki Update Logs](https://garden-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Logs)).

**Next week and after.** Garden TD's weekly Endless board pays a Glass unit to the top 50 and Gold, Silver and Bronze units to the top 3 per map, and bans 5 godly and 9 exclusive units at random each week ([GTD wiki Endless Mode](https://garden-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Endless_Mode)). Anime Vanguards rotates 4 weekly bosses. TDS gates modes at levels 5, 15, 30, 50, 60 and 250 (third-party table, [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/tower-defense-simulator/modes)); by the [level formula](https://tds.fandom.com/wiki/Levels), level 5 is about 6 Easy wins (our arithmetic).

## Other players

Lobbies of 20 to 50, matches of 1 to 4 (6 in Rifts). **Our read:** the lobby shows off units; the small match keeps each player's towers on screen. Co-op is paid but capped: TTD adds 1.2x, 1.4x and 1.5x coins for 1, 2 and 3 friends ([TTD wiki Difficulties](https://toilet-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Difficulties)); TDS cuts starting cash and splits kill cash.

**Trading.** Every game that opened trading met scams or duplicated units.

| Garden TD (28 Jun 2025) | 25 victories | Units; passes can be gifted | not known; Trading Plaza stalls cost 2,500 to 250,000 Seeds or 25 to 649 R$ |

Sources: [AV wiki Trading](https://animevanguards.fandom.com/wiki/Trading), [TTD wiki Trading](https://toilet-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Trading), [TTD Duped units](https://toilet-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Duped_units), [GTD Trading Plaza](https://garden-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Trading_Plaza), [AD Updates](https://anime-defenders.fandom.com/wiki/Updates).

## Live ops

Garden TD went from v1.0.0 (4 Jun 2025) to v1.77.0 (14 Nov 2026): 77 versions in about 528 days, one every 6.9 days, on Saturdays in Oct and Nov 2026. Each adds 2 limited-stock units with codes plus a mode or system ([GTD wiki Update Logs](https://garden-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Logs)). Its peak, 197,872, fell on 6 Sep 2025, the day v1.13.0 added the Astral Summon ([Rolimons GTD](https://www.rolimons.com/game/108533757090220)).

Updates make spikes: 30-day peaks were 2 to 5 times the 25 Nov count (TDS 65,994 against 12,114, TTD 81,831 against 38,315, Garden TD 26,478 against 7,104). What a small team can sustain: not known. **Our read:** Garden TD's 2 units a week plus one system is the smallest cadence a leader has shown. See [foundations/13-live-ops.md](../foundations/13-live-ops.md).

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Players fall over 99% within a year | Updates stopped. Anime Defenders: 377,836 (21 Jul 2024) to 188, no numbered update after 18 Dec 2024. No source states the cause | Keep the weekly unit drop first |
| Sudden near-total collapse | Not known. Anime Last Stand: 157,103 (23 Oct 2025) to 27. Takedown, move or data error: none confirmed | Own your characters; keep one place |
| Content delayed, units redrawn | Borrowed characters. Anime Vanguards: a DMCA impact delayed Update 9.0 and 12 of 14 planned units needed rework; the game now carries the title "Vanguards: Embers of Justice" | Original units from day one |
| Duplicated units in trades | Trading before items were safe. TTD: about 50 duped unit types listed | Gate trading; trade only items no longer earnable |
| Peak in weeks, then a slide | A themed wave. Cameraman TD: 133,264 about a week before 25 Nov, 26,413 on 25 Nov | Ship updates two and three before the peak passes |
| Veterans stop spending | No sink after the roster is complete | Rerolls with their own pity (AV Monarch: 1,500) |

The survivors: TDS (2019) holds 12,114, 7.6% of its 159,824 peak; TTD (2023) holds 38,315, 7.3% of its 522,376 peak (Rolimons; Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). Both still ship.

## Checklist

1. The intro mode is 20 waves or fewer and 7 to 10 minutes long.
2. Starting cash buys at least 3 basic towers.
3. A new enemy type appears at least every 3 waves, and one forces a second tower by wave 8.
4. The first win buys at least one unit or two pulls.
5. Each extra player adds less than 1x a solo player's cash to the team.
6. Each tower upgrade costs more per point of damage than the last.
7. Gacha: Legendary and Mythic pity at about 2 times expected pulls, with a counter on screen.
8. Gacha: idle income pays at least one pull per hour.
9. Something resets every 30 minutes, every day and every week.
10. A weekly leaderboard pays named items to a fixed top N.
11. Trading locked until level 50 or 25 victories, or limited to items no longer earnable.
12. Every unit is an original character.
13. An update ships at least every 14 days with at least 2 units.
14. Every paid item that changes odds is checked against [foundations/15-the-line.md](../foundations/15-the-line.md).

## Twists and open space

**Twists that already produced hits.**

- **A meme series.** TTD tied "Episode N" updates to a video series and peaked at 522,376 (9 Mar 2024). Cameraman TD (created 26 Aug 2026; release update 19 Sep 2026 per its wiki) copied TTD's ladder, 52 units over 9 rarities with hourly crates, and peaked at 133,264 ([Units](https://cameraman-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Units), [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/132929771812494)).
- **A theme from another genre's hit.** Garden Tower Defense (created 14 May 2025) peaked at 197,872 and holds 7,104 on weekly versions.
- **Long gacha progression.** Anime Crusaders X (created 7 May 2025) set its all-time high, about 110,000, on 25 Nov 2026, with 38.82-minute sessions ([Rolimons ACX](https://www.rolimons.com/game/107573139811370)). Banners, prices and stages: not found.

**Card-collection games.** These are roll games. In Anime Card Clash each card has base odds "1 in N" and four tiers, each 100 times rarer and 4 times stronger ([ACC wiki All Cards](https://anime-card-clash.fandom.com/wiki/All_Cards)). The wiki has 25 articles and one editor and may contain errors.

| Tier (1-in-2 card) | Odds | HP | DMG |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 1 in 2 | 14 | 7 |
| Gold | 1 in 200 | 56 | 28 |
| Rainbow | 1 in 20,000 | 224 | 112 |
| Secret | 1 in 2,000,000 | 896 | 448 |

Gold and Rainbow DMG are our arithmetic from the x4 rule. A daily quest asks for 250 rolls; our arithmetic: the Secret version of the commonest card averages 2,000,000 rolls, 8,000 days of that quest. Anime Card Clash peaked at 40,572 (22 Nov 2025) and has about 6,000; Anime Card Collection (created 15 Dec 2025, 6 per server) peaked at 50,507 six weeks later and has about 1,875 ([Rolimons ACC](https://www.rolimons.com/game/110829983956014), [ACCollection](https://www.rolimons.com/game/76285745979410)). Sessions: 17 to 31 minutes. Pack prices, set sizes and trading rules: not found.

**Strategy games.** Conquer The World WW2 (created 2023, 14 per server) peaked at 30,813 on 4 Oct 2026 and has about 10,100, with 14.58-minute sessions ([Rolimons CTW](https://www.rolimons.com/game/14422394494)). Roll Army to Fight (created 24 Aug 2026) had 5,246 players on only 1.28M visits (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). Match length and economy: not found.

**Open space.** All **Our read:**

- An original-cast gacha TD. The branch's one documented DMCA delay came from recognisable characters, and the two biggest collapses have no stated cause that rules this out.
- Card tiers on TD units: a fixed roster with Gold, Rainbow and Secret versions at 100x odds and 4x stats, so the set stays small while the chase grows.
