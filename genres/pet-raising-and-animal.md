# Pet raising and animal games

> **The feeling sold:** "I hatch a baby pet, look after it until it grows up, and swap pets with my friends until I have the rare glowing one."

Two related shapes. Pet-raising social games, led by [Adopt Me!](../teardowns/adopt-me.md), where you care for pets in a town and trade them. Animal and creature games (Dragon Adventures, Creatures of Sonaria, Horse Life, Wild Horse Islands, Warrior Cats: Ultimate Edition), where you play as or ride the animal, grow it and breed it. Pet collection inside a simulator, where pets are power, is on [simulator.md](simulator.md).

## Why people play it

Adopt Me began in 2017 as a game about adopting children and turned to pets in 2019; monthly players went from 30M to 60M between January 2020 and January 2021, a figure RoWatcher attributes to Roblox ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/roleplay-games-rule-roblox-adopt-me-and-brookhaven-command-900k-players)). The audience age split for pet games was not found.

**Our read:** the player gets two things. A pet that needs them, every few minutes, with a visible growth stage. And a collection that other people want, which turns every pet into something to show and trade. Adopt Me's tasks mix both: three of its four daily tasks are care, the bonus task needs a friend.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Leader: [Adopt Me!](../teardowns/adopt-me.md). Seconds per need were not timed in any source.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. A need appears | Reads the need over the pet (Hungry, Sleepy, Dirty, School) | not known | The need icon | none |
| 2. Fill it | Goes to the place or item the need names | not known | The pet eats, sleeps or bathes | none |
| 3. Paid | Need completes | not known | Bucks added | +10 to +20 Bucks ([wiki: Bucks](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Bucks)) |
| 4. Grows | Ageing progress fills | not known | Stage name changes (Newborn to Full Grown, six stages) | +1 task toward the next stage ([wiki: Pets](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Pets)) |
| 5. Spend | Buys an egg at a stall | not known | Egg hatches into a pet | -350 to -1,450 Bucks |
| 6. Combine | Takes 4 Full Grown copies to the Neon Cave | not known | 1 Neon pet | 4 pets become 1 ([wiki: Neon Pets](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Neon_Pets)) |

The loop stops paying at 300 Bucks per in-game day (500 with a paid certificate) ([wiki: Bucks](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Bucks)). The length of an in-game day was not found.

**How the creature games differ.** In Dragon Adventures the player is the rider and the pet grows on a clock: age 1 to 100 over four stages, a timer per species that runs even when the dragon is put away ([wiki: Age](https://dragon-adventures.fandom.com/wiki/Age)). The loop is breed, wait, raise, breed again. In Creatures of Sonaria the player is the creature and earns 5 Shooms every 2 minutes alive ([wiki: Game Currencies](https://creatures-of-sonaria-official.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Currencies)).

### Eggs: price, odds, days of cap

| Egg | Price | Legendary | Ultra-Rare | Days of the 300 cap | Bucks per expected Legendary (our arithmetic) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | free | 0% | 0% | 0 | | [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/adopt-me/eggs) |
| Cracked | 350 | 1.5% | 6% | 1.2 | 23,333 | [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/adopt-me-every-egg-on-sale-right-now-with-prices-and-odds/) |
| Pet | 600 | 3% | 15% | 2.0 | 20,000 | same |
| Fairytale | 750, or 99 R$ for 4 | 3% | 12% | 2.5 | 25,000 | same; [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/adopt-me/eggs) |
| Royal | 1,450 | 8% | 30% | 4.8 | 18,125 | [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/adopt-me/eggs) |

Snapshot dated 22 to 26 Oct 2026. The two sources list odds in opposite column orders; the Pet Egg's 3% Legendary matches in both.

Our arithmetic on pacing: price rises 1.7 times from Cracked to Pet and 2.4 times from Pet to Royal, while Legendary odds rise 2 and 2.7 times. Dearer eggs are slightly better value per Legendary. At the 300 cap alone, one expected Legendary from Pet Eggs takes about 67 in-game days. Through Robux, 99 R$ buys 4 Fairytale Eggs, about 825 R$ per expected Legendary.

**Our read:** the cap is what keeps eggs worth wanting. Without it, a player who grinds needs for three hours could buy a week of eggs, and every pet's trade value would fall.

### Breeding in the creature games

Dragon Adventures ([wiki: Breeding](https://dragon-adventures.fandom.com/wiki/Breeding)):

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Who can breed | One male and one female, both Adult or older, a free Lair slot |
| Result | One baby; 33% chance of a non-basic element; at most 4 of 5 mutations |
| Cooldown | 7 min 26 s for the starter species at level 1 (4 min 30 s at level 50) up to 2 h 06 min for the slowest species |
| Fee (coins) | Common 125, Uncommon 350, Rare 750, Epic 5,000, Legendary 12,500, Relic 500,000; doubled for event species, whose female parent becomes untradable |

Our arithmetic: fees step up 2.1 to 6.7 times per rarity, then 40 times to Relic. Levelling the starter species from 1 to 50 cuts its cooldown by 39%.

Creatures of Sonaria ([wiki: Mutations](https://creatures-of-sonaria-official.fandom.com/wiki/Mutations), [wiki: Game Currencies](https://creatures-of-sonaria-official.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Currencies)): mutations come from hatching in another player's nest or at growth milestones, up to 3 per creature, none if tokens were used to grow; keeping them after death costs 50 Shooms (20 minutes of passive pay, our arithmetic). Odds are not published. Wild Horse Islands reportedly gives both players identical foals when they co-breed, for a fee (low-quality source, unverified: [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/5-best-breeds-roblox-wild-horse-islands)). ## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** The next need, every task. The Task Board's need-count tasks (complete 5, 10 or 15 needs on a pet of a set rarity) ([wiki: Task Board](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/Task_Board)). In Dragon Adventures, a breeding cooldown of 7 minutes to 2 hours.

**Tomorrow.** Adopt Me's cap resets each in-game day; 4 new Task Board tasks; the 5-day login cycle and 1 Star per login toward Star Rewards eggs (the Gemstone Egg is a 28-star goal) ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/adopt-me-every-egg-on-sale-right-now-with-prices-and-odds/)). The Pet Pen (September 2025) lets four pets age while the player is offline ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/adopt-me-in-2026-sugarfest-500k-players-and-what-s-next)). Dragon growth timers run while the dragon is put away ([wiki: Age](https://dragon-adventures.fandom.com/wiki/Age)). Sonaria pays 150 Shooms an hour alive, so players leave long-lived creatures idle to farm ([wiki](https://creatures-of-sonaria-official.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Currencies)).

**Next week and after.** Weekly event chapters, a four-week Halloween with a limited dragon, a Homepass seasonal battle pass, and "Admin Abuse" gift events on 5 Sep and 14 Nov 2026 ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/adopt-me-in-2026-sugarfest-500k-players-and-what-s-next), [wiki: XP Potion](https://adoptme.fandom.com/wiki/XP_Potion)). Retention rates: not known.

## Other players

**Server size.** 20 to 35 in pet and dragon games; 60 to 75 in games where you play as the animal in a pack (Warrior Cats UE, Sonaria).

**What a player sees.** Other players' pets following them, neon pets glowing. **Our read:** a Mega Neon is 16 pets' work visible from across the map; that is the status object.

**What they can do with each other.** Trade (above), visit homes (a Task Board task asks for 3 or 5), host parties. In Sonaria, hatch in another player's nest for a mutation chance. In Wild Horse Islands, co-breed.

**What can be lost and how it is protected.** Pets in a bad trade. Adopt Me's answer is the licence test, the 5-second restart timer, the unfair-trade warning, 30 days of history and a report button. In Sonaria, mutations on death unless 50 Shooms are paid.

**The clip.** Not measured. **Our read:** a hatch reveal of a Legendary, and the four-into-one neon merge.

**Why there is no power to sell.** None of these passes beats another player. **Our read:** pets do not fight, so strength has no audience. What is sold is what other players can see (looks, homes, a ridable pet), more room (pen slots, backpack, listings) and more tries at rarity (eggs). Sonaria's official wiki says ordinary creatures hold little value because they are easy to earn ([wiki](https://creatures-of-sonaria-official.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Creature_Rarities)); value sits in limited and mutated ones.

**The line.** Robux eggs and Sonaria's Robux plushies that boost mutation chance are paid random items, so exact odds must be shown before purchase. ## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Live players a fraction of the record | A peak built on one event; Adopt Me peaked at about 1.88M to 1.92M in April 2021 (Ocean Egg) and sat at 430K to 465K on typical 2026 days, about a quarter ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/a-roblox-game-about-adopting-critters-just-set-a-new-concurrent-player-record/1100-6490282/), [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/adopt-me-in-2026-sugarfest-500k-players-and-what-s-next)) | A limited pet every season, not one giant drop |
| Trade values collapse | No cap on soft currency, so eggs flood | A daily cap (Adopt Me 300) |
| Common pets worthless | Too easy to earn, as Sonaria's wiki says of its non-limited creatures | Merges that eat commons (4 into 1) and a release reward (Adopt Me's Pet Releaser) |
| Scam reports | Open trading for young players | Licence test, 5-second restart timer, unfair warning, history |
| Breeding stops after a week | No rarity-scaled waits | Cooldowns from minutes to hours, fees rising about 2.5 times per rarity |
| Players AFK instead of playing | Passive pay with no cap; Sonaria pays 150 Shooms an hour alive | Cap passive income, as Adopt Me caps the Money Tree at 200 a day |

## Checklist

1. A new player has a free pet within 30 seconds.
2. The first need appears within 60 seconds and pays 10 to 20 soft currency.
3. A visible growth stage happens within 3 minutes for a common pet.
4. Soft currency from care has a daily cap of 15 to 30 needs.
5. Eggs come in at least three price tiers, each 1.5 to 2.5 times the last, with odds shown.
6. The cheapest egg costs about one day of capped earnings.
7. A merge ladder exists: 4 into 1, at least two levels.
8. Trades restart a confirm timer of at least 5 seconds on any change.
9. Top-rarity trades need a safety step (a licence or test).
10. Passive income is capped per day.
11. Breeding cooldowns rise with rarity, from under 10 minutes to over 1 hour.
12. No pass or product gives power over another player.
13. Paid random items show exact odds before purchase.
14. A limited pet arrives at least every 4 weeks.

## Twists and open space

Variations that have worked (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026):

- **Play as the animal in a pack.** Warrior Cats UE (4,318 live, 60 per server, 50 cosmetic passes); Creatures of Sonaria (21,714, 75 per server).
- **Horses.** Wild Horse Islands (2,525, 20 per server) and Horse Life (2,076, no passes).
- **Dragons you ride and breed.** Dragon Adventures (6,842, 20 per server).
- **Pets inside a city roleplay.** Dubai RP [Pets] (14,182, created November 2024); see [roleplay.md](roleplay.md).

**Our read** on open space:

- No animal game created in 2025 or 2026 appears in our research, while themed roleplay newcomers reached 2K to 15K players within 9 months. A pet game with a strong theme has room.
- A cross-server trading board is new (Adopt Me, July 2026). A smaller game could launch with one.
- Offline ageing slots (Adopt Me's Pet Pen, 4 pets, +1 for 295 R$) suit a care game better than a speed boost.
