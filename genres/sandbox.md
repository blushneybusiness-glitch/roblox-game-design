# Sandbox, building, jobs and physics toys

> **The feeling sold:** "I built it myself, and now we find out if it works." For the job games: "I have a job, and the paycheck buys my house." For the physics toys: "I can break anything and watch it fall apart."

This page covers three branches that share one trait: the player sets their own goals inside a toy. Building games ([Build a Boat for Treasure](../teardowns/build-a-boat-for-treasure.md), Plane Crazy, Theme Park Tycoon 2, Obby Creator), job simulators (Work at a Pizza Place), and the physics, ragdoll and destruction toys (Fling Things and People, Car Crushers 2, and the October to November 2026 ragdoll wave). Player counts marked "now" are from the Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026, a weekday morning in the US.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Build a Boat for Treasure is the leader with numbers. Gold figures are from the wiki ([Gold](https://build-a-boat-for-treasure.fandom.com/wiki/Gold), [Building Space](https://build-a-boat-for-treasure.fandom.com/wiki/Building_Space)). The length of one voyage is not known.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build | Places blocks on the team plot | not known | Boat grows on a plot about 95 x 128 studs | Blocks left |
| Launch | Starts the voyage | not known | The plot turns to water | none |
| Sail | Steers through colour-graded stages: green, yellow, red, purple | not known | Hazards break the boat | +8 gold at the black wall ending each stage |
| Reach the end | Opens the Treasure | not known | Chest | +25 gold |
| Spend | Buys blocks (Wood: 50 for 250 gold) or chests (5 to 405 gold) | not known | New blocks in the inventory | Gold down, blocks up |
| Rebuild | Fixes what broke, tries farther | not known | | Stage reached |

The wiki gives a theoretical rush rate of 54,000 gold an hour at 7 seconds per chest cycle, or 122,000 with both boosts. That is a ceiling for a boat built only to rush, not a normal run.

**How Pizza Place differs.** There is no goal at the end of a run. The player takes one of six jobs (Manager, Cashier, Cook, Pizza Boxer, Delivery, Supplier) and works. A paycheck lands every 15 minutes ([Paycheck](https://workatapizzaplace.fandom.com/wiki/Paycheck)).

**How the physics toys differ.** No loop could be sourced. Coins per spin or per crash: not known for Hyper Realistic Ragdoll, BloxNG or Fling Things and People.

**The loop stack** (Build a Boat, with Pizza Place where it has the only number):

| Every | What finishes | Example |
|---|---|---|
| One stage | +8 gold | Build a Boat |
| One run | +105 gold for 9 stages and the Treasure | Build a Boat wiki formula |
| 10 minutes | The manager can hand out a bonus | Pizza Place: 150 coins, 10-minute cooldown |
| 15 minutes | A paycheck | Pizza Place |
| One session | A new chest tier or a quest reward | Build a Boat quests pay 100 to 450 gold |
| One day | A daily check | Pizza Place: 200 coins |

## The first five minutes

No source describes how building is taught, step by step, in any of these games. What is known:

- Build a Boat gives 10 gold and 8 free blocks on first join ([Gold](https://build-a-boat-for-treasure.fandom.com/wiki/Gold), [Wood Block](https://build-a-boat-for-treasure.fandom.com/wiki/Wood_Block)). 10 gold buys exactly two Common chests or two Wood blocks.
- Build a Boat has three quests for newer players: Cloud (100 gold and 25 balloon blocks), Soccer (300 gold and a ball), Find Me (450 gold and 25 glue) ([main page](https://build-a-boat-for-treasure.fandom.com/wiki/Build_A_Boat_For_Treasure)).
- Pizza Place's first house step (Tiny, 100 coins) is paid for by the first paycheck, 15 minutes in ([House](https://workatapizzaplace.fandom.com/wiki/House)).

Gates for a new game. These are **Our read**, not measured:

| By | This has happened | Source or reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 0:05 | The player stands on their own plot with blocks in hand | Build a Boat's 8 free blocks |
| 0:30 | First block placed | A builder with nothing placed by 30 s is a menu |
| 1:00 | First test: the build moves, floats or breaks | The goal wrapper is the test |
| 3:00 | First currency from the test | Build a Boat pays at the end of each stage |
| 5:00 | First purchase from that currency | 10 gold start against a 5-gold chest; one stage pays 8 |

**Our read:** the game gives the toy away and charges for the parts that move. A plain wooden boat costs 5 gold a block in the shop, and a Common chest's wood costs 4 times that. A first moving part (a Cannon from a Rare chest) is expected after about 2.5 runs, and the parts that fly or grab (Thruster, Harpoon) after 5 to 8. The goal of reaching the chest pays for the next tool, and the next tool makes a farther run possible.

### Pizza Place: pay rules and the house ladder

Pay ([Paycheck](https://workatapizzaplace.fandom.com/wiki/Paycheck), [Jobs](https://workatapizzaplace.fandom.com/wiki/Jobs)):

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Paycheck | Every 15 minutes, to anyone who worked (or is on break after working) |
| Size | Set by orders delivered by the whole server: normal 10 coins, gold 20, neon 100, plus a combo bonus |
| Good shift | Over 500 coins possible |
| Star rating | 3 stars 100% of pay, 2 stars 80%, 1 star 30%, 0 stars nothing |
| Daily check | 200 coins |
| VIP (800 R) | +50% on work checks, +100% on daily checks (a promotional site says it doubles all coins; the wiki figures are used here) |
| Manager | One per server: 150-coin bonus every 10 minutes, Employee of the Day, can fire a 0-star worker without a vote; 8 votes remove the manager ([Manager](https://workatapizzaplace.fandom.com/wiki/Manager)) |

Cumulative coins to the Estate: 946,100. The coin shop sells 51 to 66 coins per R$ ([Coins](https://workatapizzaplace.fandom.com/wiki/Coins)). A promotional source said the Estate has no coin price; the wiki lists 600,000 coins.

**Our read:** at 300 to 1,000 coins per 15-minute paycheck, the Large House (10,200 cumulative) takes about 2.5 to 7.5 hours, and the Estate takes 237 to 789 hours. Nobody works that long, so the Estate's Robux price is the real path, and it gives about 3 times the coins per R$ of the coin shop. The Basement's Robux price (47 per R$) is worse than buying coins (62.5 per R$ in the 800 R$ pack). The house a player can buy at the end of a shift is the goal; the house they cannot is the product.

**Our read** on why it has lasted: pay only counts while the player is at a station, the paycheck depends on the whole server's orders, and the star rating cuts pay to 30% or 0% for slackers. In a 12-player server each worker's output is everyone's money. No developer statement confirms this.

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** Build a Boat: the next stage, and the next chest tier (one base run buys 2.3 Rare chests). Pizza Place: the next 15-minute paycheck. Car Crushers 2: a 20-minute energy core clock (see [driving.md](driving.md)).

**Tomorrow.** Pizza Place's daily check of 200 coins. The new builders pay offline: Build a Car to Go Far's car "earns coins offline", and Build a Racetrack's track earns offline (game pages, Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). Build a Car sells treadmill passes at 99, 299 and 799 R$ for 2x, 5x and 10x AFK earnings.

**Next week and after.** Build a Boat has 42 stages, of which 5 are seasonal ([Earnaldo, promotional](https://earnaldo.com/blog/build-a-boat-for-treasure-beginner-guide)). Obby Creator gives a social score: players "earn Likes to climb the leaderboards". BloxNG releases a code at each like target ("next code at 40K likes").

## Other players

Builders keep servers at 6 to 12. Pizza Place's 12 players share one paycheck pool. Build Your World lets players troll other plots with TNT and tornadoes, and sells Permanent Base Protection for 59 R$ (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). Fling Things and People lets players grab each other: 20-stud reach by default, and 12 seconds to escape a grab, lowered to 4 by a pass ([Earnaldo, promotional, 13 Apr 2026](https://earnaldo.com/blog/fling-things-and-people-free-robux-guide)).

**Our read:** the clip in a builder is the launch, when the boat works or comes apart in front of the team.

## Live ops

Build a Boat was updated 8 days before 25 Nov 2026 and Pizza Place the day before; a Spring 2026 Pizza Place update added an island. Islands, last updated 19 Jan 2026, has 417 players on 2.36B visits. Hyper Realistic Ragdoll was updated 2 days before (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).

A Dusty Trip, a driving journey with a heavy toy layer, released new story badges every 1 to 3 weeks from May 2024 to May 2025 ([badges](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/5650396773/badges?limit=100)). Its distance funnel loses 75% of players before 1,000 m and 86% of the rest before 5,000 m (our arithmetic: 368.7M "Welcome!", 91.7M at 1,000 m, 13.1M at 5,000 m). That structure belongs to [survival.md](survival.md).

**Our read:** a toy needs a new event or prop more often than a builder, because the toy itself does not change between visits.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| A copy launches days after the original and stays small | Same idea, no difference: Realistic Ragdoll Engine (12 Oct) has 2,599 against Hyper Realistic Ragdoll's 88,269 | Ship one feature the others lack, visible in the icon |
| Players fall with no update | Islands: last update 19 Jan 2026, 417 players on 2.36B visits | A dated update at least monthly |
| Vote ratio falls toward 80% | Fling Things and People: 80.5%, no update since 29 Oct 2025 | Fix griefing and add content; it still holds 33,575, so the toy alone is strong |
| A toy draws a crowd then has nothing to save | No currency or goal found for the 2026 ragdoll toys | Add a goal wrapper: a distance, a stage, a like count |
| Players quit at the first wall | A Dusty Trip: 75% never reach 1,000 m | Put the first reward before the first wall |

**Our read** on why toys have short lives: the 2026 wave's pages describe things to do (weapons, events, vehicles) and nothing to keep. Without a ladder, a player's second visit is the same as their first. The older toys that survive each added a layer: Car Crushers 2 has a money multiplier ladder and Platina prestige, Fling has plots that hold 100 to 200 objects. Retention numbers for any toy: not known.

## Checklist

- [ ] The player places their first block within 30 seconds.
- [ ] A free starting kit is enough for one complete build (Build a Boat: 8 blocks).
- [ ] Starting currency buys 2 of the cheapest items.
- [ ] Every checkpoint of a test pays about one cheapest item.
- [ ] A goal with a clear end exists (a chest, a distance, a like count).
- [ ] The first moving or special part arrives within 3 runs.
- [ ] Chest tiers triple in price; odds are published and sum to 100%.
- [ ] Limit passes cost 75 to 500 R$, and free space fits a complete build.
- [ ] Job pay lands every 15 minutes or less and needs the player at the station.
- [ ] A rating scales pay so slacking costs at least 70% of it.
- [ ] The first house or plot step is paid by the first paycheck.
- [ ] An update or event lands at least monthly.

## Twists and open space

Variations that have drawn players:

- **Build plus idle income.** Build a Car to Go Far (Sep 2026, 10,789 now): build, drive for coins, get crates with better engines, earn offline.
- **Build, then troll.** Build Your World (Oct 2026, 31,492 now): own plot, TNT and tornadoes, a 59 R$ protection pass.
- **Ragdoll as a sport.** Ragdoll Soccer (Oct 2026, 41,315 now): 4v4 and 8v8 servers with referees.

**Our read**, open angles: a ragdoll toy with Build a Boat's goal wrapper (stages and a chest), so the second visit has a target; a job simulator in a 2026 setting with Pizza Place's shared-pay rule; a builder whose test is a race against the next plot.
