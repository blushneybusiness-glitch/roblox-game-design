# Sports

> **The feeling sold:** "I rolled a rare player style and scored with it in front of nine other people."

This page covers team sports games on Roblox, with most of its numbers from the anime sports games of 2024 and 2025: [Blue Lock: Rivals](../teardowns/blue-lock-rivals.md) (soccer), Volleyball Legends and Azure Latch (soccer). These games put a spin system for playstyles on top of a short team match. Basketball Legends appears where its wiki adds something.

## Why people play it

The sources do not give ages or devices. Azure Latch's guide says the on-screen phone buttons are less precise and a controller is hard to use ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-azure-latch-beginner-tips-tricks/)), which tells you phone players are there in numbers.

**Our read:** the need is to play as a character from an anime the player already watches, with that character's signature move. The style spin decides which character you get. A match then gives you nine other players to show that move to. The cosmetic passes all sell the moment of showing: a custom goal sound (149 R$ in Blue Lock: Rivals, 129 R$ in Azure Latch), a custom flow music track (150 R$ in Azure Latch), awakening outfits (149 and 200 R$) ([Rolimons BLR](https://www.rolimons.com/game/18668065416), [Rolimons Azure](https://www.rolimons.com/game/94647229517154)).

## The loop, with a stopwatch

The leader is Blue Lock: Rivals. Seconds are not published for any step except the match, and that number is unverified.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lobby | Picks a mode: 3v3, 5v5, 8v8, Ranked, Pro Servers or AFK World | Not known | Lobby | None |
| Position | Takes a slot: in 5v5, CF, LW, RW, CM or GK | Not known | Team sheet | None |
| Match | Plays the match with the style's moves | 300 or 360 to 480 (two unofficial guides) | Style moves, flow awakening | Goals, cash |
| Spin | Spends 2,500 cash on a Style Spin or 2,000 on a Flow Spin | Not known; a 99 R$ pass skips the animation | Roll animation, rarity reveal | Rarity rolled, pity counter (50) |
| Equip | Sets the new style and goes back to the lobby | Not known | New kit in the next match | |

Sources: [Bloxguides BLR](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/blue-lock-rivals), [BLR wiki: Spins](https://blue-lock-rivals.fandom.com/wiki/Spins), [clip.dor.gg](https://clip.dor.gg/en/blog/blue-lock-rivals-roblox-guide), [earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/blue-lock-rivals-vs-volleyball-legends). Cash earned per match: not known, so minutes per spin are not known.

How the others differ:

- **Volleyball Legends** is 6v6 and rolls two things separately: Styles and 14 Abilities, each with its own spin ([RBLXGuide](https://rblxguide.com/games/volleyball-legends)).
- **Azure Latch** pays Style Coins as well as Cash, earned by scoring, dribbling, stealing and winning, and has 5v5 and 11v11 modes ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-azure-latch-beginner-tips-tricks/), [azurelatch.net](https://azurelatch.net/)).
- **Basketball Legends** splits a 5v5 "Main" mode that pays XP and Coins from a 1v1 to 3v3 "MyPark" with win records, leagues and trading ([Main](https://basketball-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Main), [MyPark](https://basketball-legends.fandom.com/wiki/MyPark)).

The loop stack, as far as it is known:

| Every | What finishes | Number |
|---|---|---|
| A few seconds | A dribble, steal, shot or save | Azure Latch pays Style Coins for each kind |
| One match | Cash for a spin | Not known per match |
| A few matches | A Style Spin | 2,500 cash; a Legendary or better about every 12.6 spins (our arithmetic, below) |
| 50 spins | Pity guarantees a Legendary or better | Both games |
| One week | Saturday update in Volleyball Legends; limited styles enter and leave | 11:30 EST |
| About 5 weeks | A Volleyball Legends season | 20 seasons by Update 95 (our arithmetic) |

Daily rewards: not known for any of the three games.

## The first five minutes

What is known: Azure Latch's first steps are load into the lobby, press Play, pick a team and a position, and optionally watch a game in progress or practise in a private server ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-azure-latch-beginner-tips-tricks/)). Volleyball Legends keeps the Secret tier out of the spin pool until account level 15 ([RBLXGuide](https://rblxguide.com/games/volleyball-legends/styles)). Codes hand out free spins: 10 Style Spins and 10 Flow Spins in Blue Lock: Rivals ([Stevivor](https://stevivor.com/guides/blue-lock-rivals-codes/)), 5 Lucky Spins in Volleyball Legends.

What is not known: a second-by-second first session for any sports game, how a new player is taught, and whether bots fill empty slots.

| By | This has happened | Source or reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 0:05 | The player is in a lobby with a Play button | Azure Latch's flow starts here |
| 0:30 | The player has a team and a position in a match | **Our read:** Azure Latch lets you pick team and position straight from Play |
| 1:00 | First touch of the ball | **Our read:** a 10-player server with 5 per side means about one in five touches is yours |
| 3:00 | First code redeemed or first spin rolled | **Our read:** codes give 5 to 10 spins; a first spin before the first match ends teaches the meta-loop |
| 5:00 | First match over, cash counted against the 2,500 spin price | **Our read:** the player should see how many matches to the next spin |

### Pity

### What a top style costs (our arithmetic)

Expected spins = 1 / chance. Cash = spins x 2,500. Robux = Lucky Spins x 199.

| Target | Blue Lock: Rivals, regular | Cash | Lucky Spins | Robux |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legendary or better (7.9%) | 12.6 | 31,600 | 1 (every Lucky Spin is Legendary or better) | 199 |
| Mythic (0.5%) | 200 | 500,000 | 20 | 3,980 |
| World Class (0.25%) | 400 | 1,000,000 | 40 | 7,960 |
| One Limited (0.125%) | 800 | 2,000,000 | 100 | 19,900 |
| Master (0.0325%) | 3,077 | 7,690,000 | 500 | 99,500 |

| Target | Volleyball Legends, regular spins | With pity | Lucky Spins |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legendary or better (2.5%) | 39.8 | 28.6 | 2 |
| Godly (0.495%) | 202 | | 21 |
| A chosen Secret (0.01%) | 10,000 | about 300 (50% at 200, sure at 400) | 200 |
| Ultra or Evo (0.005% each) | 20,000 | | 400 |

Volleyball Legends' price per spin in cash or Robux is not in the sources, so its costs stay in spins.

The chance that the 50-spin pity ever fires: in Blue Lock: Rivals (1 - 0.079)^50 = 1.6%; in Volleyball Legends (1 - 0.025)^50 = 28%.

**Our read:** the two pities do different jobs. Blue Lock: Rivals' pity almost never fires, so it is a promise more than a mechanic. Volleyball Legends' fires for more than a quarter of players and its 200 and 400-spin steps put a ceiling on the hunt for a Secret. A free player can see the end of that hunt; nobody can see the end of a 3,077-spin hunt for a Master.

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** The next spin is always a few matches away, and the pity counter goes up with every spin. **Our read:** the counter is the reason to play one more match.

**Tomorrow.** Not known. No daily reward or daily task was found for any of the three games.

**Next week and after.** Volleyball Legends updates every Saturday at 11:30 EST. Update 95, which opened Season 20 (Wild West), came on 7 Nov 2026 ([RBLXGuide](https://rblxguide.com/games/volleyball-legends)). Our arithmetic: 95 updates in about 99 weeks since the game was created, and a season about every 5 weeks. 22 of its 45 styles are limited and roll only while they are in the pool. Blue Lock: Rivals' ranked mode came in June 2025, and one guide reports another ranked update in March 2026 ([earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/blue-lock-rivals-vs-volleyball-legends)). Azure Latch runs limited events ([azurelatch.net](https://azurelatch.net/)).

## Other players

**Server size.** 10 to 12, so each match is the whole server. Pro Servers, AFK World and private servers sit beside the public match in Blue Lock: Rivals. Private servers are sold: 99 R$ in Blue Lock: Rivals, 300 R$ in Azure Latch.

**What they see.** The other players' styles in action, and the awakening forms each style has.

**What they can do with each other.** Pass, steal, block. Basketball Legends adds trading, with values checked against recent average price; cross-trading is banned and the Exalted tier cannot be traded ([Rarities](https://basketball-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Rarities)).

**Status.** Basketball Legends shows win streaks on the player: glowing eyes under 75 wins, an aura from 75 ([Streaks](https://basketball-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Streaks)). Its top 10 on a leaderboard get a Discord role and a chat tag.

**The moment that gets clipped.** **Our read:** a goal scored with a Master or Limited style during its awakening, with the scorer's paid goal sound.

Matchmaking by device: not found. Bots: not found.

## Live ops

Volleyball Legends is the model: one update every Saturday at 11:30 EST, about 95 updates in 99 weeks, a season about every 5 weeks, codes worth 5 Lucky Spins. Blue Lock: Rivals' update rhythm is not known; its November 2026 codes give 10 Style and 10 Flow Spins. Azure Latch was updated on 24 Nov 2026 (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026), rhythm not known.

What happens when updates stop: not measured. **Our read:** the only weekly updater of the three is also the only one still in the sports top three, at 41,347 players. This is one game, so it is a hint, not proof. See [foundations/13-live-ops.md](../foundations/13-live-ops.md).

**Our read:** a small team can sustain one new style a week if each style reuses the move set of an old one with new effects. Volleyball Legends' 14 abilities, rolled separately from 45 styles, give 630 combinations from 59 pieces of content.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CCU falls to about 1% of peak within 20 months | Blue Lock: Rivals: 1,097,082 to 14,469. Cause not known | **Our read:** a fixed weekly update with a limited style, as Volleyball Legends does |
| Top tiers appear almost never | Blue Lock: Rivals Master at 0.0325% with no pity: 3,077 spins | A second pity that ends the hunt (Volleyball Legends: sure Secret by 400 spins) |
| Pity never fires | Legendary-or-better odds so high that 50 spins always hit first (Blue Lock: Rivals: fires 1.6% of the time) | Lower first-prize odds to 4% to 5% so the counter matters |
| Phone players lose every duel for the ball | Azure Latch's guide calls phone buttons less precise | Fewer buttons on phone; matchmaking by device is not done by any leader |
| Odds disputes or policy trouble | Published tables that sum to 100.16% to 101.2% | Exact odds from the server, summing to 100% |
| A crowded chart with no room | 75 sports games, top ten from 9,000 to 36,209 players | **Our read:** pick a sport the anime leaders have not covered; the top two in late Nov 2026 are basketball games |

## Checklist

1. One match fills one server of 10 to 12.
2. A new player is in a match within 30 seconds of pressing Play.
3. Match length is fixed and shown on screen. (No leader publishes one; do better.)
4. The spin table is on screen before every spin and sums to exactly 100%.
5. First-prize odds give the 50-spin pity a chance of 10% or more to fire.
6. No top tier needs more than about 400 spins once all pity steps are counted.
7. The pity counter is visible.
8. The player can see how many matches pay for the next spin.
9. Paid spins show the odds that apply to that account (level gates included).
10. No pass sells stats; passes sell sounds, outfits, slots and skips at 49 to 299 R$.
11. One update a week, same day and time.
12. Each limited style shows how long it stays in the pool.
13. Ranked unlocks after a counted amount of play (Azure Latch: 500 goals or 250 saves).
14. Phone controls are tested in a full 5v5.

## Twists and open space

Variations that already produced hits:

- **Gacha for a playstyle, not a stat.** Blue Lock: Rivals: 32 styles and 25 flows on two separate spins, 1,097,082 peak.
- **Two rolls per player.** Volleyball Legends: a style and an ability, rolled on separate spins.
- **A goal count as a ranked gate.** Azure Latch: 500 goals or 250 saves.
- **A trading economy around a sport.** Basketball Legends: Common to Mythic plus an untradeable Exalted tier, MyPark leagues and records.

**Our read** on angles that look under-served:

- **Basketball with the anime style system.** The two biggest sports games in late November 2026 are Practical Basketball (36,209) and Basketball: Zero (31,754) ([rblxdb](https://rblxdb.com/games/genre/sports)); the style-spin structure is proven on soccer and volleyball.
- **Bots to fill a match.** No leader is known to use them. In a 10-player game, a short server cannot start.
- **A daily reason.** No daily reward was found for any leader. A daily free spin is the cheapest addition.
