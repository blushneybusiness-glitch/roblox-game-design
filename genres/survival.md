# Survival

> **The feeling sold:** "Me and my friends kept the fire going one more night, and we got further than last time."

Four shapes: co-op runs ([99 Nights in the Forest](../teardowns/99-nights-in-the-forest.md)), co-op journeys ([Dead Rails](../teardowns/dead-rails.md), A Dusty Trip), round-based survival (Natural Disaster Survival, Survive the Killer, Escape Tsunami for Brainrots, Survive Your Ideas) and sandboxes (The Survival Game, Booga Booga). Live numbers are from 25 Nov 2026.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Core verb | Feed the clock: put fuel in the fire or the train before it runs out |
| Study these first | [99 Nights in the Forest](../teardowns/99-nights-in-the-forest.md): the fire as clock, the calendar speed-up, classes bought with earned diamonds. [Dead Rails](../teardowns/dead-rails.md): fuel weighed in metres, a safe town every 10 km. A Dusty Trip: a vehicle you build as the base. Natural Disaster Survival: a 2.5-minute round that has run since 2011. Survive Your Ideas: a 2026 round game where one player's typed word is the disaster. |
| Session that works | 9 to 19 minutes on average across 11 games; 99 Nights 16.13, Dead Rails 14.87 ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/79546208627805), [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/116495829188952)) |
| Players per server | Runs and journeys: 16 to 25 per server with parties of 1 to 5. Rounds: 8 to 40 in one shared arena (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026) |
| Who owns the genre | Open to new games. The big 2025-26 hits peaked young: 99 Nights at 7 months, Dead Rails at 3.5 months, Escape Tsunami at 40 days. Their 30-day peaks are now 10.7%, 2.6% and 0.57% of those records. Two old round games hold flat: Natural Disaster Survival (2008) and Survive the Killer (2020, 30-day peak 70% of record) |
| Build cost | Not known from sources. **Our read:** a first version needs one map, one clock object, 3 to 4 enemy types and a 5-step upgrade ladder; the cost is enemy AI and night pacing, then the weekly content that the leaders ship |
| Money comes from | Revives (39 to 55 R$), premium-currency packs for permanent classes, weapon and storage passes |
| What kills clones | A run longer than a session with nothing kept when it ends, and no update after the spike |

## Why people play it

Who plays (age, device) is not known from these sources. What the numbers show:

- **They play together.** Parties of 1 to 5 in 99 Nights ([fan wiki](https://99nightsintheforest.wiki/)), up to 4 in Dead Rails ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/dead-rails)).
- **They chase a day count or a distance.** 408.7M players have survived 10 days in 99 Nights and 447.4M have survived a disaster in Natural Disaster Survival ([badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/7326934954/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc), [badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/65241/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc)).
- **They rate it well.** 90% to 92.5% for 99 Nights, Dead Rails, A Dusty Trip and Natural Disaster Survival (Rolimons: [99 Nights](https://www.rolimons.com/game/79546208627805), [Dead Rails](https://www.rolimons.com/game/116495829188952), [A Dusty Trip](https://www.rolimons.com/game/16389395869), [NDS](https://www.rolimons.com/game/189707)).

**Our read:** the need is a shared near-miss. A night that almost killed the party is the story friends retell, and the badge proves it.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

The leader is 99 Nights in the Forest. One cycle is a 3:00 day plus a 1:30 night ([wiki: Days](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Days)).

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gather | Chops logs, loots, grinds junk into scrap | 0 to 180 | Sack of 5, 15 or 20 slots fills | Logs, scrap |
| Feed | Drops fuel into the campfire | Any time in the day | Fire bar rises; at each level the map and safe zone widen | Fire level 1 to 6 |
| Build | Spends wood and scrap at the bench | Any time in the day | New object at camp | Bench tier 1 to 5 |
| Hold | Stays in the safe zone, stuns the deer with a flashlight | 180 to 270 | Deer, wolves, cultists on raid nights | HP, hunger |
| Count | Survives to dawn | 270 | Day counter jumps | +1 to +9 days |

Sources: [wiki: Campfire](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire), [wiki: Crafting](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Crafting), [guide](https://ddingpy.github.io/mynote/posts/99-nights-in-the-forest-roblox-survival-guide).

How the others differ:
- **Dead Rails** uses an 8-minute day (5:20 day, 2:40 night) and a distance counter. The fuel goes in a train and the "camp" moves ([wiki: Day Cycle](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Day_Cycle)).
- **A Dusty Trip** starts with 9 L of fuel in a red can. The player builds a car or van (engine, wheels at minimum) and drives. It has no defined win ([DroidGamers](https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/a-dusty-trip-tutorial/)).
- **Natural Disaster Survival** has no resources. About 40 seconds to prepare, then a hidden disaster for 1:40 to 2:25; one round in three stacks several disasters ([Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Stickmasterluke/Natural_Disaster_Survival)).
- **Survive Your Ideas** gives one random player per turn the "Writer" role. They type a word and it happens to everyone; last alive wins ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/survive-your-ideas)).

The loop stack in 99 Nights:

| Every | What finishes |
|---|---|
| A few seconds | A log chopped, an item ground into 1 to 8 scrap |
| 4:30 | A day and night; the counter moves |
| 13:30 to 18:00 | Pelt Trader visit (every 3 to 4 days); a Cultist raid (every 4 days) ([wiki: Night Raids](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Raids)) |
| One session (16 min) | Fire level 3 or 4, the first raid at 16:30 |
| One run (about 90 to 445 min) | Day 99: 3 diamonds and an ending cutscene |
| One day | Three daily quests, 5 diamonds each or 15 for all; a 24-hour class discount |
| One week or two | Saturday update ([wiki: Update Log](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Update_Log)) |

## The first five minutes

99 Nights at a 1x counter (days 1 to 10 ignore multipliers):

| By | This has happened | Source or reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 0:05 | Party size picked (1 to 5) in the lobby box | [fan wiki](https://99nightsintheforest.wiki/) |
| 0:30 | Fire lit for 1 log | [wiki: Campfire](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire) |
| 1:00 | Working toward level 2 (6 logs) | Our estimate |
| 3:00 | Dusk. Target: fire level 3 (11 logs) | [wiki: Days](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Days) |
| 5:00 | Night 1 survived without a deer attack; day 2 started at 4:30 | [wiki: Days](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Days) |

A Dusty Trip's lobby gives 10 seconds to set the party size and starts the run 15 seconds later ([fan wiki](https://a-dusty-trip.pages.dev/)); the player then assembles a car from parts in a garage before driving ([DroidGamers](https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/a-dusty-trip-tutorial/)).

Gates for a new game (**Our read**, from the funnels in "Numbers that work"):

- Playing within 25 seconds of joining (A Dusty Trip's 10 + 15).
- The clock object visible and fed once by 0:30.
- The first visible upgrade (a bigger fire, a moving vehicle) by 1:00.
- The first night or threat by 3:00, and survived by 5:00, with the counter moving on screen.
- A Dusty Trip shows why this matters: only 24.9% of players who earn its Welcome badge reach 1 km ([badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/5650396773/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc)).

## Numbers that work

**Clocks** (wiki pages linked above):

| Game | Active phase | Danger phase | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 99 Nights | Day 3:00 | Night 1:30 | 2:1 |
| Dead Rails | Day 5:20 | Night 2:40 | 2:1 |
| Natural Disaster Survival | Prep about 0:40 | Disaster 1:40 to 2:25 | about 1:3 |

**The burnable clock.** Dead Rails fuel, gauge out of 50 ([Beebom](https://beebom.com/dead-rails-fuel/amp/)): coal 13 units (4,225 m), barrel 6, chair 5, book 3, any body 2 (650 m), newspaper 1 (325 m). Coal costs $20 ([DroidGamers](https://www.droidgamers.com/guides/how-many-coal-do-you-need-to-finish-dead-rails/)). Our arithmetic: a full tank is 20% of the 80 km run, one coal 5%, and 19 coal ($380) finishes it.

99 Nights campfire, logs per level: 6, 11, 50-75, 70-120, 275-350. Coal and canisters join at the second step, oil barrels at the fourth ([wiki: Campfire](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire)). If the fire dies the run is effectively lost: the deer always spawns, the safe zone and regen vanish.

**The calendar.** Each rescued kid and each Old Bed adds 1 day per night, capped at 9 ([wiki: Missing Children](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Missing_Children)). 99 days takes 445.5 minutes at 1x and 49.5 at 9x ([wiki: Days](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Days)).

**Threat steps.** Dead Rails: 6 runners on night 1, +6 per cloudy night, 30 at night 5 ([wiki: Day Cycle](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Day_Cycle)). 99 Nights: first raid day 4, then every 4 days, up to 8 cultists ([wiki: Night Raids](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Raids)).

**Permanent progress across runs:**

| Game | Currency | Free income | Class prices | Everything costs | Packs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 99 Nights | Diamonds | About 35 per full-routine day | 10 to 600 | 9,165 | 3.57 to 4.95 R$ per diamond |
| Dead Rails | Bonds | 10 per win, 100 to 130 per fully looted run | 15 to 100 | 1,545 classes + 3,100 trains | 2.83 to 13 R$ per bond |

Sources: [wiki: Diamonds](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Diamonds), [wiki: Classes](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Classes), [wiki: Bonds](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Bonds), [wiki: Classes](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Classes), [wiki: Train](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Train).

**Party size.** 99 Nights fixes difficulty by party size at the start and does not lower it when players leave; solo is easiest ([fan wiki](https://99nightsintheforest.wiki/)). The fire is also harder to fuel with more players ([wiki: Campfire](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Campfire)). No source gives a formula. Dead Rails sets difficulty by chosen mode instead: Hard +25% bonds, Extreme +50%, Nightmare +100% ([wiki: Changelogs](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Changelogs)).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 20 | 68.6% | 20 km | 64.0% | 1 km | 24.9% |
| Day 30 | 50.7% | 30 km | 47.5% | 5 km | 3.6% |
| Day 50 | 34.2% | 50 km | 33.2% | 10 km | 1.4% |

Sources: [99 Nights badges](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/7326934954/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc), [Dead Rails badges](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/7018190066/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc), [A Dusty Trip badges](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/5650396773/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc).

**Our read:** runs die early. The first step of each ladder loses the most players: 31% in 99 Nights, 36% in Dead Rails, 75% in A Dusty Trip. After the halfway mark almost nobody quits: 82% of Dead Rails players past 50 km reach 80 km. Then a boss is a second wall: 44% of finishers beat Tesla.

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** The counter. Each night survived moves it, and kids or beds make it jump. Dead Rails' next safe town is never more than 10 km away, about 2.6 minutes of driving at top speed if the [wiki's](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Train) speed of 65 is metres per second (our arithmetic). **Our read:** the next goal is always under 5 minutes away, so "one more night" or "one more town" is the reason to stay.

**Tomorrow.** 99 Nights has three daily quests (5 diamonds each, 15 for all three) and a 24-hour class discount of 10%, 15% or 20% ([wiki: Classes](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Classes)). Dead Rails has three weekly challenges at 25 bonds ([wiki: Bonds](https://deadrails.fandom.com/wiki/Bonds)).

**Next week and after.** A class bought with diamonds is kept forever and changes the next run: Camper cuts hunger drain 10%, Berserker auto-revives once. Each class has 3 levels unlocked by counters, for example Medic level 2 at 30 supplies and 25 revives ([wiki: Classes](https://99-nights-in-the-forest.fandom.com/wiki/Classes)). Hard Mode unlocks after a best of 10 days.

## Other players

- **Server size.** Runs: 25 (99 Nights, A Dusty Trip), 16 (Dead Rails). Rounds: 8 (Escape Tsunami), 13 (Survive the Killer), 30 (NDS), 40 (Survive Your Ideas) (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). **Our read:** a run server holds several small parties; a round server is one arena where more players means more deaths to watch.
- **What they do together.** Split roles. The guide roles in 99 Nights are fuel runner, scavenger, hunter and builder ([guide](https://ddingpy.github.io/mynote/posts/99-nights-in-the-forest-roblox-survival-guide)). Healer, fuel and tank classes exist in both run leaders: Medic and Doctor; Lumberjack, Miner and Conductor; Base Defender and Ironclad.
- **What can be lost.** The run. Permanent currency and classes are never lost. Revives soften a death.
- **Full parties are rare.** 99 Nights' Teamwork badge (99 days with a team of 5) is 2.2% of day-10 players ([badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/7326934954/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc)).
- **The clip.** **Our read:** the deer at the fire, a raid wave, the train running dry. In Survive Your Ideas, the typed word itself.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 30-day peak under 1% of all-time peak within a year | A meme spike on a thin loop. Escape Tsunami: 4,996,032 peak 40 days after launch, 0.57% ten months later, 11.67-min sessions ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/131623223084840)) | A permanent layer that runs keep feeding. The games with classes (99 Nights, Dead Rails, A Dusty Trip) hold 2.6% to 10.7% |
| Average session under 7 minutes | Rounds too short to build attachment. Survive Your Ideas: 6.80 min; 107,836 peak around 18 Nov 2026, 25,930 in a European-evening snapshot a week later ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/77900175935766)) | Add something kept between matches beyond the Index. Peak and snapshot are at different hours, so the size of the fall is not yet known |
| Over 30% of players lost at the first milestone | Run longer than a session. Dead Rails: 30+ min runs, 14.87-min sessions, 36% lost before 20 km | Put the first badge and the first permanent payout inside 15 minutes |
| Three quarters never pass the first marker | Setup before play. A Dusty Trip: build a car first; 24.9% reach 1 km | Hand over a working vehicle; teach building on the second run |
| Players fall flat after the spike, no update in months | Live ops stopped. Break In 2 last updated Apr 2025, 2.0% of peak | One headline update every 1 to 2 weeks |
| Long sessions, tiny peak | A sandbox with no shared goal. The Survival Game: 19.25-min sessions, 63,905 peak, 435 now ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/11156779721)) | A counter (days, km) that every party races |
| Low rating | Unfair deaths or paid power. Field Trip Z 77.9% sells Infinite Lives 499 and a Gravity Gun 1,600 ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/4954096313)) | Sell relief, not wins; keep revive prices near the 45 R$ median of the charts |

## Checklist

1. The player is in play within 25 seconds of joining.
2. The clock object (fire, fuel gauge, round timer) is on screen by 0:30.
3. The first visible upgrade happens by 1:00.
4. Day : night is about 2:1, one cycle 4 to 8 minutes.
5. The first real threat arrives near your target session length (99 Nights: 16:30 for a 16-minute session).
6. A safe stop or checkpoint every 1/8 of the run.
7. Every fuel item and enemy has a stated fuel value; the best common item fills about 25%.
8. The upgrade ladder has 4 to 6 steps and a new resource at least twice.
9. Difficulty is fixed by party size at the start (1 to 5).
10. Something permanent is earned in every run, win or lose, and the cheapest class costs less than one run.
11. The first distance or day badge is reachable in one session; measure the drop to the second and keep it under 35%.
12. A revive costs 39 to 55 R$, and a free route to revive exists.
13. One update every 7 to 14 days, released on the same weekday.
14. The full run fits 1 to 6 sessions, or can be shortened by player choice (99 Nights 9x: about 90 min).

## Twists and open space

Variations that already produced hits:
- **The calendar the party speeds up:** 99 Nights kids and beds, 1x to 9x.
- **The moving base:** Dead Rails train, A Dusty Trip car.
- **A player makes the disaster:** Survive Your Ideas, 107,836 CCU in about 6 weeks ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/77900175935766)). 85% of Welcome badge holders typed a first idea, 14.0% won once, 1.8% won twice in a row ([badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/10769671316/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc)).
- **Stacked rounds:** NDS makes one round in three multi-disaster, up to 7 with the 85 R$ Weather Machine.

Why Natural Disaster Survival still plays 15 years after its full release (4 Dec 2011): 13 disasters on 23 maps, about 40 s of prep and a disaster of 1:40 to 2:25, no health regeneration, and three cheap passes ([Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Stickmasterluke/Natural_Disaster_Survival)). That makes a round about 2.5 to 3 minutes (our arithmetic). It holds a 13,895 30-day peak and 11.97M favorites ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/189707)). **Our read:** a 9.19-minute session is three rounds; nothing is lost between rounds, so any minute is a good minute to join, and a new player is level with a veteran.

Open space, all **Our read:**
- A round game with a run inside it: NDS-length rounds, but surviving rounds feeds a camp that persists for the server's life.
- A journey with a short leg: a 15-minute route with the same fuel maths as Dead Rails, so a full win fits one session.
- A round game with permanent classes bought with earned currency. Survive the Killer comes closest (killer bundles sold for Robux, then rebought with in-game gems) and its 30-day peak is still 70% of its 2022 record ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/4580204640), [game-passes API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/1489026993/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=50)).
- A survival sandbox with a shared counter. Booga Booga and The Survival Game have the longest sessions in the set (19.27 and 19.25 min) and small crowds (927 and 435 now) ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/11729688377), [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/11156779721)). A day or distance counter on top of their crafting is untried among the games studied.
