# Story

> **The feeling sold:** "We played the whole story together, beat the boss, and next time we're getting the secret ending."

This page covers co-op story and adventure games: Break In and Break In 2, Field Trip Z, and new 2026 entrants such as Say Cheese! [STORY]. The research behind this page is thin. Chapter lengths, wave timers and the "camping"-style story series were not found, and this page says "not known" wherever that matters. Live numbers are from 25 Nov 2026.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Core verb | Do the current task (gather food, train, fight a wave) so the story moves on |
| Study these first | Break In 2: waves, a 4-phase boss, 27 badges, endings gated by items. Break In: five in-game days ending in a vote, four endings, a Hardcore mode. Field Trip Z: the same structure with zombies. Say Cheese! [STORY]: a 2026 entrant on 12-player servers. None has a teardown |
| Session that works | 10 to 11 minutes on average: Break In 2 11.05, Field Trip Z 10.14 ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/13864661000), [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/4954096313)) |
| Players per server | Break In 2 55, Break In 45, Field Trip Z 43, Say Cheese 12 (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). How many play one story together is not known |
| Who owns the genre | Old games, mostly unattended. Break In (2019) and Break In 2 (2023) hold about 1.8K each with no update since Oct 2023 and Apr 2025. Break In 2 peaked at 181,535 three months after launch. Say Cheese (Oct 2026) had 27.99M visits in five weeks but 1,081 players at snapshot |
| Build cost | Not known from sources. **Our read:** high per hour of play. A story run is scripted content that is used up, while a survival run reuses the same map every night |
| Money comes from | Class passes from 299 to 1,299 R$; small helper passes at 99 to 199 R$; a heal product |
| What kills clones | A story that has been seen once, with no new badge to chase and no update |

## Why people play it

Who plays is not known from these sources. The badge counts show what most players do and what a few chase:

- The most-awarded badge listed in our notes for each Break In game is Pizza Party: 136.0M in Break In, 24.6M in Break In 2 ([badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/1318971886/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc), [badges API](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/4807308814/badges?limit=100&sortOrder=Asc)). Its condition is not in our notes.
- Break In 2 ratings sit at 86.0%, Field Trip Z at 77.9% ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/13864661000), [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/4954096313)).

**Our read:** the need is a shared story with a clear end and a boss, played with friends in one sitting, then replayed for a different ending.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

The leader is Break In 2. Its structure comes from a badge guide that is over three years old ([The Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/how-to-get-all-21-badges-in-roblox-break-in-2)). No source gave a time for any step.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pick a role | Kid or Adult, or a paid class | not known | Role kit in hand | Starting stats |
| Prepare | Train strength and speed at the gym, collect food | not known | Stat level | Strength and speed, levels 1 to 5 |
| Wave | Defend against waves 1/3, 2/3, 3/3 | not known | Wave counter | Wave number |
| Tool Time | After wave 1, take a random legendary weapon from a vending machine | not known | Vending machine roll | Weapon rarity |
| Side quest | Uncle Pete's quest: about 15 enemies | not known | Quest marker | Enemies defeated |
| Boss | Fight Scary Mary in 4 phases | not known | Boss phases | Boss HP |
| Ending | Good ending, team wipe back to the lobby, or a secret or alternate ending | not known | Ending scene, badge | Badge |

The 4 boss phases and the damage dealer, protector and medic roles come from a low-quality aggregator ([endings guide](https://roblox-break-in-2-endings-guide.pages.dev)).

How the others differ: **Break In** runs over five in-game days and ends with a vote on day 5, with four endings ([wiki: Final ending](https://break-in-story.fandom.com/wiki/Final_ending)). **Field Trip Z** sells Infinite Lives (499 R$) and a revive pass (199 R$) ([game-passes API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/1701332290/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=50)). **Our read:** death costs more there than in Break In. Say Cheese's design was not researched.

The loop stack:

| Every | What finishes |
|---|---|
| A wave | Wave counter, maybe a weapon roll (Tool Time after wave 1) |
| One run | An ending and its badge |
| Several runs | Stat-gated badges: Avoid Humiliation needs Level 3 strength and speed, 7+ food items and an Epic or Legendary weapon ([The Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/how-to-get-all-21-badges-in-roblox-break-in-2)) |
| 10+ badges | Break In's Hardcore mode opens through the Guest class ([wiki: Hardcore](https://break-in-story.fandom.com/wiki/Hardcore)) |
| Day, week | Nothing found in the sources |

Gates for a new game (**Our read**, from the 10 to 11 minute average session):

- The role choice is made before 0:30, and the role's job (lift, fight, heal) is used before 1:00.
- The first story beat (a threat at the door, a call for help) by 1:00.
- The first wave or chapter ends by 5:00, so one session holds two or more beats.
- A first ending is reachable in about two average sessions (20 to 25 minutes), or the run saves between sessions.

**How an alternate ending is gated** (all from badge and wiki pages):
- Break In 2 Secret Ending: a Golden Crowbar, Scary Larry's Hat and Mask ([The Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/how-to-get-all-21-badges-in-roblox-break-in-2)).
- Break In Final Ending: Uncle Pete's quest (three foods), the rat Louie and a key ([wiki: Final ending](https://break-in-story.fandom.com/wiki/Final_ending)).
- Break In 2 Dream Team: unlocks in the last wave if three side badges are done (The Nerd Stash, same link).
- Break In 2 Family Bonding: a Kid needs an Adult to lift a gate (same link).

**Hardcore.** Break In's Hardcore gives a sword, very low energy, no Medkit healing, much higher damage and ignores gamepasses. It opens through the Guest class after 10+ badges. A full-heal product costs 399 R$ ([wiki: Hardcore](https://break-in-story.fandom.com/wiki/Hardcore)).

**Our read:** each alternate ending reaches 5% to 15% of players who reach the boss. Hardcore reaches 0.03% to 0.1%. Endings are content for the top tenth, but they are the reason a finished story gets played again.

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** The next wave (1/3 to 3/3) and the boss. Wave timers are not known.

**Tomorrow.** Nothing found in the sources: no daily rewards or quests were described.

**Next week and after.** Badges and endings. Break In 2's stat-gated badges need Level 3 or 5 in strength and speed, so many can only be earned on a later run ([The Nerd Stash](https://thenerdstash.com/how-to-get-all-21-badges-in-roblox-break-in-2)). Hardcore opens after 10 badges. Updates have stopped in all three older games (see Live ops).

## Other players

- **Server size.** 43 to 55 per server for the older games; 12 for Say Cheese (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).
- **What they do together.** Roles depend on each other: a Kid needs an Adult to lift a gate. A healer, a protector and a damage dealer are named for the boss fight (low-quality source, above). Break In sells "Extra Cash" (199 R$) to carry leftover money between rounds ([game-passes API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/1318971886/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=50)). **Our read:** money is spent inside each round and normally resets.
- **What can be lost.** The run: a team wipe returns everyone to the lobby.
- **Revive.** Field Trip Z sells an "Antidote" pass to revive friends for 199 R$ and Infinite Lives for 499.
- **The clip.** **Our read:** the boss's phase change and the secret ending scene.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Peak three months after launch, then 2% | Story used up. Break In 2: 181,535 on 24 Sep 2023, 3,590 30-day peak now | Ship an ending or a mode every 1 to 2 months while the peak lasts |
| No update for over a year | Team moved on. Break In 2 (Apr 2025), Break In (Oct 2023) | One new badge set or ending per update |
| Rating under 80% | Paid power and death relief. Field Trip Z 77.9%, sells Infinite Lives 499 and a 1,600 R$ Gravity Gun | Sell roles and convenience; keep Hardcore pass-free, as Break In does |
| Main ending under 20% of boss fights | A wall between the boss and the ending. Break In 2: 18.7% | Put a badge on each step and lower the steepest drop |
| Many visits, few players | A launch spike without a hook. Say Cheese: 27.99M visits in five weeks, 1,081 at snapshot | not known from our notes; re-check its numbers |

## Checklist

1. A role is chosen before 0:30 and used before 1:00.
2. Two free roles need each other at least once per run (a gate, a heal).
3. The first wave or chapter ends by 5:00.
4. The run has 3 waves (or 5 days) and a boss with phases.
5. The main ending is earned by about 20% of boss fights; measure it with a badge.
6. At least 3 alternate endings, each gated by 2 to 3 items or a side quest.
7. 20 or more badges, some gated by stats that take more than one run.
8. A Hardcore mode opens after 10 badges and ignores paid items.
9. A starter class around 299 R$ and an elite class around 1,299 R$; Break In 2 sells 2 class passes and Break In 3.
10. A team wipe returns players to the lobby within 10 seconds, with the next run one click away.
11. An update with a new ending, badge set or mode at least every 2 months.
12. Run length timed on a device and written down, since no leader's is public.

## Twists and open space

Variations that have shipped:
- **The ending vote:** Break In's day-5 vote ([wiki: Final ending](https://break-in-story.fandom.com/wiki/Final_ending)).
- **The Hardcore unlock:** 10+ badges, gamepasses ignored ([wiki: Hardcore](https://break-in-story.fandom.com/wiki/Hardcore)).
- **Playing the villain:** Field Trip Z's Become a Zombie pass, 899 R$.

Open space, all **Our read:**
- **Story plus survival clock.** A story told across a [99 Nights](../teardowns/99-nights-in-the-forest.md)-style run, where each rescued character is a chapter and an upgrade, as the kids with jobs already are in 99 Nights.
- **A story with live chapters.** The older games stopped updating. A team that ships one chapter every two weeks on a set day has no direct competitor in our research.
- **Small servers.** Say Cheese uses 12 players per server against 43 to 55 in the older games. Whether smaller servers work better for story is not known yet.
