# Party and minigames

> **The feeling sold:** "Every few minutes there is a new game, and this time I might be the one who wins."

This page covers four shapes that share one idea, short rounds against a full server:

- **Minigame collections**: a random playlist of small games. Leader: [Epic Minigames](../teardowns/epic-minigames.md).
- **Elimination shows**: one long match of games where losers are out. Leader: Ink Game.
- **Mass playground games**: one rule, a big server, everyone at once. Example: Huss Valley.
- **Whole-server goals**: everyone builds one thing together. Example: Build the Pyramid!

Round survival games with one hazard per round (Natural Disaster Survival) are covered in [survival.md](survival.md). Hidden-role rounds are in [social-deduction.md](social-deduction.md).

Player counts marked "now" are from the Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026, a weekday.

## Why people play it

Our research found no player survey. The structure shows three things:

- Short rounds. Ink Game's games last 1 to 3 minutes ([PropelRC](https://www.propelrc.com/?p=134445)). Natural Disaster Survival gives about 40 seconds to prepare, then a disaster of 1:40 to 2:25 ([Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Stickmasterluke/Natural_Disaster_Survival)).
- Rules in the name. Epic Minigames' games have one-to-three-word names: Whack-a-Block, Lava Forecast, Hurdle Hurry ([wiki](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Minigames)).
- Cheap losses. A dead Epic Minigames player waits in a lobby with a pool and a slide ([wiki: The Lobby](https://epic-minigames-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Lobby)). A caught Huss Valley player becomes a chaser ([Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=10764627709)).

**Our read:** the need met is "a group game I can join halfway through and understand in one round". A loss lasts one round, so a weaker player still gets a new chance every few minutes. Age and device split: not known.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Leader: Epic Minigames. Its wiki records rewards and prices but almost no clocks, so most seconds are "not known".

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Wait in the lobby | Plays with lobby toys: Spleef area, cannon, beach, leaderboard room | not known | The other 11 players, the shop stalls, an Update Board with the last 10 or 11 updates | none |
| 2. Next minigame picked | Nothing, or pays for a pick | not known | The name of the next game. It is random unless a player spends a "minigame choice" (R$9 on a normal server) | Choices owned |
| 3. Play | Runs, dodges, shoots, jumps, by the game's one rule | not known | One of three types: Solo Winner, Free-For-All, or Team (Red against Blue) | Players still alive |
| 4. Win or lose | Last one standing, or top 3, or the winning team | not known | Losers return to the lobby during the game | 10 coins per win (15 on Pro servers) |
| 5. Timers pay | Keeps playing | 5, 20 and 40 minutes | Playtime rewards | 15, 30, 45 coins |

Sources: [Typical Games wiki: Epic Minigames](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Minigames), [Solo Winner](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Solo_Winner), [Free-For-All](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Free-For-All), [Team](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Team).

How the other shapes differ:

- **Ink Game** runs a fixed sequence in a 100-player lobby: Red Light Green Light, Dalgona, Lights Out, a voted slot (Tug of War or Hide and Seek; Pentathlon is the newest option), Glass Stepping Stones, Mingle, Last Dinner, then the Squid Game final. Sky Squid Game replaces Last Dinner when 4 to 8 players remain. An O/X vote can turn the match into a Rebellion against the guards. That October 2026 guide counts 13 games and 3 endings ([PropelRC](https://www.propelrc.com/?p=134445)); the fan wiki says 9 and lists Jump Rope and Glass Bridge instead ([wiki: Games](https://ink-game.fandom.com/wiki/Games)). Treat it as 9 to 13 games listed, fewer played per match.
- **Huss Valley** has one chaser. Everyone else must cross the map. A caught player becomes a chaser. There is a ranked mode on an ice-skating map and abilities that level up ([Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=10764627709)). Round length: not known.
- **Build the Pyramid!** has no rounds that our research could find. Players carry blocks to one shared pyramid, train speed and strength, and each finished pyramid makes them stronger ([Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=10765012427)). Pyramid sizes and timers: not known.

| 1 day (00:00 UTC) | 3 daily missions, 30 coins each | Epic Minigames |

Our arithmetic: in a 12-player Solo Winner game where every player has an equal chance, the expected win income is 10 / 12, about 0.8 coins per game. A 40-minute session that clears the three missions and one chest earns 90 (playtime) + 90 (missions) + 30 (chest) = 210 coins from timers. **Our read:** for most players, timers and missions pay far more than winning. That keeps weak players earning, and it means the reward for skill is the win itself.

### Ink Game power rolls

| Rarity | Odds | Powers |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 72% | Medic, Player 120 |
| Rare | 17% | Trickster, Parkour Artist |
| Epic | 8% | Super Strength, Sharpshooter, Black Flash |
| Legendary | 2.9% | Phantom Step, Weapon Smuggler, Hercules |
| Mythic | 0.1% | Lightning God, Quicksilver, The Doctor, Time Stop |

Price: R$175 per roll, or 10 for R$1,699. Free rolls come from finishing the Main Ending, the Rebel Ending, or staying 6 hours in AFK World ([wiki: Powers](https://ink-game.fandom.com/wiki/Powers)). The odds sum to 100%.

Our arithmetic on that table:

| Target | Expected rolls | Expected R$ at 175 each | Expected R$ in 10-packs (169.9 each) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any Legendary | 1 / 0.029 = about 34.5 | about 6,030 | about 5,860 |
| Any Mythic | 1 / 0.001 = 1,000 | 175,000 | 169,900 |

The chance of at least one Mythic in a 10-pack is 1 − 0.999^10, about 1.0%. In 1,000 rolls it is about 63%. One specific Mythic power, if the four share the 0.1% equally, takes about 4,000 rolls. Powers change how a player does in the games, so this is a paid random item that affects winning; see [foundations/15-the-line.md](../foundations/15-the-line.md).
**Ink Game Hide and Seek** splits the survivors 50/50; an odd count gives the extra player to hiders (15 players: 8 hiders, 7 seekers). It lasts 4 minutes. Seekers carry knives and are eliminated if they kill nobody; hiders find triangle, square or circle keys to open doors ([wiki](https://ink-game.fandom.com/wiki/Hide_and_Seek)).

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** Epic Minigames' timers (loop stack above). In Ink Game, finishing an ending pays a free power roll ([wiki: Powers](https://ink-game.fandom.com/wiki/Powers)).

**Tomorrow.** Epic Minigames resets three 30-coin missions at 00:00 UTC, such as "Win 3 in a row" or "Win a named minigame". Clearing all three pays a random effect, gear or title ([Daily missions](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_missions)).

**Next week and after.** The Epic Pass starts by itself at 00:00 UTC on 1 March, 1 June, 1 September and 1 December and runs 56 days ([wiki: Epic Pass](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Pass)). Our arithmetic: it is live 224 days a year (61%), with gaps of 34 to 37 days. Ink Game pays a free roll for 6 hours in AFK World. Huss Valley's level badges (100 and 500) are the long goal.

**Our read:** a pass on fixed calendar dates is the cheapest return hook here. Players can learn "it starts on the 1st" and the studio does not need to announce it.

## Other players

- **Server size.** 12 gives an even-chance win in 1 of 12 Solo Winner games. Epic Minigames' 36-player servers allow 3 winners in solo games, which keeps that rate ([Typical Games wiki](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Minigames)).
- **What they can do to each other.** Ink Game: push others in Red Light Green Light with the Super Push gamepass ([wiki: RLGL](https://ink-game.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Light,_Green_Light)), knife hiders, vote for Rebellion. Huss Valley: catch a player and turn them into a chaser.
- **Trading.** Epic Minigames: trading since 2 Feb 2024, Shop Stands since 11 Apr 2025.
- **What can be lost.** One round. No source shows items lost on defeat.
- **The clipped moment.** **Our read:** the O/X Rebellion vote, the last two players in a final, a Huss Valley crowd swallowed by chasers.

## Live ops

- **Epic Minigames**: last updated 20 Nov 2026 (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). An in-lobby Update Board shows the last 10 or 11 updates. Each year since 2022 has added one system: Epic Pass (2022), choice subscriptions (2023), trading and Play Rewards (2024), Shop Stands (2025) ([Typical Games wiki](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Minigames)). Update day of week: not known.
- **Ink Game**: updated 22 Nov 2026. Reported updates include playable guards with three-tier progression (Sept 2025), Mythic and Legendary powers, and a Pentathlon game ([Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/7008097940/ink-game)).
- **Build the Pyramid!**: last updated 25 Oct 2026, a month before this snapshot, with 588 players now.

Epic Minigames' three failed experiments, each with its run:

| Experiment | Ran | Days (our arithmetic) | What it tried | Why it ended |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Massive servers, 72 players | 2 to 16 May 2020 | 14 | Six times the normal server | not stated |
| Voice Chat servers | 22 Jan to 12 Mar 2022 | 49 | Voice in a separate server type | Unpopular |
| Choice Rains | 28 Jul to 21 Oct 2023 | 85 | Paid coin and choice drops on the lobby | Drops stuck on the lobby roof |

Source: [Typical Games wiki](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Minigames).

**Our read:** all three tried to change the lobby or the server, and all three went back to 12 players and a plain lobby. A 72-player server makes a Solo Winner game a 1-in-72 chance; voice splits the player base into a second queue; a physical drop in the lobby needs the lobby to be built for it. Test changes to the server shape for 2 to 7 weeks, as Typical Games did, and be ready to remove them.

**Our read:** a small team can sustain new map variants faster than new rules. Epic Minigames gives many games 2 to 4 map variants ("original, canyon"), so one ruleset fills several playlist slots ([wiki: Free-For-All](https://epic-minigames.fandom.com/wiki/Free-For-All)).

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Concurrent players fall to about 1% of peak within 14 months | A licensed or TV trend carried the launch; games did not change fast enough | Ink Game: reported 514K (Sept 2025, one tracker with conflicting numbers) to 5,841 (Nov 2026). The cause is not in the sources. **Our read:** plan new games before the trend ends |
| An older generic copy outlives the trend leader | Players stayed with the format, not the brand | Squid Game X (2021) has 17,558 now against Ink Game's 5,841 (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026) |
| Copycats appear within weeks | One-rule games are cheap to copy | Huss Valley copies already include Huss Tackle (about 1,038), Huss City [HEIST] (1,138) and Huss the Bulls! (2,782) ([omni-search](https://apis.roblox.com/search-api/omni-search?searchQuery=Huss+Valley&sessionId=abc&pageType=all)) |
| Big server, low player count, no update in a month | A shared goal with nothing new after the goal is reached | Build the Pyramid!: 100-player servers, 588 playing, last update 25 Oct 2026. A reported early figure of about 27K could not be verified |
| A server feature is removed within weeks | It changed the server shape or the lobby | Epic Minigames' three experiments (Live ops) |
| Old round game holds thousands but at a fraction of a spike | A one-off revival spike on an old game | Natural Disaster Survival about 7,000 now against a 2025 peak that trackers put between 78K and 129K ([Rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/65241/natural-disaster-survival), [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/189707)) |

## Checklist

1. A new player is in a round, or sees a countdown under 30 seconds, within 30 seconds of joining.
2. Each round's rule fits in a name of 1 to 3 words.
3. Rounds last 60 to 180 seconds.
4. A dead player is back in the lobby with something to touch within 5 seconds.
5. Server size matches the shape: 12 for a collection, about 30 for a playground, 80 to 100 for an elimination show.
6. An average player wins at least 1 round in 12.
7. At least one timer pays in the first 5 minutes.
8. Timer and mission rewards pay at least 5 times what wins pay an average player per session.
9. A daily reset with 3 missions at a fixed UTC time.
10. A season or pass on fixed calendar dates, about 8 weeks long.
11. Each ruleset has 2 or more map variants.
12. A vote or pick happens between rounds, and its price, if any, scales with server size.
13. Any paid gacha shows exact odds summing to 100% and its top tier is no rarer than 0.1%.
14. Nothing bought with Robux is a reaction to losing a round.

## Twists and open space

Variations that already produced hits:

- **Infection tag instead of elimination.** Huss Valley turns a caught player into a chaser, so nobody waits in a lobby. It reached 311M visits in 86 days (our arithmetic: about 3.6M a day) ([Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=10764627709)).
- **A voted branch in a fixed sequence.** Ink Game's O/X vote and its Tug of War or Hide and Seek slot.
- **A board-game mode beside the playlist.** Epic Minigames' Epic Party, where players race for crystals ([Typical Games wiki](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Minigames)).
- **Small-lobby collections.** "Party Minigames [2-4 Players]", created 15 Apr 2026, has 763 playing ([Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=10039334985)).

**Our read** on open space:

- A collection for 2 to 4 friends; nothing in our research holds this space above 1,000 players.
- An elimination show with its own theme, so it outlasts a TV show's moment.
- A whole-server goal with a weekly reset, so the goal is never "done".
- Host tools for private-server owners, as Epic Minigames sells (Private Server Powers, R$899: free picks, commands, bans; [Typical Games wiki](https://typical-games.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Minigames)) and Natural Disaster Survival sells (Round Control, 300 Robux; [wiki](https://natural-disaster-survival.fandom.com/wiki/Round_Control)).
