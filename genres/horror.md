# Horror

> **The feeling sold:** "Something is coming. I know the sign. I got to the closet in time, and my friend did not."

Five forms are covered: room-based runs ([Doors](../teardowns/doors.md), Pressure, Grace), chapter horror (Piggy, Apeirophobia), mascot horror (Dandy's World), anomaly-spotting ([Animal Hospital](../teardowns/animal-hospital.md)) and one killer against many ([Forsaken](../teardowns/forsaken.md), Flee the Facility). "Now" means 25 November 2026.

## Why people play it

Who can find a horror game is set by its content maturity label ([Roblox Creator docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity.md)):

| Label | Allows | Who can play |
|---|---|---|
| Minimal | Occasional mild violence, light unrealistic blood | Ages 5 to 8 and 9 to 15 by default, and older |
| Mild | Repeated mild violence, heavy unrealistic blood, mild fear | Same |
| Moderate | Light realistic blood, moderate fear | 9 to 15 and 16+; ages 5 to 8 lose access |
| Content May Vary | Varies | Age-checked 16+ |
| Restricted | Strong violence, heavy realistic blood | Age-verified 18+; blocked in Korea, Saudi Arabia, Türkiye |

Roblox's "mild fear" examples are heavy breathing, a pounding heart, screams, creepy-looking NPCs and jump scares. Off-screen violence counts as mild. Realistic blood, even once, moves a game up. A DevForum post, seen only as a search summary, says fear content also needs a Fear descriptor ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/upcoming-changes-to-experience-guidelines/3070343)). Animal Hospital is rated Minimal ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78741-animal-hospital-becomes-roblox-s-latest-horror-sensation-here-s-everything-you-need-to-know)); the Roblox fan wiki lists Pressure as Moderate ([Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Pressure)). Other leaders' labels were not checked.

**Our read:** every beat horror needs is on the mild list. Moderate gives up ages 5 to 8 for realistic blood, which adds no new beat. Design to Mild.

Why children choose fear: no study in our research. **Our read:** the scare is short, the loss is a run and never an item, and the story is told to friends. 40% of Doors joiners hold the badge for a run with a friend (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).

Dandy's World floors: two elevators, Machines to repair and Twisteds that roam. The default machine share falls from 100% on Floor 1 to 70% on Floors 2 to 4, 55% on 5 to 9 and 40% after. A skill check rolls every second at a Machine; a miss alerts nearby Twisteds. Pressure keeps forced locker exits off for the first 5 doors ([wiki](https://pressure.fandom.com/wiki/Hiding)). Animal Hospital adds its CCTV check after shift 1.

| By | This has happened | Source or reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 0:05 | In a party queue or a first room, not a menu | Doors' 5 to 30 s countdown |
| 0:30 | One item bought or picked up | Doors' 30 s shop |
| 1:00 | A tell has played with nothing behind it | Doors fakes Rush's flicker |
| 3:00 | The first real threat passed and was answered | Door 3 to room 10. **Our read:** the 3:00 is ours |

## Numbers that work

### One tell, one counter, one punishment (Doors)

From each entity's wiki page, read 25 Nov 2026:

| Threat | Tell | Counter | Damage | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Rush](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Rush) | Flicker, then a rumble that becomes a screech; heard about 2 rooms out | Hide, or leave its line of sight | 125 | From Door 3, about every 10 doors. Never on Doors 1, 2, 48, 49, 51, 52, 59, 62, 87, 90, 99, in a room with no hiding spot, or in the 2 rooms after a Seek chase |
| [Ambush](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Ambush) | Distorted scream; next room's lights break | Hide, step out and re-hide between passes | 100 | Doors 3 to 86, likelier at 82 to 86; returns at most 3 times |
| [Seek](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Seek) | 2 to 5 rooms of eyes on the walls | Run, jump, avoid hands | Death | Every run: a 6-room chase in rooms 28 to 46, an 8-room chase in 66 to 85 |
| [Figure](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Figure) | Footsteps; it is blind | Crouch-walk; heartbeat minigame in a closet | Death | Doors 50 and 100 |
| [Halt](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Halt) | Blue room, "TURN AROUND" prompts | Obey prompts | 60 | Almost every run |
| [Screech](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Screech) | "Psst" in a dark room | Look at it | 40 | Dark rooms |
| [Eyes](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Eyes) | Whispers, a glowing eye | Do not look | 3 to 10 per tick | 3% per room |
| [Dupe](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Dupe) | Up to 8 or 9 fake doors | Pick the real one | 40 | Varies |
| [Hide](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Hide) | Red pulse after about 12 s in a closet | Get out | 40 and a 10 s lockout | Any closet |

Doors' base health is not stated; heals restore 100 HP ([OP.GG items](https://op.gg/roblox/wiki/doors/items)) and Pressure uses 100 ([wiki](https://pressure.fandom.com/wiki/Health)). The Mines bring Screech, Giggle and Gloombats in at Doors 101 to 105, three new threats in five rooms ([Giggle](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Giggle), [Gloombat](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Gloombat)).

**Our read:** damage follows warning length. Long warnings kill (Seek's 2 to 5 rooms, Figure's footsteps, Rush's two-room sound). Short warnings take 40. Rush may only spawn where a closet exists. So every death followed a fair signal, and the death screen can blame the player in one line. Pressure keeps the shape with harsher values: its common monsters kill on sight, and the hard one, Pinkie, has no flicker at all ([wiki](https://pressure.fandom.com/wiki/Pinkie)).

### Warning time: not published

No wiki gives the seconds between a tell and the hit, for any game here. Nearest: Rush is heard about two rooms out at a wiki speed of 75 studs per second marked "citation needed". Grace's Carnation appears 11 rooms behind and must be hidden from within 3 to 5 rooms; Grace's Sorrow brings a red sky for about 5 to 6 s, and the player must take cover ([Carnation](https://grace-rbx.fandom.com/wiki/Carnation), [Sorrow](https://grace-rbx.fandom.com/wiki/Sorrow)). Pressure's Squiddles attack after 1.5 s of light ([wiki](https://pressure.fandom.com/wiki/Squiddles)).

Share of the 536.7 million "Welcome" holders (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026; badge meanings from the wiki):

| Badge | Means | Holders | Share of joiners | Of row above (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Joined | 536,732,850 | 100% | |
| Out Of My Way | Survived Rush | 293,357,345 | 55% | 55% |
| You Can Run | Survived Seek | 101,553,724 | 19% | 35% |
| Sshh! | Survived Figure | 50,987,967 | 9.5% | 50% |
| Rock Bottom (both versions) | Finished the Hotel | about 14.5M | 2.7% | 28% |

**Our read:** a badge cannot tell "died" from "left", and the counts are all-time, so treat each drop as both. The biggest loss is between Rush and the first Seek chase in rooms 28 to 46: two of three Rush survivors never clear it. One joiner in 37 finishes the first floor. Doors puts its difficulty late and keeps the start easy enough that half of all joiners beat the first threat. A new game without Doors' name needs a higher first-threat pass rate than 55%, never lower.

### Pacing theory from a non-Roblox game

Alien: Isolation (console and PC) splits the monster in two ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-perfect-organism-the-ai-of-alien-isolation)). A director always knows where the player is and sends the alien hints. The alien only senses, through a behaviour tree of over 100 nodes, and cannot cheat. A menace gauge rises with closeness and line of sight; when it sits at its peak too long, the alien withdraws into the vents. New behaviours unlock with progress, not with deaths. Some areas are never searched, so no ambush there is unfair. The alien teleports twice in a 12 to 18 hour campaign. Left 4 Dead's director works similarly, but its timings could not be verified.

**Our read:** Doors' two empty rooms after Seek are the same rule as the alien going backstage: after a peak, a quiet stretch the player can count on.

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** A failed Doors run still pays 1 Knob per door, so a death at Door 40 plus a short second run buys a 50-Knob Lighter. The next elevator leaves within 30 seconds.

**Why horror is replayed after the rules are known.** The rules stay fixed; the timing does not.

- Spawns are random and some tells lie: Rush comes "around every 10 doors", and lights sometimes flicker with nothing behind them.
- Knowing a counter is not doing it in time: 2.7% of Doors joiners have finished the Hotel.
- The rules grow: 71 Doors entities, and 80 modifiers worth +5% to +10% Knobs each after the first Hotel finish ([wiki](https://doors-game.fandom.com/wiki/Modifiers)).
- Trust breaks on purpose: Animal Hospital's static photo stops being a sure sign after shift 10, and a patient admitted by mistake comes back as a roaming hazard ([OP.GG](https://op.gg/roblox/wiki/animal-hospital)).
- Friends change the run: +0.1x Knobs per friend up to +0.3x in Doors; +5% Kroner per friend in Pressure ([wiki](https://pressure.fandom.com/wiki/Currency)).

**Our read:** the question moves from "what is that?" to "will I react in time?" and "will my friend?". The first is used up in one run; the second is asked every run.

**Tomorrow.** Doors' Daily Runs (since 2 Jan 2026) and daily skin deals. Animal Hospital ran double-coin weekends, per a fan blog ([animalhospital.blog](https://animalhospital.blog/release-date/)).

**Next week.** Soft-currency unlocks: Forsaken killers at 1,000 to 2,000 points against 25 per survivor win ([wiki](https://forsaken2024.fandom.com/wiki/Player_Points)); Dandy's Toons at 100 to 5,000 Ichor against about 32 per floor (our arithmetic from [Ichor](https://dandys-world-robloxhorror.fandom.com/wiki/Ichor)); 99 Nights classes at 10 to 600 Diamonds ([OP.GG](https://op.gg/roblox/wiki/99-nights-in-the-forest)). **Our read:** first unlock in 1 to 3 sessions, top ones after tens of hours.

## Other players

**Our read:** small servers (5 to 9) are for games where players act on each other, like Forsaken's killer and survivors. Big servers (25 to 50) are lobbies; the run is a party of 1 to 4.

Helping: a Doors player can pull Giggle off a friend's face, and a party has 2 minutes to revive. A dead Pressure player with the 199 R$ Post-Mortem Haunting pass can speed a living one for 3 s ([wiki](https://pressure.fandom.com/wiki/Gamepasses)). Animal Hospital's ten classes split co-op work. Pressure allows one player per hiding spot.

Loss: only the run. No leader in our research takes items on death.

**Our read** on the clip: the friend who did not reach the closet, filmed from inside it.

Revives: Doors sells one on the death screen for 40 R$, or 19 to 29 each in packs (our arithmetic). The window is 5 minutes solo, 2 in a party. It is blocked while Rush, Ambush or Seek is active and in the finale rooms, and it resets the run's door reward. Pressure sells revives in the lobby at a price not found; using one resets doors passed. Forsaken and Dandy's World sell none in the pages read. Range: 19 to 40 R$.

**Our read:** the death screen is a moment of loss. Doors softens its offer with limits (none mid-threat, none at the finale, the reward resets). Copy the limits, and never open the offer by itself. See [foundations/15-the-line.md](../foundations/15-the-line.md) and [foundations/12-monetization.md](../foundations/12-monetization.md). No leader sells weapons or health as a pass.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| A clone sits at 200 to 2,500 players | Desk check copied and reskinned, no second verb. Anomaly Cafe 1.3K, Bake or Die 2.5K, Night 67 204 ([RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/games-like/scary-shawarma-kiosk-the-anomaly/), date not shown) | A second layer, like Animal Hospital's hazards and surgery |
| 1% to 2% of peak within 5 months | Viral spike, no long goal (Animal Hospital) | An unlock ladder of 10 to 30 hours |
| Under half of joiners earn the first-threat badge | First threat too early, too hard or unsignalled | Two-channel tell, closet in the room, room 3 or later |
| Big loss at the first chase | Chase beyond new players' skill (Doors loses two in three there) | Test the chase on new players |
| Players hide through everything | Hiding is free | 12 s hiding limit |
| Slow fall for a year | No new floors (Pressure's list stops in March 2025) | One new floor or mode a year |
| The game stops | The team stops (Grace) | |

## Checklist

1. First real threat between room 3 and 10, about 3 minutes in.
2. Each threat has one tell, one counter and one fixed damage number, written down before building.
3. Lethal threats warn on two channels.
4. A lethal hide-threat never spawns where there is no hiding spot.
5. Hiding is limited to about 12 s.
6. Two quiet rooms after every chase.
7. A checkpoint or set piece every 20 to 40 rooms.
8. The death screen names the counter in one line.
9. A failed run pays enough soft currency for one item by mid-floor.
10. 55% or more of joiners earn the first-threat badge.
11. The next run starts within 30 s of death.
12. Scares stay on Roblox's mild-fear list, with no realistic blood.
13. Revives, if sold, cost 19 to 40 R$, are blocked mid-threat and never open by themselves.
14. Three new threats are planned for the first three updates.

## Twists and open space

Hits that changed the form:

- **The desk check.** Animal Hospital turned spot-the-difference into a shift with three checks and roaming hazards, and won four 2026 awards including Best New Game ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/2026-roblox-innovation-awards)).
- **Horror plus survival.** 99 Nights; see [genres/survival.md](survival.md). **Chapters to open world.** Piggy's Intercity won Best RPG 2026 ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/79176-full-list-of-winners-at-the-roblox-innovation-awards-2026)).

**Our read** on open space:

- A threat director with a stated quiet rule, on the Alien: Isolation model, in 4-to-8-player co-op. No Roblox leader in our research documents one.
- An anomaly game whose mistakes return later, set somewhere other than a counter. Every clone listed kept the counter.
- Mascot horror where the cast is earned by surviving, first character inside 2 sessions.
