# Roleplay and life simulation

> **The feeling sold:** "I get a house, a car and a job right away, and my friends and I play pretend in a real-looking town."

Town and city roleplay (Brookhaven, Berry Avenue, Livetopia, LifeTogether), goal-driven life sims (Welcome to Bloxburg) and serious team roleplay (Emergency Response: Liberty County). Pet-raising games with a town around them, such as Adopt Me!, are on [pet-raising-and-animal.md](pet-raising-and-animal.md).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|

| Study these first | [Brookhaven RP](../teardowns/brookhaven.md): the free-toolkit model at the largest scale. Welcome to Bloxburg: the goal-driven model with jobs, moods and building. Emergency Response: Liberty County: teams, a rank ladder and paid private servers. San Diego Roleplay: a 2026 entrant that reached 15K players in under nine months. |
| Session that works | Not known. No tracker session length was found for any roleplay game. |
| Players per server | 12 to 35 in the town games (Bloxburg 12, Livetopia 17, Brookhaven 30, Berry Avenue 31); 50 to 60 in team games (Maple Hospital 50, San Diego 50, Liberty County 60) (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026) |
| Who owns the genre | Old games hold the top. Brookhaven (created April 2020) had 332,494 players live, 11 times the next roleplay game, LifeTogether (29,977, created July 2023). Themed newcomers still get in: San Diego Roleplay 15,409 at about 8.5 months old, SweetPit City 5,307 at about 7.5 months, Highschool Experience 2,404 at 5 months (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). |
| Build cost | Not known from sources. **Our read:** the cost is content volume. Brookhaven ships 1 to 2 houses, 1 to 3 vehicles and 5 to 12 props every week. |
| Money comes from | Passes that unlock more of a free thing (more plots, estates, vehicle packs, VIP), build-limit steps sold as products, and private-server ownership in team games |
| What kills clones | A town with too few houses, cars and roles to fill a scene, no weekly drop, and nothing a newcomer cannot already get in Brookhaven for free. |

## Why people play it

The leaders give everything away at the start. Brookhaven has no currency, no quests and no tutorial; the sidebar menu is the onboarding ([RoWatcher explainer](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game)). Berry Avenue has no combat, no grinding and no money system ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/berry-avenue-rp)).

The audience is young. RoWatcher puts Brookhaven's target audience at ages 9 to 12, and Common Sense Media rates it 8+ ([RoWatcher explainer](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game)). An analyst piece puts the genre audience at 7 to 16 and calls friends the social core of it; that is analyst opinion, not Roblox data ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/roleplay-games-rule-roblox-adopt-me-and-brookhaven-command-900k-players)).

**Our read:** the game is a stage and a prop box. The player brings the story. That is why the content counts matter more than any loop: a scene needs a house, a car, a role icon and an outfit, and the player has to find all four in under a minute.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Leader: [Brookhaven RP](../teardowns/brookhaven.md). No timed playthrough exists, so seconds are not known.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Spawn | Lands in mid-town | not known | Sidebar icons: Houses, Vehicles, Jobs, Avatar Editor ([PixelTwelve](https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/brookhaven-beginner-guide-houses-cars-jobs)) | none |
| 2. House | Picks a house, spawns it on a plot marked Vacant, saves it by hand | not known | The house appears on the plot | 1 of 15 free standard plots taken ([wiki: House Guide](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/House_Guide)) |
| 3. Car | Picks a vehicle from the menu | not known | It spawns beside the player | at least 82 free street vehicles to choose from ([wiki: Vehicles/Street](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicles/Street)) |
| 4. Role | Equips a job | not known | An icon above the head; matching clothing racks at the workplace | 80 of 81 roles free; no wages ([wiki: Jobs](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Jobs)) |
| 5. Scene | Drives to one of about 40 named buildings and acts with others | open-ended | Other players' role icons, cars and houses | none |

There is no number that goes up. The loop ends when the scene ends, and the next one starts from the same menu.

**How Bloxburg differs.** Bloxburg is goal-driven. The player works one of 14 jobs, earns Money, and spends it on a house and furniture. Pay per minute depends on total work experience (up to level 100), efficiency and mood; at level 100 and about 70% efficiency it is roughly $79 a minute, $119 with the Excellent Employee pass ([wiki: Work](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.fandom.com/wiki/Work)). The cheapest furnished prebuilt house costs $7,500 ([wiki: Prebuilt Houses](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.fandom.com/wiki/Prebuilt_Houses/After_2024)). Our arithmetic: 7,500 / 79 = about 95 minutes of work at the maximum level. A new player's starting money and starting pay are not known, so the time to a first house for a new player is not known. A 2024 report gave $2,625 a minute at maximum ([404 Media](https://www.404media.co/roblox-community-protests-communism-update)); the wiki does not support it, and the two are unreconciled.

**How Liberty County differs.** Players join a team and earn XP per action toward ranks that unlock gear (numbers below).

### Loop stack (Brookhaven)

| Every | What closes |
|---|---|
| Few seconds | A car or house spawns from the menu |
| One scene | A story with other players ends; no number records it |
| Not known | Any timer inside a session (none found) |
| One week | The Friday update, 6 pm EST: new house, vehicles, props ([wiki: Changelog](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Changelog)) |
| 2 to 3 weeks | A new pass or paid bundle (our count from the changelog) |
| About 2 weeks | A seasonal event; Halloween ran 23 Oct to 6 Nov 2026 ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp)) |

**Our read:** Brookhaven's stack is empty below the week. Nothing finishes every 5 minutes or every day. The players' own scenes fill that space. A game with fewer players per scene, or fewer props per scene, has nothing to fall back on.

### Bloxburg's goal model

| Rule | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pay at total level 100 | about $79 a minute at 70% efficiency; $119 with Excellent Employee (x1.5) | [wiki: Work](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.fandom.com/wiki/Work) |
| Efficiency | 70% task speed + 30% mood | [wiki: Mood](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.fandom.com/wiki/Mood) |
| Mood pay bands | lowest mood above 80%: 100% pay; all above 50%: 85%; any below 51%: 70% | same |

| Plot | 30x30; Large Plot pass 50x50 (2.8 times the area, our arithmetic); up to 5 floors with a pass | same |

### Liberty County's rank ladder and private servers

Teams: police, sheriff, fire, transport, civilian, criminal. XP per action: arrest +25, patrol +10. Police ranks by XP ([wiki: XP System](https://emergency-response-liberty-county.fandom.com/wiki/XP_System)): Cadet 0, Officer 500, Corporal 1,200, Sergeant 2,250, Lieutenant 4,000, Captain 6,500, Major 9,000, Colonel 13,500, Commander 20,000. Each step is 1.4 to 2.4 times the last (our arithmetic). Officer unlocks a Taser, Sergeant two rifles, Colonel a drone. At 25 XP per arrest, Commander takes at least 800 arrests.

Private servers ([wiki: Private Server](https://emergency-response-liberty-county.fandom.com/wiki/Private_Server), prices may be stale): 50 R$ once to create, 185 R$ to make it premium, packs at 500 to 650 R$ (Map Editor, API Pack, Custom Livery). Owners set rules, uniforms, banned vehicles and weapons, weather and time of day. Cash, XP and purchases are not saved there.

**Start with:**

|---|---|---|

| Houses at launch | 20 | About one fifth of Brookhaven's 107 to 115, which took years of weekly drops |
| Vehicles | 45 | Brookhaven has about 2.2 vehicles per house (234 / 107, Bloxodes counts) |
| Roles | 20, all free but one | Brookhaven: 80 of 81 free |
| Named buildings | 10 to 15 | Brookhaven has about 40; aim for 2 roles per building as there (81 / 40) |

| Build-limit product | +10 props for 80 R$, cap at 3 steps | Brookhaven's public-server step |
| Weekly drop | 1 house, 2 vehicles, 6 props, every Friday | Brookhaven's typical entry, at the low end |

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** No in-session timer was found in Brookhaven or Berry Avenue. Bloxburg's mood gauges fall while you work and cut pay to 85% or 70%, so the player stops to eat, sleep and wash ([wiki: Mood](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.fandom.com/wiki/Mood)). Liberty County pays XP per action with 1.5x and 2x boosts ([wiki: XP System](https://emergency-response-liberty-county.fandom.com/wiki/XP_System)).

**Tomorrow.** Bloxburg's 5-day reward ladder ($100 to $1,000, then B$20) with trophies at 7, 14 and 30 days ($10,000, $30,000, $75,000) ([wiki: Daily Reward](https://welcome-to-bloxburg.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Reward)). Brookhaven had no daily reward in any source. Redeemable codes: Berry Avenue 279 active, Brookhaven 70 ([Bloxodes Berry](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/berry-avenue-rp), [Bloxodes Brookhaven](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp)).

**Next week and after.** The Friday update. Brookhaven's changelog lists 20 weekly entries from 19 June to 6 November 2026 ([wiki: Changelog](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Changelog)). Retention rates (D1, D7, D28) for any roleplay game: not known.

## Other players

**Server size.** 12 (Bloxburg) to 35, and 50 to 75 where teams or creatures organise play (see the table above). Our arithmetic: Brookhaven's 332,494 players at 30 a server is about 11,000 parallel towns.

**What a player sees.** Role icons over heads, other players' houses on numbered plots, their cars on the road. Almost every Brookhaven house has a hidden wall safe for crime scenes ([wiki: House Guide](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/House_Guide)).

**What they can do to each other.** In Brookhaven, act; nothing can be taken. In Liberty County, arrest and rob: criminals carry a wanted level of 1 to 5 ([Guide](https://emergency-response-liberty-county.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Response:_Liberty_County_(Guide)), self-flagged outdated, about 2022).

**What can be lost.** In Brookhaven, a house that was not saved by hand. Builds over the public prop cap do not load in public servers.

**The clip.** Not measured. **Our read:** the house reveal, a disaster (13 weather and disaster types per [Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/brookhaven-rp)) and secret rooms (about 10 documented on the [wiki](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Secrets_and_Easter_Eggs)).

Safety: Brookhaven's chat features drew scrutiny for inappropriate content and dating behaviour ([RoWatcher explainer](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game)). Its March 2026 update expanded Quick Chat ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/roleplay-games-rule-roblox-adopt-me-and-brookhaven-command-900k-players)). Roblox's 2026 chat and age rules were not retrieved; see [foundations/15-the-line.md](../foundations/15-the-line.md) and [foundations/09-other-players.md](../foundations/09-other-players.md).

## Live ops

Brookhaven updates every Friday at 6 pm EST, weekly since Voldex bought it (announced 4 Feb 2025) ([wiki: Changelog](https://brookhaven.fandom.com/wiki/Changelog), [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/1686885941/brookhaven-rp)). The changelog lists 20 weekly entries from 19 June to 6 November 2026. Two examples: 6 Nov added 1 premium mansion, 1 free house and 5 props; 31 Jul added 9 pets, 6 tools, 11 props, 3 locations, a House Pets pass and the higher prop-limit purchase. The [teardown](../teardowns/brookhaven.md) has more. Our count from the list: a typical week adds 1 to 2 houses, 1 to 3 vehicles, 5 to 12 props and 1 to 6 tools; a location or system changes every second week; a pass or paid bundle every 2 to 3 weeks; a film or brand collaboration every 2 to 4 weeks. The list as read has no entry for 26 June. Visits rose from about 60B to about 80B in just over a year after the sale ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/roleplay-games-rule-roblox-adopt-me-and-brookhaven-command-900k-players)).

Every active leader showed an update within 22 days of 25 Nov 2026 (Roblox public API). What happens to a roleplay game when updates stop: not known from sources. **Our read:** a small team can sustain one house, two vehicles and six props a week; plan 4 weeks of drops before launch. See [foundations/13-live-ops.md](../foundations/13-live-ops.md).

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Players fall while the game still updates | The goal model stops scaling: Bloxburg 281K peak (June 2025) to 9,545 at the same moment Brookhaven had 332,494 | Make the first house free or reachable in 30 minutes; keep jobs optional |
| Reviews call it aimless after a few days | Nothing new to act with; Brookhaven draws this criticism ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/what-is-brookhaven-rp-why-400k-players-choose-roblox-s-biggest-game)) | A weekly drop with at least one house and one location change every 2 weeks |
| Players protest an economy change | Taking away a reward players earned; Bloxburg's equal-pay update in 2024 | Raise the low end; do not cut the top |
| Trackers disagree and counts sit in low thousands | A long list of seasonal passes on a 17-player server; Livetopia has 27 passes and 4,294 players (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026) | Fewer, cheaper passes; one tier at 35 to 99 R$ |
| Reports and press about chat | Free-form chat in a young audience; Brookhaven's scrutiny | Quick Chat presets, as Brookhaven expanded in March 2026 |
| Newcomers never arrive | A plain town copy; LifeTogether had 10 to 25 times fewer players than leaders in an older count ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/roleplay-games-rule-roblox-adopt-me-and-brookhaven-command-900k-players)) | Pick a theme Brookhaven lacks (a real city, a school, a hospital) |

## Checklist

1. A new player owns a house without paying within 30 seconds of gaining control.
2. A car spawns beside the player from a menu in one tap.
3. At least 95% of roles are free.
4. At least 70% of standard plots are free.
5. At least 20 houses, 45 vehicles and 20 roles at launch.
6. Players per server is between 17 and 35, unless teams organise play.
7. No pass gives power over another player.
8. Pass prices form three tiers: one item under 100 R$, most at 180 to 399, a few at 599 to 799.
9. A build-limit step costs 100 R$ or less and has a cap shown on screen.
10. An update ships on the same weekday every week, with at least one house.
11. Four weeks of drops are built before launch.
12. Quick Chat or preset lines exist for players without free chat.
13. If the game has jobs: the first house is reachable in 30 minutes or less, and mood penalties never cut pay below 70%.
14. If the game has a rank ladder: each rank costs 1.4 to 2.5 times the last, and each unlocks something visible.

## Twists and open space

Variations that have already worked, each with players on 25 Nov 2026 (Roblox public API):

- **A real city.** San Diego Roleplay (15,409, created March 2026), Dubai RP [Pets] (14,182).
- **A single building.** Maple Hospital (6,490, 50 per server, Director Pack 799 R$), Twilight Daycare (2,199, 25 passes from 29 R$).
- **A school.** Highschool Experience (2,404 at 5 months old, 50 per server).
- **Emergency services with ranks.** Liberty County (2,573 live, 1.62B visits); a paid game until 25 June 2021 ([Guide](https://emergency-response-liberty-county.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Response:_Liberty_County_(Guide))).
- **A global theme.** Berry Avenue's French, Greek, Swiss and Korean districts and an airport with flights ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/berry-avenue-rp)).

**Our read** on open space:

- A town that saves player builds between sessions. Brookhaven's world saving is not available in public servers, and public builds cap at 45 to 50 props.
- A town with one light clock: a 5-minute event in the square (a fire, a parade) that gives every role something to do. Brookhaven's stack has no row between a scene and a week.
- Team roleplay for younger players. The ranked team games run at 50 to 60 per server with weapons; nothing found offers ranks without combat.
