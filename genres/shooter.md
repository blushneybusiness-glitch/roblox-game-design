# Shooters and arena PvP

> **The feeling sold:** "I beat a real person in a fair fight, and everybody in the lobby saw it."

This page covers gun games ([RIVALS](../teardowns/rivals.md), Arsenal, Phantom Forces) and round-based PvP arenas where players knock each other out ([BedWars](../teardowns/bedwars.md), Blade Ball). Melee and anime combat games are in [fighting.md](fighting.md).

## Why people play it

The sources do not give ages or devices. They do show what the games pay out: status that other players can see. RIVALS gives every placed ranked player a rank charm and gives the top 200 a Mythical Arch skin ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/roblox-rivals-ranked)). Arsenal prints a streak tag at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 kills, ending at "SERVER SWEAT" ([wiki](https://robloxarsenal.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay_Mechanics)). BedWars gives a kit skin per ranked division from Gold up and an emote to the top 200 ([wiki](https://robloxbedwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ranked)).

**Our read:** RIVALS halves ranked aim assist and caps it at 0.5 instead of removing it ([Miraheze](https://robloxrivals.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranked)), which suggests phone and controller players matter to it. The need is a clean test of skill with a quick answer: 5 to 9 rounds in a RIVALS duel, one missed 500 ms window in Blade Ball, then straight into the next one.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

The leader is RIVALS. Round length in seconds is not published in any source found, so the seconds column is mostly empty.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Queue | Presses Play, or steps on a Duel Zone pad in the lobby | Not known | Lobby of up to 40 players with pads on the floor | Queue count |
| Map | Votes on a map; in ranked each team bans one map and two weapons, and the vote narrows three maps to one | Not known | Map cards | None |
| Round | Fights until one side is eliminated | Not known | 100 health per player; a sniper hits for 50 body, 150 head | Health, round score |
| Round timeout | If the timer runs out, the side with more combined health takes the point; an exact tie goes to a random side | Timer length not known | On the Graveyard map a church bell rings for sudden death | Round score |
| Match | Repeats rounds until one side has 5 wins (5 to 9 rounds) | Not known | Win screen | Duel won, task progress, pass progress |
| Rewards | Daily task, contract and pass progress | | Keys, charms, wraps | 1 to 3 Keys per task; 1 pass tier per 25 round wins |

Sources: [Bloxguides](https://bloxguidesgg.com/games/rivals), [RIVALS wiki: Game Hints](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Hints), [RIVALS wiki: Gamemodes](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Gamemodes), [Miraheze: Ranked](https://robloxrivals.miraheze.org/wiki/Ranked). A player may swap weapons at most 2 times per duel.

How the others differ:

- **BedWars** puts an economy inside the match: 1 iron a second from your base generator, a shop, a bed to guard. Once your bed breaks, death is final. Deathmatch starts at 20:00 (Squads). See the [teardown](../teardowns/bedwars.md).
- **Blade Ball** is one free-for-all round of up to 12. The ball starts at speed 0 and turns red for its target. A parry inside 500 ms sends it on a little faster; a miss starts a 2-second cooldown. At 2 players left, a 1v1 "Standoff" begins ([wiki: Gameplay](https://bladeball.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay), [Gamemodes](https://bladeball.fandom.com/wiki/Gamemodes)).
- **Arsenal** rotates your weapon with each kill and ends on the golden knife: a kill with it wins the game ([wiki](https://robloxarsenal.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Knife)). **Phantom Forces** runs 15-minute team matches (table below).

## The first five minutes

Known: RIVALS has a Beginner 2v2 queue for players below a win threshold, on one map (Arena), where enemies show through walls for a moment at round start. One-off Beginner Tasks at a Shooting Range pay 18 Keys in total ([Gamemodes](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Gamemodes), [Tasks](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Tasks)).

Not known: seconds from joining to the first fight, for any game in this genre. No wiki had a beginner's guide. No source mentions bots for new players. The table is therefore a set of gates for a new game.

|---|---|---|

| 0:30 | The player is in a match against other new players | **Our read:** RIVALS keeps beginners in a separate 2v2 queue with a win threshold |

| 5:00 | Second match started; the player knows one thing to unlock with their currency | RIVALS: the cheapest bought gun (Uzi) costs 20 Keys, 2 more than the 18 beginner-task Keys |

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** The next round starts at once. RIVALS contracts pay a Charm Capsule after 5 minutes with a weapon and a wrap at 20 ([Contracts](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Contracts)). BedWars' emerald generators speed up every 5 minutes, from one per 69 s to one per 24 s after 20:00 ([wiki, marked outdated](https://robloxbedwars.fandom.com/wiki/Emerald_Generator)).

**Tomorrow.** RIVALS daily tasks pay 6 Keys and bonus tasks 6 more; the free ELO Shield returns. BedWars multiplies the first 5,000 XP of the day by 2.5 ([Player Level](https://robloxbedwars.fandom.com/wiki/Player_Level)). Blade Ball allows 3 coin wheel spins a day at 3,000 Coins plus one discounted spin at 49 R$ ([Wheel](https://bladeball.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel)).

**Next week and after.** RIVALS reopens 7 pass tiers every 7 days. BedWars rotates 3 free kits every Friday. Ranked seasons reset: BedWars Seasons 14 and 15 ran about 19 and 15 weeks; RIVALS Season 0 about 7 months ([Ranked Seasons](https://robloxrivals.fandom.com/wiki/Ranked_Seasons)). RIVALS season Glory = 1,250 x (ranked wins / 250) + 1,250 x (final ELO / 3,600), capped at 2,500. Our arithmetic: 100 wins at 2,000 ELO earns about 1,194, so volume and skill count about equally.

## Other players

**Server size.** Lobbies of 16 to 40, fights of 2 to 16. RIVALS puts 40 in a lobby and splits them into duels: a busy lobby, small fights.

**What they can do to each other.** Eliminate, and in BedWars break a bed (20 health, 2 to 6 hits by tool tier). A team alive 60 s after losing its bed is highlighted for the rest of the match ([Bed](https://robloxbedwars.fandom.com/wiki/Bed)).

**What can be lost, and the protection.** Ranked points. RIVALS limits parties to a 400-ELO window (800 in Season 3) and makes leaving ranked a loss plus a 15-minute lockout. BedWars blocks Gold players from queueing above Platinum. Cheating: Arsenal reports about 40,000 exploiters banned a month ([wiki](https://robloxarsenal.fandom.com/wiki/Arsenal:_Reloaded)); FRONTLINES relies on /votekick (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).

**Devices.** RIVALS ranked halves aim assist, caps it at 0.5, locks Auto Shoot to Dynamic and turns off third person. A low-quality source reports that mobile aim assist breaks after some updates ([marix.app](https://marix.app/library/games/rivals-mobile-aim-assist-broken)). Input-based matchmaking (phones against phones): not found for any game.

**The moment that gets clipped.** **Our read**, from what the games put on screen: the Arsenal golden-knife kill that turns the victim into a gold statue; the Blade Ball Standoff when 2 players remain; the RIVALS sudden-death bell; a BedWars bed breaking in the last seconds of plating.

## Live ops

- **RIVALS:** numbered updates about every 6 weeks (dates in the [teardown](../teardowns/rivals.md)). Ranked Season 4 went live with Update 25 on about 14 Nov 2026; one guide gave 13 Nov ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/roblox-rivals-ranked), [RobloxTools](https://www.robloxtools.net/blog/rivals-season-4)).
- **BedWars:** free kits rotate every Friday. Themed battle passes now run about 4 weeks; the wiki lists five from Holiday 2025 to Halloween 2026.
- **Blade Ball, Arsenal, Phantom Forces:** update rhythm not known.

What happens when updates stop: no source measured it. **Our read** from the peaks above: a viral peak 6 to 18 months after launch, then 1% to 5% of peak unless a weekly or seasonal rhythm holds ([foundations/13-live-ops.md](../foundations/13-live-ops.md)). A small team can hold a weekly free rotation (BedWars' 3 kits) and a 4-week pass more easily than 6-weekly content updates.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| CCU at 1% to 5% of peak two or more years later | Arsenal 152,613 to 2,101; BedWars 334,747 to 16,300; Blade Ball 511,924 to 15,811. No developer statements on cause were found. **Our read:** the format aged while the audience moved to short duels | Add a short mode (first to 5, 1v1 to 3v3) before adding guns |
| A recent peak is gone within a year | FRONTLINES peaked at 11,650 in Jan 2026 and has 166 now (down 99% in 10 months); Bad Business fell from 33,739 (Dec 2020) to 114. Cause not known | **Our read:** a spike with nothing to come back for; give a weekly reason (a free rotation, a pass tier cap) before chasing the next spike |
| Exploiters fill servers | Arsenal: about 40,000 bans a month; FRONTLINES hands the job to /votekick | Server checks on hits; account-age and hardware gates before ranked, as RIVALS does |
| Ranked full of smurfs and boosted accounts | No account gate | RIVALS: 30-day account, Level 100. BedWars: 7 days, level 20, rank-pairing limits |
| Phone players quit after the first deaths | Aim assist missing or broken | Full assist in casual, halved in ranked (RIVALS); retest assist after every update |
| Top players stop playing once they peak | Rank can be parked | Decay from the second-highest tier, 10 duels every 3 days (RIVALS) |

## Checklist

1. A new player can be in a match within 30 seconds of joining. (Gate; leader's time not known.)
2. New players meet only new players for their first matches, as in RIVALS' Beginner 2v2.
3. A match lasts 5 to 9 rounds or at most 8 minutes in the casual mode.
4. Every round ends with a winner, with a health or score fallback at the timer.
5. Close-range time to kill is 0.15 to 0.3 s and far-range 0.3 to 0.4 s, or you have a reason it is not.
6. The first unlock is reachable in the first session.
7. A mid-tier unlock is reachable by free play in about 30 days.
8. Ranked is gated by at least 5 days of play or an account age of 7 days or more.
9. Leaving ranked costs a loss and a lockout of about 15 minutes; parties stay inside a skill window (RIVALS: 400 ELO).
10. Ranked aim assist is reduced to about half of casual, not removed.
11. The season pass has a weekly tier cap and a skip price per tier shown up front.
12. Every crate's odds are shown and sum to exactly 100%; duplicates refund about 50%.
13. Something free changes every week (BedWars: 3 kits every Friday).

## Twists and open space

Variations that already produced hits:

- **Short duels in a shared lobby.** RIVALS: duels first to 5 rounds, pads on the lobby floor, 967,342 peak.
- **One button and one window.** Blade Ball: a 500 ms parry, 511,924 peak.
- **A bed as a respawn token.** BedWars: deaths are free until your bed breaks, 334,747 peak.
- **Rotating odd modes.** RIVALS' Juggernaut puts one player with 3,000 health against 7 hunters with 100 ([Bloxodes](https://bloxodes.com/wiki/rivals/gamemodes)).

**Our read** on angles that look under-served:

- **Input-based matchmaking.** No source shows any game here matching phones with phones. A phone-only casual queue is open space.
- **Bots for the first match.** No source mentions them. A first match against bots that lose by a round or two lets a new player win once inside 3 minutes.
