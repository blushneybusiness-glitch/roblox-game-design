# Simulator

> **The feeling sold:** "My pets get stronger, my number gets bigger, and every new area has a better egg."

Four kinds of game share one ladder: pet-collection simulators, clickers and incrementals, training or lifting simulators, and gather-and-convert games like Bee Swarm. Bubble Gum Simulator INFINITY is shortened to BGSI. Player counts are from Roblox's public API on 25 Nov 2026 unless another date is given.

## Why people play it

Who plays (age, device) is not in our sources. Players approve of these games: BGSI 96.8% up-votes, Ride A Pet 96%, Bee Swarm 95.6%, Pet Simulator 99 94.7%, Muscle Legends 88.3% ([votes API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games/votes?universeIds=3317771874,6504986360,4582358979,6443367640,1268927906,10035204815,10563114921,601130232,10766972131), 25 Nov 2026; percentages are our arithmetic).

**Our read:** the genre sells three things at once. A number that always goes up. A collection whose next item is named on screen. A calendar of free gifts. The player is never asked to be good at anything.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Pet Simulator 99. Seconds per step are not published and were not timed.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clicks a breakable (coin pile, chest); equipped pets run to it | not known | Pets swarm the object, coins burst out | Coins |
| 2 | Walks to the egg for the current area and hatches | not known | Egg cracks, pet revealed with its rarity | Pets owned, best pet strength |
| 3 | Equips the strongest pets | not known | Pets follow the player | Damage per second on breakables |
| 4 | Buys the next area | not known | A gate opens to a new biome with a new egg | Area number (1 to 274) |
| 5 | Every 25 or so areas, claims a rebirth | not known | Rewards list, new feature unlocked | Rebirth (max 9), +75% pet strength |

Sources: [wiki: area list](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Template:PS99SpawnWorldAreaList), [wiki: Rebirth](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth_(Pet_Simulator_99)).

How the others differ. Bee Swarm gathers pollen and converts it to honey at the hive. Training games repeat an exercise until a stat passes a gate, then rebirth. BGSI inflates gum to a storage cap, then sells ([guide](https://earnaldo.com/blog/bubble-gum-simulator-infinity-beginner-guide)). In Ride A Pet the egg is on the map: find it with a radar, carry it home under a break timer, hatch it ([guide](https://allthings.how/ride-a-pet-on-roblox-how-to-play-full-guide/)).

## Numbers that work

**Egg prices and odds.** Pet Simulator 99, World 1 ([wiki template](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Template:PS99World1EggsList)):

| Egg | Price (coins) | Ratio to previous | Rarest pet | Hatches to expect it | Coins to expect it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cracked | 90 | | four pets at 25% each | 4 | 360 |
| 2 Spotted | 150 | 1.67 | Dalmatian 6.45% | 16 | 2,300 |
| 3 Paw | 250 | 1.67 | Parrot 5.2% | 19 | 4,800 |
| 4 Grass | 650 | 2.60 | Tiger 3.4% | 29 | 19,000 |
| 5 Wood | 1,500 | 2.31 | Tiger 4.75% | 21 | 32,000 |
| 6 Pumpkin | 4,000 | 2.67 | Fox 23% | 4 | 17,000 |
| 7 Hive | 6,000 | 1.50 | Scarecrow Cat 5.4% | 19 | 111,000 |
| 8 Acorn | 9,500 | 1.58 | Llama 4.1% | 24 | 232,000 |
| 9 Blossom | 20,000 | 2.11 | Flamingo 0.16% | 625 | 12,500,000 |
| 10 Corgi | 45,000 | 2.25 | Flamingo 6.1% | 16 | 738,000 |

Ratios, hatches (1 divided by the chance) and coins: our arithmetic. Eggs 11 to 15 cost 70k, 100k, 200k, 350k, 500k (ratios 1.56, 1.43, 2.0, 1.75, 1.43). Across eggs 1 to 15 the average step is 1.85x.

How odds fall inside an egg: a filler pet at 60% to 90%, a middle pet at 20% to 38%, and a prize at 3% to 6%. The first sub-1% prize is at egg 9. The prize of one egg often returns as filler in the next (Tiger in eggs 4 and 5, Scarecrow Cat in 7 and 8). **Our read:** Flamingo shows the trick. It costs about 12.5 million coins to expect one from egg 9 and about 738,000 from egg 10. One area later the prize is cheap, so each new area reads as a step up.

BGSI's eggs run 10 to 1,500,000 coins, about 3.3x per step with 11x jumps at zone changes; its Common Egg pays 40/30/25/5%, then 1 in 100 million and 1 in 10 billion ([wiki module](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Module:Egg_Data)).

**Area costs.** Pet Simulator 99, World 1 ([wiki template](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Template:PS99SpawnWorldAreaList), [itemlevel](https://itemlevel.net/pet-simulator-99-complete-biomes-list-update-5/)):

| Currency | Areas | Costs | Average step (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coins | 2 to 8 | 900, 2.5k, 8k, 20k, 60k, 150k, 400k | 2.76x |
| Gold Bars | 9 to 25 | 1, 2, 6, 15, 35, 80, 200, 450, 1,000 ... 350,000, 750,000 | 2.33x |
| Platinum Coins | 26 to 42 | 1 (+500k Gold Bars), 4, 8 ... 350,000, 750,000 | 2.33x |

Each currency covers about 17 areas and ends near 750,000; the next starts at 1 to 4 units plus a 500,000 bridge payment in the old one. **Our read:** the player never reads a number above about a million. Area 2 costs 10 Cracked Eggs and area 8 about 9 Corgi Eggs, so every area is a choice between "go forward" and "hatch about ten more".

**Rebirth.** Pet Simulator 99 now has 9 rebirths and they no longer reset areas or coins ([wiki: Ranks](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks_(Pet_Simulator_99)), [wiki: Rebirth](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth_(Pet_Simulator_99))). When this changed is not in our sources; older guides describe 8 rebirths. Each rebirth gives +75% pet strength, diamonds (10k, 25k, 50k, 100k, then 500k) and a feature: free Auto Hatch and trading at 1, clans at 2, Tech World at 4, secret pets at 5. Ride A Pet still resets cash and bought luck; a guide calls that the main disincentive and names rebirth 3 ($2.5B plus a 1-in-1-trillion Unicorn) as the wall ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/ride-a-pet-progression-guide-best-pets-and-all-7-rebirths/)). Training games reset often: Muscle Legends needs 10,000 + 5,000 x (rebirths so far) strength and gives +5% per rebirth; Arm Wrestle allows 150 rebirths at +15% each ([Muscle Legends wiki](https://muscle-legends.fandom.com/wiki/Gyms|Rebirths|How_Rebirthing_Works), [Arm Wrestle wiki](https://arm-wrestle-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Worlds|Rebirths)).

**Our read:** removing the reset turned rebirth from a loss into a milestone. The player who reaches area 25 sees a rewards screen with a new feature, and loses nothing. Whether this changed Pet Simulator 99's player counts is not known.

**The payback rule, checked.** [slop/01-anatomy.md](../slop/01-anatomy.md) shows Steal a Brainrot pricing every unit so it pays for itself in 25 to 300 seconds, with income rising 2x to 4x per step. The simulators can be checked only partly: no source gives coins per second for any of them.

| Game | Cost growth per step | What income does | Follows the payback rule? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pet Simulator 99 areas | 2.3x to 2.8x | Not known per area | Cannot be checked |
| Muscle Legends rebirths | First rebirth 10,000 strength, 150th 755,000 (75x) | Multiplier on equipment gains about 8.5 at rebirth 150, if the +5% steps add | Close: time per rebirth rises only about 9x over 150 steps (our arithmetic) |
| Ride A Pet rebirths | 50x per step ($1M, $50M, $2.5B, $125B, $6.2T) | +1x money and one pet slot per rebirth | Only with the required pet, at 1 in 500K down to 1 in 5 quintillion |
| Bee Swarm hive slots | 1.3x to 2.8x per slot; the last costs 723,000x the first | One more bee per slot, 1 of 26 to 50 | No, on purpose (see the teardown) |
| Pet Simulator 99 golden and rainbow pets | 10 pets for +13%, 100 pets for +27% | Barely moves | No: a sink for duplicates |

**Our read:** the simulators keep the payback rule only where we cannot see it, in the first minutes. Their late game breaks it on purpose. Bee Swarm and Pet Simulator 99 are built to last years, so their late prices are walls for players whose income has grown a thousandfold. A new simulator should copy both halves: payback-priced units early, and a few visible walls later.

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** Timed free gifts. BGSI gives 9 playtime gifts at 2, 3, 4, 5, 7.5, 15, 30, 60 and 90 minutes; the last two carry 20x luck ([wiki](https://bgs-infinity.fandom.com/wiki/Playtime)). **Our read:** four gifts in the first five minutes cover the moment a new player decides to stay; the two best sit past the hour, so a player who would stop at 30 minutes has a reason to reach 90. Ranks do the same with quests: Pet Simulator 99 rank 1 needs 13 stars, rank 2 needs 25, rank 3 needs 32, and those three ranks unlock 6 pet-equip slots and the first enchant slot ([wiki: Ranks](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks_(Pet_Simulator_99))).

**Tomorrow.** Map pads with cooldowns. Pet Simulator 99 has at least 8 in World 1 ([wiki: Free Items](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Free_Items_(Pet_Simulator_99))):

| Area | Gift | Cooldown | Diamonds per hour (our arithmetic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 Castle | 300 to 450 diamonds | 30 min | about 660 |
| 17 Jungle | 1 to 2 potions | 30 min | |
| 21 Coral Reef | 2 enchants | 45 min | |
| 24 Palm Beach | 1 item | 1 h | |
| 32 Red Desert | 1,000 to 1,750 diamonds | 3 h | about 356 |
| 78 Dungeon Entrance | 5,000 to 10,000 diamonds | 5 h | about 1,170 |

**Our read:** 30-minute pads hold a session; 3- and 5-hour pads pay a player who opens the game two or three times a day.

**Next week and after.** A weekly update on a fixed day and seasonal events: Pet Simulator 99 "[TURKEY]", a BGSI Thanksgiving egg with more than 7 secret pets, Gym Star's Advent Calendar from 1 December (game descriptions, Roblox public API). Bee Swarm adds 96-hour respawns and hive slots whose last five cost 98.8% of the total ([teardown](../teardowns/bee-swarm-simulator.md)).

## Other players

**Our read:** other players matter less here than in steal games; they are pets to look at and trade partners. Pet Simulator 99 opens its Trading Plaza at rebirth 1 and clans at rebirth 2 ([wiki: Rebirth](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Rebirth_(Pet_Simulator_99))). Nothing can be lost to other players in the classic games. The 2026 hybrids add loss back: in [Steal An Egg](../teardowns/steal-an-egg.md) players steal eggs from pets and from each other. What gets clipped is not in our sources. **Our read:** the reveal of a secret pet, which in BGSI comes at 1 in 50 million to 1 in 250 million ([ginx.tv](https://www.ginx.tv/en/?p=72288)), is the moment worth recording.

**Free versus paid automation.** Pet Simulator 99 gives Auto Hatch free at rebirth 1 and sells Auto Tap and Auto Farm. BGSI unlocks continuous hatching as a progression step ([guide](https://earnaldo.com/blog/bubble-gum-simulator-infinity-beginner-guide)). Arm Wrestle sells Auto Open Eggs for 3 R$ and Auto Rebirth for 5. Bee Swarm's four passes are all off sale. **Our read:** automation is given away or priced as a near-gift; the high prices are on luck. Arm Wrestle shows it: automation at 3 to 29 R$, Premium Rewards and Double Garden Coins at 399.

Developer products are not visible through the public API, so best sellers are not known.

The line: luck passes change random outcomes, and some eggs cost Robux (Bee Swarm Silver Egg 100 R$, Gold Egg 200 R$; BGSI's Inferno Egg 59 R$ at 86/10/4%). Roblox's paid-random-item rules apply to both. Pet Simulator 99's in-game tooltip says a rainbow pet is "10x Better"; the wiki measures about +27% ([wiki](https://pet-simulator.fandom.com/wiki/Rainbow_Machine_(Pet_Simulator_99))). Do not copy that. See [foundations/15-the-line.md](../foundations/15-the-line.md).

## Live ops

BGSI updates Fridays at 2 PM EST; its changelog runs to Update 76 in about 83 weeks (our count from the wiki's page index) and it holds 7,409 players 19 months after launch. Arm Wrestle updates on Saturdays. Pet Simulator 99 was updated on 24 Nov 2026, Bee Swarm on 21 Nov 2026. Updates alone do not hold players: Mining Simulator 2 was updated on 17 Nov 2026 and has 21 players. **Our read:** a small team can sustain one new area and one new egg a week.

## How these games die

Players now per million lifetime visits (our arithmetic, Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026): Pet Simulator 99 40, BGSI 12, Bee Swarm 10, Gym Star 8, Ride A Pet 3.6, Muscle Legends 1.3, Arm Wrestle 1.2, Gym League 0.6, Strongman 0.4, Mining Simulator 1 and 2 0.1.

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Under 100 players, hundreds of millions of visits | Same loop again: Mining Simulator 1 (833M visits, 103 players) and 2 (21) | Ship a new loop, or keep updating the original |
| Peak to under 1% within weeks | Nothing after the spike: Ride A Pet, reported near 1M in October (no tracker confirms it), now 1,646 | Plan the second and third month before launch |
| Players stop at one rebirth | The reset takes what they built: Ride A Pet's rebirth 3 wall | Keep pets and areas; reward rebirth with features |
| Whole sub-genre under 6,000 | Many copies of one structure: Gym Star 5,940, Muscle Legends 2,999, Arm Wrestle 1,582, Strongman 651 | Enter with a new verb; the 2026 egg games did |
| Approval under 90% | Muscle Legends 88.3%, the lowest here | Read the down-vote reasons before adding content |

## Checklist

1. First egg costs one tenth of area 2 or less.
2. Egg prices step 1.5x to 2.7x inside a zone.
3. Every egg shows each pet's chance.
4. Each egg's rarest pet is 3% to 6% until about egg 9.
5. Egg N's prize is more common in egg N+1.
6. Area prices step 2.3x to 2.8x; each equals 4 to 10 current eggs.
7. No price shown above about 1,000,000 of one currency.
8. A free gift lands in each of minutes 2 to 5.
9. At least 6 timed gifts with cooldowns from 30 minutes to 5 hours.
10. Rank 1 finishes in the first session and unlocks pet slots.
11. Rebirth keeps pets and areas and gives a new feature.
12. Auto-hatch is earned in play, not only sold.
13. Paid luck shows live odds.
14. A weekly update on a fixed day and hour.
15. One late wall whose last steps each cost more than all earlier steps.

## Twists and open space

Twists that produced hits: the egg as an object on the map (Steal An Egg, 843,938; Ride A Pet's distance bands, 1,000 to 3,000 m); throwing to reach farther zones ([Throw Food For Animals](../teardowns/throw-food-for-animals.md), 238,007); altitude as the zone axis (BGSI, 410 m to 15,960 m); a new clicker in 2026 (Tap Simulator REBORN, created Aug 2026, 35,062).

**Our read:** open space. A training game with no-reset rebirth: the lifting games all reset and all sit under 6,000. A Bee Swarm-style game with 5 players per server. A pet simulator that shows every pet's chance in game, including the top pet; Pet Simulator 99's wiki lists the last pet of many eggs from egg 13 on as "??".
