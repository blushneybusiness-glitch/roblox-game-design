# Dress-up

> **The feeling sold:** "I made the best look for the theme, everyone saw it, and they gave me stars."

This page covers fashion-round games (a theme, a clock, a runway, a vote) and the older fashion worlds built on levels and a currency. Player counts marked "now" are from Roblox's public API on 25 Nov 2026.

## Why people play it

The player builds a look under a clock and a live audience scores it in public. Status shows as rank titles, the podium, VIP tags and rare items ([guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/beginners-guide)). Dress to Impress grew through celebrity collabs, TikTok moments such as the "Pose 28" emote and big streamers ([RoWatcher news](https://rowatcher.com/news/dress-to-impress-how-a-teen-developer-s-fashion-game-hit-9-7-billion-visits)). Age split and device split for the genre are not known.

**Our read:** almost every product the genre sells makes the player more visible (reactions, spotlights, walks, poses, signs) and almost none makes them stronger. Players pay to be seen.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Leader: Dress to Impress. Full detail is in the [teardown](../teardowns/dress-to-impress.md).

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lobby | Waits | not known | The theme appears near the end of the countdown | none |
| Dress | Picks up to 18 items | 360 (300 for new players) | The avatar changes with each item | Items worn against the cap |
| Runway | Walks and poses, in join order | not known per player | One model on the runway at a time | none |
| Vote | Rates each other model | During the runway | 1 to 5 stars | That model's star total |
| Podium and payout | Watches | not known | Top three on a podium, then a board of stars and Cash | Career stars; 0 to 60 Cash |

Sources: [wiki: Game Mode](https://dti-dress-to-impress.fandom.com/wiki/Dress_to_Impress/Game_Mode), [wiki: Cash](https://dti-dress-to-impress.fandom.com/wiki/Currency/Cash).

How the others differ: Style Showdown, inside Dress to Impress, seats 6 to 7, dresses for 180 seconds (90 in Speed Round) and eliminates the lowest-voted player each round until 3 remain ([guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/style-showdown-guide)). Slay or Nay replaces voters with three AI judges. Royale High has no rounds: classes, homework, sleep and pageants pay EXP, levels pay diamonds, diamonds buy sets ([wiki: Levels](https://royale-high.fandom.com/wiki/Levels)).

The loop stack in Dress to Impress:

| Every | What finishes |
|---|---|
| 1 to 5 seconds | An item goes on and the avatar changes |
| 6 minutes or more | A round: stars, Cash, a placement |
| 30 rounds | Accessory Adjustment unlocks (or 399 R$) |
| 24 hours | Daily Tasks and the Daily Login step |
| 7 days | Weekly Boutique, 4 items, Saturday 11 AM Eastern |
| 8 to 16 weeks | A Baddie Pass season and a large item drop |
| Months to years | The next rank on a 14-rank ladder to 150,000 stars |

Sources: [guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/beginners-guide), [Baddie Pass guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/baddie-pass-guide); round length and week ranges are our arithmetic.

## The first five minutes

No source gives a timed first session for any game in this genre. Dress to Impress puts new players on a Casual server (300-second clock, 13-item cap) with a tutorial whose contents are not known ([guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/beginners-guide)). The table is our reasoning for a new game.

| By | This has happened | Source or reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 0:05 | The player stands in a lobby with the next theme or its countdown on screen | Our read: the theme is the whole instruction |
| 0:30 | The player is in the dressing room with the theme pinned at the top | Our read: Dress to Impress shows the theme near the end of the intermission |
| 1:00 | Ten or more items tried on; each one shows on the avatar within a second | Our read: the dressing room is the only reward source before the first runway |
| 3:00 | The clock is visible and the player has a full look under the item cap | Casual cap is 13 items |
| 5:00 | The clock ends and the first runway starts | The 300-second Casual clock |

**Our read:** the first score comes after about 5 minutes. Shorten the first clock (Style Showdown uses 180 seconds) or pay during dressing, as Dress to Impress does with Cash piles on the floor (pink 1 to 3, gold 15 to 25).

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** A new theme every round from more than 790 ([guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/beginners-guide)); Accessory Adjustment at round 30; Freeplay minigames with a daily cap, such as Flower Arranging at 5 rounds then a 20-hour wait ([Freeplay guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/freeplay-mode-guide)).

**Tomorrow.** Dress to Impress: Daily Tasks (150 Blue Hearts each) and a login ladder from 15 to 105 Cash over 7 days. Royale High: a free fountain wish every 2 hours, a four-choice story with a rare halo (odds not published) ([wiki: Fountain](https://royale-high.fandom.com/wiki/Fountain)), and homework due by 3 PM the next day.

**Next week and after.** Dress to Impress: the Saturday Weekly Boutique, Saturday drops, a 30-tier pass over 8 to 16 weeks. Royale High: about 5 fountain halos a year plus calendar and bingo halos ([wiki: Halos](https://royale-high.fandom.com/wiki/Halos)); its seasons stopped gating shop items on 19 Jan 2026 ([wiki: Seasons](https://royale-high.fandom.com/wiki/Seasons)).

## Other players

Round games hold 6 to 15 players so each look gets a full audience of voters within minutes. Nothing can be stolen; the score is what is at risk, and other players hold it. Dress to Impress added vote penalties against spite and clique voting in July 2025 and removed them a week later below Master servers ([how-to-win guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/how-to-win-guide)); the full history is in the [teardown](../teardowns/dress-to-impress.md#5-other-players). Players also throw paid reactions at the runway (29 to 129 R$ for 5 in Dress to Impress).

Royale High trading ([wiki: Trading](https://royale-high.fandom.com/wiki/Trading)):

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | Level 75 and up, since 23 Mar 2020 |
| Where | Three places only: the Intergalactic Trading Hub, a Global Player Realm, Luna Lagoon at full moon |
| Size | Up to 40 items per trade |
| Timers | Request expires in 30 s; 10 s cooldown after changing diamonds or removing an item; 30 s to accept on the final screen |
| Banned | Scams, cross-trading, trust trades, real-money trades, alt-account farming |
| Blocked countries | South Korea and the Netherlands, for legal reasons |

**Our arithmetic:** at 325 EXP a level, level 75 is 24,375 EXP and 142,500 diamonds at today's 1,900 a level, about 3,900 R$ at the best shop rate. The gate keeps new and throwaway accounts out of trading.

Prices: Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026.

Where offers appear: the theme prop is offered every round. Dress to Impress changed it from an automatic purchase to an optional prop on 29 Feb 2024 ([themes guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/themes-guide)). **Our read:** extra time is bought during the clock, when the player runs short. Both are moment-of-need sales, priced under 80 R$ so they are bought without thinking long.

What backfired:
- Royale High retired its 2x and 4x diamond passes (749 and 1,649 R$) in Feb 2026 and paid owners in a halo, a badge and diamonds worth more than they paid ([wiki: Diamonds](https://royale-high.fandom.com/wiki/Diamonds)).
- Critics call Dress to Impress VIP pay-to-win; no scoring advantage is documented ([VIP guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/vip-guide)).

Policy: until Oct 2019 Royale High's fountain let players stake 5 to 200 diamonds to win 2 to 4 times as many; the wiki says it was likely retired because it resembled gambling with a currency sold for Robux ([wiki: Fountain](https://royale-high.fandom.com/wiki/Fountain)). A free wish every 2 hours replaced it. Paid random items and odds disclosure are covered in [foundations/15-the-line.md](../foundations/15-the-line.md).

**Our arithmetic:** 20 entries in the 37 weeks from 7 Feb to 24 Oct, one every 1.9 weeks. 17 of 20 land on a Saturday. Seasonal drops are 8, 13 and 15 weeks apart. The KATSEYE collab ran 3 weeks from launch to removal. Filler entries are as small as 3 items, a mini-quest and a code.

Collabs make the spikes (Charli XCX, Aug 2024, tied to the 651K peak, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_%28video_game%29)) and the crashes. Without updates, Fashion Famous 2 sits at 122 players on 2.3B visits (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026).

**Our read:** a small team can hold the Dress to Impress rhythm by alternating one seasonal drop per quarter with a 3-item mini-drop every second Saturday.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Players fall over 99% in six weeks | Nothing to return for. Makeup Mania! (created 25 Sep 2026) peaked near 65K in Oct ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10768023539)) and had 89 on 25 Nov. Its store: VIP 399, Shape Stencils 199, 3 saved looks 149, second hair slot 99, theme 30, +2 min 40, coins. No ladder, season or collection was found. Cause unverified. | A rank ladder and a season pass at launch |
| "New content coming soon" still in the description a month after the peak | Update gap (Makeup Mania!) | A mini-drop every 2 weeks from launch |
| Complaints about votes; good looks finish last | Spite and clique voting (Dress to Impress) | Separate servers by stars; weight or penalise outlier votes; or score with fixed criteria (Slay or Nay) |
| Crashes at an event start | Everyone joins at once for a 3-hour code item (Dress to Impress, Dec 2024) | Longer windows |
| Like ratio under 80% in month one | Players reject the judging. Slay or Nay 73.6% on 2K likes against Dress to Impress 90.5% ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10772471673)) | Show each judge's score and reason on screen |
| Near zero despite billions of visits | No updates (Fashion Famous 2) | The rhythm above |
| Anger at a paid multiplier | Pay-for-power in a status game (Royale High 2x and 4x diamonds) | Retire it and pay owners back with a collectible |

**Our read on Makeup Mania!:** it copied the round, the vote, VIP, paid theme and paid time, changed the build tool, and shipped nothing to return for after the novelty. A similar game, Makeup Salon, still has 6,799 players, so demand for makeup rounds survived.

## Checklist

- [ ] The theme is on screen before the dressing clock starts.
- [ ] The first clock is 240 s or less; the standard clock is 300 to 360 s.
- [ ] An item shows on the avatar within 1 s of the tap.
- [ ] Servers hold 6 to 15 players, and every other player scores each look in the same round.
- [ ] Something pays during the first dressing phase (Cash on the floor, a quest step).
- [ ] The first earned cosmetic costs 5 rounds or fewer.
- [ ] A rank ladder of 12 or more steps exists at launch, with its next rank shown after every round.
- [ ] New players get their own server tier with a shorter clock or smaller cap.
- [ ] Higher tiers are gated by stars, not by Robux.
- [ ] VIP gives no scoring or voting advantage.
- [ ] Extra time and theme choice are sold at under 80 R$ each, offered only during the round.
- [ ] A season pass of about 30 tiers runs 8 to 16 weeks.
- [ ] A drop of at least 3 items ships every 2 weeks, on the same weekday.
- [ ] There is a plan for vote abuse before launch: tiered servers, vote weighting or fixed criteria.

## Twists and open space

The 2026 followers and what each changed (players now: Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026):

| Game | Launched | Players now (peak) | What it changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Catalog Avatar Runway | 25 May 2026 | 14,938 (33K, Nov 2026, [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10222476236)) | Real catalog items; buying one puts it in your Roblox inventory, and the game earns a commission (see [hangout](hangout.md#money)) |
| Makeup Mania! | 25 Sep 2026 | 89 (about 65K, Oct 2026) | Freehand face painting; 10 seats; VIP 399 |
| Makeup Salon | 5 Oct 2026 | 6,799 | Makeup under a clock, rate others; tier skips 99 |
| Slay or Nay | 22 Oct 2026 | 9,095 | 240 s, 6 seats, three AI judges, buy what you wore after the show |

Older variations: Style Showdown's elimination rounds; Freeplay with no theme, no timer, 17 players and minigames ([Freeplay guide](https://rblxguide.com/games/dress-to-impress/updates/freeplay-mode-guide)); Royale High's school roleplay. Paying to pick the theme is in every 2026 entrant.

**Our read:** open space.
- Vote weight by rank: no game here gives a 15,000-star voter more weight than a new player.
- Visible judging: show each of Slay or Nay's three criteria as a score after every walk.
- A collection in a makeup game: neither makeup game sells or rewards a list to complete.
- A buy-through runway with a rank ladder: neither Catalog Avatar Runway nor Slay or Nay shows one.
