# Driving, racing, and cops and robbers

> **The feeling sold:** "I have a garage full of cars, and I drive the fast one past everybody." In the cops-and-robbers branch: "I rob the bank and get away, or I catch the one who did."

This page covers car collection and racing games (Driving Empire, Car Dealership Tycoon, Midnight Racing: Tokyo), destruction around cars (Car Crushers 2), and open-world cops and robbers ([Jailbreak](../teardowns/jailbreak.md), Emergency Hamburg). Player counts marked "now" are from the Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026, a weekday morning in the US.

## Why people play it

**Our read**, from the badge counts below: most players come to own and drive cars, not to race. Driving Empire's "Won your first race!" badge has 59,342,742 awards against 409,225,122 for "You played!". Both badges were made on 3 Dec 2020, so they cover the same period. That is about 1 player in 7 who has ever won a race (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026; [badges](https://badges.roblox.com/v1/universes/1202096104/badges?limit=100)). The "Own 5 Cars!" badge, made almost four years later, already has 82,430,740 awards, more than the race badge. Driving Empire is a car collection game with racing on the side.

Who the players are (age, device): not known.

## The loop, with a stopwatch

Jailbreak is the only game in this genre whose timings are published, so the table uses it. Times are from the Jailbreak wiki ([Rising City Bank](https://jailbreak.fandom.com/wiki/Rising_City_Bank), [Jewelry Store](https://jailbreak.fandom.com/wiki/Jewelry_Store), [Arrest](https://jailbreak.fandom.com/wiki/Arrest)). Travel time between places is not known.

| Step | Player does | Seconds | Sees and hears | Number that moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pick a target | Drives to an open robbery | not known | Robbery marked open on the map | none |
| Rob | Robs the bank or store | up to about 300 for the bank (the wiki says robbable for about 5 min) | Duffel bag fills | Bag cash, up to $9,000 at the bank |
| Escape | Drives off with police chasing | not known | Bounty rises | Bounty +$1,500 for the bank |
| Bank the cash | Reaches safety | not known | Cash counter rises | Cash |
| Or get caught | Arrested by police | 10 handcuffed, about 5 in a cell | Items stripped | Police officer collects the bounty as cash |
| Spend | Buys a vehicle or upgrade | not known | New car in the garage | Cash down |

Each robbery then stays closed for about 5 minutes in a public server before it can be robbed again. Since the "Capless" system, every robbery can be open at once ([Robberies & Heists](https://jailbreak.fandom.com/wiki/Robberies_%26_Heists)), so the player chains robberies instead of waiting.

**How Driving Empire differs.** Cash comes from driving itself, from speed traps and from race wins ([Currencies](https://drivingempire.fandom.com/wiki/Currencies)). The Delivery Driver job gives 2 minutes to collect a package and 40 seconds to deliver it, and a miss resets the job level to 0 ([Jobs](https://drivingempire.fandom.com/wiki/Jobs)). Money per mile or per minute: not known for any activity.

**The loop stack.**

| Every | What finishes | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 10 seconds to 5 minutes | A robbery or a delivery | Gas Station: hold E about 10 s. Bank: about 5 min. Delivery: 40 s. |
| 4 to 8 minutes | A robbery reopens | Jailbreak public servers: about 4 to 8 minutes per robbery |
| 20 minutes | A server-wide event | Car Crushers 2 energy core charge |
| One session | A new car | Jailbreak: the $9,000 Pickup, about 15 minutes after escaping (arithmetic below) |
| One day | A login reward | Midnight Racing: Tokyo streak; Jailbreak VIP $1,000 a day |
| One week | The streak ends on a car | Midnight Racing: Tokyo, day 7 |

## What brings them back

**Later in the same session.** The next robbery reopens in 4 to 8 minutes (Jailbreak). Car Crushers 2's energy core charges for 20 minutes at about 20.8 MWh per second up to 25,000 MWh. When it blows, a 10-second countdown runs and the whole server has to reach the helicopter (marked with a light column) or the boat (unmarked). Escape pays 100 Credits by helicopter and 150 by boat, or 150 and 240 with the pass, and the Money page says escaping pays up to $200B to $300B ([Energy Core](https://car-crushers-2.fandom.com/wiki/Energy_Core), [Money](https://car-crushers-2.fandom.com/wiki/Money)). Starting the charge needs one player with the 350 R$ Energy Core pass, and the charge has to be restarted after every meltdown. **Our read:** that is a 20-minute clock for the whole server. One paying player sets it off, and every player in the server gets the escape and the payout.

**Tomorrow.** Midnight Racing: Tokyo pays ¥100,000 on day 1, rising to ¥1,000,000 by day 6. Day 7 gives a free Tier 2 car. Missing a day resets the streak ([Earnaldo, promotional](https://earnaldo.com/blog/midnight-racing-tokyo-free-robux-guide)). Tier 2 cars cost ¥2,372,000 to ¥5,325,000, so the day-7 car is worth at least 2.4 times the day-6 cash (our arithmetic). Jailbreak's VIP pass pays $1,000 a day and one tier-two safe a day ([passes, Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/245662005/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100)).
**Next week and after.** Codes tied to like counts: BloxNG "GARAGE" for $20,000 with "next code at 40K likes"; Drag Drive Simulator's next code at 225,000 likes (game pages, Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). Collectible hunts: Driving Empire's May 2024 hunt gave 10 "TIX" badges, each awarded about 437,000 to 728,000 times. Limited cars: Driving Empire's 20 Nov 2026 update added a new Limited car.

## Other players

Racing games run 8 to 22 players a server; open-world cops and robbers runs 30 to 50. In Jailbreak a criminal's bounty rises with each robbery (the bank adds $1,500), the top 6 show on a board, and the cap is $25,000 since 13 Jun 2026 ([Bounty](https://jailbreak.fandom.com/wiki/Bounty)). The officer who makes the arrest collects the bounty; the criminal loses no cash. Museum and Tomb need 2 players to start. Full rules are in the [teardown](../teardowns/jailbreak.md).

Driving Empire's shared moment is the car meet. Car Dealership Tycoon has leaderboard rewards and ranks. **Our read:** the moment that gets clipped is the escape: a chase in Jailbreak, or the whole server running for Car Crushers 2's helicopter in the last 10 seconds.

## Live ops

Driving Empire, Jailbreak, Car Dealership Tycoon and Midnight Racing were all updated within 3 days of 25 Nov 2026, and each writes the update into its description with a date or a code (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). Driving Empire's 20 Nov 2026 update and four Jailbreak event starts fell on Fridays (calendar arithmetic; no source states an update day). Jailbreak's last 12 months had events of 4 to 21 days and one 60-day season ([Bloxodes events](https://bloxodes.com/events/jailbreak)). On 29 Oct 2026 it removed seasons after Season 33 ([Seasons](https://jailbreak.fandom.com/wiki/Seasons)). Driving Empire ran its first 2X cash event on 5 Feb 2022 ([Currencies](https://drivingempire.fandom.com/wiki/Currencies)).

**Our read:** a small team can copy the burst event: 4 to 11 days around one named feature, announced in the description with a cash code.

## How these games die

| Symptom you can measure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Concurrent players fall while visits stay high | No updates. Vehicle Legends, last update 29 Sep 2026, has 914 players on 1.96B visits. | A dated update at least every 2 to 4 weeks |
| A twin game far behind the leader | Not documented. Mad City has 142 players against Jailbreak's 4,843 on the same day. | Not known; study what the leader changed |
| Players complain about the income rules | Jailbreak's 2023 robbery groups were removed after wide dissatisfaction | Change the throttle in small steps; Jailbreak tried four systems in about three years |
| Top cars cannot be bought | Seasonal and limited cars retired: Jailbreak lists 37 unobtainable seasonal vehicles; Car Dealership Tycoon's top two are unobtainable | Keep at least one buyable car above every player's balance |
| Top-end prices get cut | Driving Empire cut the Jesko from $16M to $13M on 30 Jan 2026 | Add cars at the top on a schedule instead of re-pricing |
| A long progression system is dropped | Jailbreak removed seasons after Season 33 | Prefer events of 4 to 21 days |

## Checklist

- [ ] Starting cash is 25% to 50% of the first paid car.
- [ ] The first paid car can be bought within 15 minutes of play.
- [ ] The cheap band has at least 8 cars, each 1.1x to 1.35x the last.
- [ ] The ladder spans at least 250x from cheapest to dearest.
- [ ] The top car earns no more than 2x the free car per mile or per minute.
- [ ] Collection badges exist at 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100 cars.
- [ ] Each robbery or job finishes in 10 seconds to 5 minutes.
- [ ] A reopen timer of 4 to 8 minutes is shown on the map or HUD.
- [ ] A 7-day login streak ends on a car worth at least 2x the day-6 reward.
- [ ] Robux car packs show a cash value, priced at 1,000 to 3,000 cash per R$.
- [ ] Server size: 8 to 22 for racing, 30 to 50 for cops and robbers.
- [ ] A dated update or event lands at least every 4 weeks.

## Twists and open space

Variations that have already drawn players:

- **Crash physics.** BloxNG, made 13 Oct 2026, has 37,835 now, more than Driving Empire's 29,055 (Roblox public API, 25 Nov 2026). See [sandbox.md](sandbox.md).
- **Build the road.** Build a Racetrack, made 2 Nov 2025, has 13,668 now: players build a track, others drive it, and it earns offline.
- **A journey.** A Dusty Trip turns a drive into a survival run; see [survival.md](survival.md).

**Our read**, open angles: a car collection game with Midnight Racing's cosmetic-only pass list and Driving Empire's funnel; a cops-and-robbers game sized for 8 to 12 players, since every live one is 30 to 50; a racing game whose daily reward is a part, so the garage fills one piece a day.
